How the estimate works

  • Trading allowance or expenses. HMRC lets a sole trader deduct £1,000 instead of actual expenses; the estimate picks whichever leaves the lower profit, as the workbook does.
  • Income Tax is charged on your trading profit plus other taxable income after the Personal Allowance, which tapers away above £100,000.
  • Class 4 National Insurance is charged on trading profit alone. Class 2 is treated as paid for profits above the small profits threshold; below it you can pay voluntarily.
  • Payments on account are two advance payments towards next year, each half of this year's tax and Class 4, when that is over £1,000 and less than 80% was deducted at source.
  • Rates read from gov.uk in October 2026. Made with the help of AI tools; the arithmetic is the workbook's, tested against a hand-worked year.
