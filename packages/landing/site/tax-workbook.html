Questions buyers ask

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Import the .xlsx with File → Import in Google Sheets; it also opens in Excel 2010 or later, LibreOffice Calc and Apple Numbers. Formulas only, no macros.

Which SA103 boxes does it fill in?

The SA103S short self-employment pages: turnover and other income (boxes 9–10), the expense boxes 11–19 from your categorised expenses log, and the net profit figure, ready to copy into your Self Assessment return.

Does it do Making Tax Digital quarterly updates?

It totals your income and expenses for each of the four MTD quarters (standard or calendar quarters) so the figures are ready for your MTD software. It is not itself HMRC-recognised filing software.

How does it estimate my tax bill?

From the 2026/27 rates on gov.uk: Income Tax after the Personal Allowance, Class 4 National Insurance, the trading allowance check and payments on account. The same arithmetic runs the free tax estimate on this site. Scottish Income Tax bands are not covered.
