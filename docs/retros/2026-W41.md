## 7. Proposals

**1. The run watchdog's burn-down slot (and this routine's own prompt text) still assume
Wednesday-only, six days after the schedule moved to nightly.** Evidence: `docs/SCHEDULERS.md`
has said nightly 17:00-02:00 since the 2026-10-04 16:20 commit; `scripts/run-watchdog.ts`
`ROUTINES` still has the `burn-down` entry at `days: [3]` only, and this file's own Measure
step 1 still lists "burn-down Wed 18:00–Thu 02:00". Fix: change the `burn-down` entry in
`scripts/run-watchdog.ts` to drop the `days` restriction (watch the first hour — 17:00 UTC —
every night, keeping the existing "only the first hour" comment and rationale), update the
`docs/SCHEDULERS.md` "every routine slot is watched" convention bullet and the watchdog job
row to say nightly, and replace this file's own sentence "burn-down Wed 18:00–Thu 02:00" with
"burn-down nightly 17:00 UTC (first hour only; later hours may be usage-limit cutoffs)" in the
same commit. `effort_days`: 0.1. `proof`: the watchdog reports a slot for every night of the
following week (7 `burn-down` entries in its internal slot list, not 1); a deliberately silent
non-Wednesday night produces a `watchdog | missed:` line within 2 h in a dry run. Evidence 3
(a real detection gap, confirmed by reading the script) × reach 3 (every night, indefinitely,
until a silent night actually happens) ÷ 0.1 days. **Score: 7.**

**2. `handoff:` entries that only an attended session can clear get no aging signal, while
`owner:` asks get a 7-day digest.** Evidence: ReadFocus 0.3.0 (handoff opened 10-04) and
Highlight Keep 0.4.0 (opened 10-05) sat 6 and 5 days respectively with nothing on the owner's
phone naming them, although in practice only the owner opening Claude Code (to use the `.env`
signing keys) can clear a `handoff:` line — the same actor the `owner-ask-digest` already
exists to prompt, just under a different label. Fix: in `scripts/handoffs.ts`, extend
`digestLine` (or add a second notify line alongside it) to also count open `handoff:` entries
against a threshold — a shorter one than the 7-day owner-ask default is reasonable here, since
these are typically few-minute signing jobs blocking an already-finished release — e.g. "N
releases waiting on your next Claude Code session, oldest N days", printed whenever any
`handoff:` entry passes that threshold; the weekly report's notify-line sentence
(`docs/routines/weekly-report.md`) picks it up the same way it already does the owner digest.
`effort_days`: 0.1. `proof`: the next `handoff:` entry that crosses the threshold produces a
`notify:` line within a day of crossing it; a month from now no shipped-but-unsigned release
handoff is older than the threshold without one. Evidence 3 (two real releases, a full week
each, zero signal) × reach 2 (every future signing/attended-session handoff) ÷ 0.1 days.
**Score: 5.**
