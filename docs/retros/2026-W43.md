## 7. Proposals

**1. Pace the nightly burn on observed cutoffs, not an unreported percentage.** Observation: BURN.json
`report` is dated 2026-10-04 (`docs/ops/BURN.json`), so `stop_if_weekly_pct_at_least: 80` has had no
input for 20 days; the burn ran to the limit and 4 daytime slots (exchange 08:00, build ×2, burn 17:00
on 10-21) plus 9 night hours (silent 10-21 18:00 → 10-22 02:00) were lost — about 36 hours of
the allowance week with no Fable routine, including the only slot that produces new lines. Fix, in
`scripts/pipeline.ts` (the `cold`/BURN gate that prints before `--routine=burn`) and
`docs/routines/burn-down.md` Gate sentence: when `report.date` is more than 7 days old, treat the
allowance as unknown and apply a calendar cap — Monday and Tuesday nights run at most the first 3
hourly slots that find work; Wednesday night (before the Thursday 03:00 reset) and Thursday–Sunday
are unchanged; any `watchdog | missed: … usage limit?` or a `silent` slot in the last 24 h ends
that night's burn at once. Replace nothing the owner said: the 70–80 % goal is kept, a fresh
`report` re-opens the old gate. `effort_days` 0.5. Proof: over 4 weeks, no daytime build, exchange
or review slot is `missed` with a `usage limit?` watchdog line on the day after a burn night, and
built slots per week do not fall below 20. Score: evidence 3 (one week, 6 lost slots, 20-day stale
input) × reach 3 (build, exchange, burn-down) ÷ 0.5 days → **6** (capped by a single week's data).

**2. File dashboard-permission and ask-first handoffs as `owner:` lines.** Observation: of the 5 open
entries `npm run handoffs` prints, two are not attended-session work: ALERTS 10-17 (Cloudflare token
needs Zone Read + Zone Analytics Read, a dashboard click on the owner's account) and 10-20

and to the `WHO DOES WHAT` paragraph of `docs/routines/inbox-triage.md`: "A step that edits an
account, token scope, dashboard setting or a path CLAUDE.md lists under 'Things to ask before doing'
is an `owner:` line batched into the next `docs/for-owner/actions/` file, never a `handoff:`; a
`handoff:` is only work the repo and a Claude session can finish." `effort_days` 0.1. Proof: no
`handoff:` line older than 3 days that names a dashboard, token or `.devcontainer/` for 30 days;
median open age of owner asks stays under 7 days. Score: evidence 2 × reach 2 (triage + build) ÷ 0.1
days, capped by frequency → **4**.
