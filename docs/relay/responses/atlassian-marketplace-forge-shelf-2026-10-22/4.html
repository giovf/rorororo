Under this Agreement, you appoint and authorize Atlassian, as your commercial agent, to promote, sell, test and distribute Marketplace Apps (as defined below) on the terms and conditions of this Agreement, and Atlassian accepts such appointment. Before clicking to agree to this Agreement, please carefully read the terms and conditions below. The Atlassian Marketplace is not intended for and may not be used by anyone under the age of 16.

This Agreement does not have to be signed in order to be binding. You indicate your assent to the terms of this Agreement by (i) checking the box (or similar action) to accept the Agreement that is presented to you at the time you sign up to list your products on the Atlassian Marketplace or (ii) by submitting any Marketplace App to the Atlassian Marketplace.

Atlassian may modify this Agreement from time to time, subject to the terms in Section 18 (Agreement Changes) below.

1. Introduction to the Atlassian Marketplace

The Atlassian Marketplace is a forum for connecting potential end users with partners who make Marketplace Apps for Atlassian Apps. Marketplace Apps can be written using the Atlassian Platform and can be written for use with Atlassian’s downloaded software or cloud products. As a partner, you may charge a fee for your Marketplace Apps and designate whether end users may order your Marketplace Apps through Atlassian’s ordering and license management system (in which case revenue sharing will apply) or directly from you (in which case there will be no revenue sharing with Atlassian). You may also offer your Marketplace Apps free of charge under certain circumstances.

2.3. “Atlassian Platform” has the meaning given in the Atlassian Developer Terms.

2.4. “End User Data” means any data, content or information of an end user that is accessed, collected or otherwise processed by you or your Marketplace App in connection with use of the Atlassian Marketplace. 

2.5. “End User Privacy Policy” means a legally adequate privacy policy provided to each end user from whom you or your Marketplace App collects, accesses, or otherwise processes End User Data.

2.6. “End User Terms” means a legally adequate agreement governing end user use of your Marketplace Apps.

2.7. “Laws” means all applicable laws, rules, regulations or orders, including those relating to data privacy, data transfer, international communications or the export of technical or personal data.

2.8. “Marketplace Apps” means cloud and downloadable applications, plug-ins or extensions that are designed to interoperate with Atlassian’s own software and cloud offerings (through use of the Atlassian Platform) and that you deliver to Atlassian or make available through the Marketplace, as further described in Section 3.1 (Types of Apps). “Marketplace Apps” also include any New Versions of existing Marketplace Apps.

2.9. “Marketplace Discount Programs” means the current Marketplace discounts listed at our Marketplace Discount Programs on the Partner Portal. Updates to Marketplace Discount Programs may be communicated to you from time to time via the Atlassian Developer Community, the Partner Portal or the Marketplace Guidelines.

Our marketplace brand guidelines are part of the Marketplace Guidelines and are located here.

2.12. “Partner Portal” means the online platform made accessible to Marketplace Partner by Atlassian on which Atlassian from time to time will communicate program details and changes, and provide sales tools and services, to Marketplace Partner.  The URL of the portal is: https://atlassianpartners.atlassian.net/ (as amended from time to time by Atlassian).  You can access the Partner Portal by activating your applicant status under ‘Validate your status’ on the Partner Portal landing page.

2.13. “Revenue Share” means the revenue based on the Marketplace Partner’s Sale as displayed at the applicable Revenue Share page on the Partner Portal at the time of the Sale in accordance with Section 4.2 (Revenue Share) below.

2.14. “Security Incident” means any actual or suspected (a) unauthorized access, acquisition, use, disclosure, modification, loss or destruction of End User Data in the possession or control of you or your agents or contractors (whether intentional or accidental), (b) security vulnerability or compromise of your Marketplace App or (c) issue involving your Marketplace App that materially degrades Atlassian systems or networks.

2.15. “Similar Service” means, in relation to Paid-via-Atlassian Apps, any online distribution service or channel (other than the Atlassian Marketplace) that makes such Marketplace Apps available for sale, use or download to end users and/or channel partners, including any website or other distribution method operated by you.

3. Using the Marketplace to Publish Your Apps

3.1. Types of Marketplace Apps. Currently, the Atlassian Marketplace allows you to offer the following types of listings for Marketplace Apps. You designate your listing type using your vendor account on the Atlassian Marketplace (subject to approval by Atlassian), based on the options below:
 

 

 

"Software Apps"

Marketplace Apps that are downloadable by end users from the Atlassian Marketplace and designed to run in end user instances of Atlassian Apps behind the end user’s firewall.

Software Apps include any “Data Center” deployments of your Marketplace Apps and Data Center approved Marketplace Apps (learn more about Data Center approved Marketplace Apps here.)

"Cloud Apps"

Marketplace Apps that are designed to be used with Atlassian’s hosted services, such as Atlassian’s Cloud offerings.

“Forge Apps” are Cloud Apps or components thereof that are developed using the Atlassian Forge Framework and hosted by Atlassian on Marketplace Partner's behalf.

All other Cloud Apps are hosted by Marketplace Partner or its agent.

Listing Options: Free and Paid

 

“Free Apps”

Marketplace Apps that you provide free of charge, which may be downloaded from the Atlassian Marketplace or from your external website, in all cases under circumstances specified by Atlassian. Free Apps may be either Software Apps or Cloud Apps.

“Paid-via-Atlassian Apps”

Marketplace Apps for which end users pay all fees to Atlassian (with Marketplace Partner receiving a Revenue Share as described in this Agreement, and Atlassian receiving the Revenue Share from end users as Marketplace Partner’s commercial agent.) Paid-via-Atlassian Apps may be either Software Apps or Cloud Apps. You must use Atlassian’s licensing mechanisms (see Section 6.4 regarding the Migration API) for all Paid-via-Atlassian Apps. For Paid-via-Atlassian Apps that are Software Apps, end users will receive license keys from Atlassian.

“Paid-via-Vendor Apps”

Marketplace Apps for which end users pay all fees to Marketplace Partner (not Atlassian). Paid-via-Vendor Apps may not be Cloud Apps, Data Center approved Marketplace Apps, or any Marketplace Apps you develop using Atlassian Connect or Forge, unless Paid-via-Atlassian Apps is not available for that product or is otherwise permitted by Atlassian in writing. For Paid-via-Vendor Apps, you provide your own license keys directly to the end user. Depending on the settings you select, end users will either download these Marketplace Apps directly from you or will be able to download the Marketplace Apps from Atlassian before navigating to your website or otherwise contacting you directly to purchase license keys.

Separately, Atlassian may offer discounts from List Price for Sales through Atlassian Partners as described in Section 4.9 (Sales through Atlassian Partners and Your Channel Partners).

The current Revenue Share pages are available at developer.atlassian.com and may be amended from time to time by Atlassian. Any changes or updates to the Revenue Share will be effective 30 days after notice.
