❓ Frequently Asked Questions
❓ I'm new to MCP. Can I still participate?
Absolutely! We provide comprehensive tutorials on MCP and will have office hours throughout to support participants.
🛠️ Can I use vibe coding tools like Bolt, Lovable, or Cursor?
Yes! Use any tools that help you build. We celebrate results, not just process.
🔨 Do I need to build everything from scratch?
No. You can use existing libraries, frameworks, and tools. Just ensure your core contribution is original and created during the hackathon.
👥 Can I participate solo or do I need a team?
Both! Participate individually or in teams of 2-5 members.
🔄 What if I participated in the June hackathon?
Welcome back! Build something new or significantly evolve your previous project. Previous winners are eligible.
💻 Is there a specific programming language requirement?
Gradio is Python-based, but you can integrate other languages/services as needed.
❓ How do I get help during the hackathon?
Discord support, office hours, documentation, and community help available throughout.
🎯 Can I submit to multiple tracks?
Yes! Submit the same project to multiple tracks or create multiple separate projects.
💰 Will I receive the $25 HF credits?
Yes! We are running the credit-assignment scripts almost every day. You should see the $25 HF credits in your account if you satify these 3 conditions:
You are registered with us for the hackathon,
You are a member of this HF org: MCP 1st Birthday
You have a valid credit card linked to your HF profile
