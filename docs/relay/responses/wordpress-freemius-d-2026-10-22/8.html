How We Help

Equalize Digital is your trusted partner for assessing and resolving all accessibility errors. Our software and services work together to help you ensure your WordPress website, web application, or plugin meets Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), as required by

  • Americans With Disabilities Act (ADA)
  • Accessibility for Ontarians With Disabilities Act (AODA)
  • European Accessibility Act
  • Section 508
  • And other laws worldwide.

Whether you need urgent accessibility fixes or are planning a new website and want to ensure it is accessible to people of all abilities, we can help. Our unique combination of automated testing software and expert accessibility consultants makes us a leader in WordPress accessibility.

The Business Case for Accessibility

Having an accessible website and supporting digital equality is not just good karma, it’s good for business, too. Disabled Americans control more than $200 billion online in discretionary spending. No matter the industry you’re in, your organization is likely engaging with customers with disabilities online. Investing in accessibility helps you reach a broader customer base and stay compliant with accessibility laws around the world.

Industry Specific Information

Reduce risk of lawsuits and fines

Ensure you’re compliant with federal and state laws for website accessibility.

Improve search engine ranking

Many accessibility fixes will also improve your website’s search engine performance.

Increase sales & conversions

Allow more people access to your website, increasing opportunities for sales.

Practice social responsibility

Take meaningful action and do your part to make the internet better.

Who Accessibility Helps

More than 1 billion people in the world have a disability. So do 1-in-4 U.S. adults. Having an accessible website allows you to reach all your potential customers online, including:

  1. Blind and visually impaired
  2. Deaf and hard-of-hearing
  3. People with cognitive or reading disabilities
  4. People unable to use a traditional keyboard and mouse
  5. People with seizure disorders
  6. The elderly and less web-savvy people
  7. People on mobile devices, in rural areas or on metered connections
  8. People with temporary or situational limitations
