Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best WordPress donation plugin for charities on a budget?

We think Donation Platform for WooCommerce is the best WordPress donation plugin free of platform fees that offers professional-grade fundraising features. You get powerful fundraising features like recurring giving and fee recovery without having to pay any fees besides payment processor fees.

How do I set up a donation campaign in WooCommerce?

Setting up a fundraising campaign in WooCommerce is simple. Once the plugin is installed, it transforms your WooCommerce environment into a fundraising powerhouse. You can choose from five modern donation templates, customize your fields, and start accepting WooCommerce donations in minutes. It works by streamlining the checkout process specifically for donors, ensuring they aren’t treated like “customers” buying a physical product.

Is this a recurring donation plugin for WordPress?

Yes! If you want to secure reliable, long-term funding, this WooCommerce donation plugin supports recurring gifts. By integrating with Subscriptions for WooCommerce (free) or WooCommerce Subscriptions (premium), you can allow donors to contribute weekly, monthly, or annually.

Does this donation plugin support credit cards and Apple Pay?

Absolutely. One of the biggest advantages of using a our WooCommerce donation plugin is the access to over 100+ payment gateways. You can accept WooCommerce donations via:
Express Checkout: Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal.
Credit Cards: Stripe, Square, Razorpay, and more.
Traditional Methods: Direct bank transfers and local payment options.

Can donors cover the transaction fees?

Yes. To ensure 100% of the intended gift reaches your cause, our donation plugin includes a Fee Recovery feature. This encourages supporters to add a small percentage to their total to cover payment processor costs a feature often locked behind a paywall in other plugins.

Why choose a WooCommerce donation solution over standalone plugins?

Most WordPress donation plugins are limited by their own proprietary ecosystems. By choosing Donation Platform for WooCommerce, you tap into the entire WooCommerce ecosystem. This means:
Full Data Ownership: You own your donor database; no third-party locking your data.
CRM Integration: Seamlessly sync with HubSpot, Salesforce, or Zoho.
Marketing Power: Connect directly to Mailchimp or MailPoet to grow your donor base.
Accounting: Export your donation WooCommerce data easily for tax season.

Is the donation form mobile-friendly?

Yes. Every donation plugin for WooCommerce needs to be mobile-first. Our forms are fully responsive and optimized for “Express Donations,” allowing your supporters to complete their gift in as little as 20 seconds from their phone.
