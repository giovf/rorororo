What every licence includes

Several fee tiers per method

Stack rules on one payment method; the first whose conditions match is charged.

Conditions

A rule can check nine things: cart total, coupon, product, category, country or state, role, shipping method, zone and class. Every one you add must match.

Fee as a product line

For invoicing and ERP exports that skip WooCommerce fee lines.

Fees beyond checkout

Buying questions

About the plugin rather than the price? Every question is on one page.

Who am I actually paying?

Freemius, as Merchant of Record. They handle the checkout, the invoice with your VAT details, renewals and refunds; I handle the plugin. Your licences live in your Freemius account (opens in a new tab).

What counts as a site?

One production install. Development and staging installs don't consume an activation — localhost, the .local, .test and .staging endings, dev. and staging. subdomains, and the staging domains of hosts like WP Engine and Kinsta are all exempt — so staging never costs you a slot.

What happens when the year ends?

The Pro features switch off and the plugin carries on as the free version. The first fee on each payment method keeps being charged; the extra tiers, conditions, fees on order-pay and manual orders, and the watchdog stop. Nothing is deleted — your setup waits in the database, so renewing restores it exactly as it was.

Can I move a licence to another site?

Yes. Deactivate it on the old install from the plugin screen, or free the slot from your Freemius account, then activate it on the new one.

Do you refund?

Within 14 days, no argument — the refund policy has the exact terms. The trial exists so you shouldn't need it.

Is there a discount for the free plugin's users?

The upgrade prompt inside the plugin carries the current offer. It changes over time, so it's shown there rather than here.
