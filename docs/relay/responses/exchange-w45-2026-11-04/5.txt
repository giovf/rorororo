Competition Overview
Competition Structure
The Marathon Match Tournament is a high-stakes, long-form coding competition that challenges competitors to solve open-ended problems with no single correct answer. It rewards creativity, optimization, and iterative problem-solving over time. Each Marathon Match consists of two phases:
[MM phase image]
1 - Provisional (Training) Phase
Competitors refine their solutions and improve their rank on a live leaderboard.
2 - System (Final) Testing Phase
Solutions are evaluated on a larger set of cases, determining the final rankings
How to Qualify for the Finals
Earn points by competing in eligible Marathon Matches throughout the year.
Only Marathon Matches completed between June 1, 2026 and March 31, 2027 contribute to leaderboard standings. We will have at least 6 Marathon Matches counting for the tournament.
You will earn points based on your ranking in each match.
Check the leaderboard to see where you stand.
The top 12 competitors on the leaderboard qualify for the Championship Round.
