Open Agent Hackathon 2026

Build an agent that does real work. Connect data, understand context, and turn intelligence into action.

Open Agent Hackathon 2026 brings builders together to build AI agents that can do real work in the real world.

Course Overview

The challenge is simple: build an agent that can access relevant information, understand context and relationships, reason across multiple steps, maintain useful context, and turn that understanding into a useful outcome.
This is not about building another chatbot or demo. The goal is to create useful, production-oriented agents that solve meaningful problems for real users, industries, or communities.
Participants get three challenge tracks plus a wildcard, sponsor technologies, live technical workshops on the Data, Build and Power layers, mentors, and a step-by-step setup guide designed to take you from zero knowledge to a functioning agent.

What you'll learn

Build a useful, production-oriented AI agent that solves a real problem, not another chatbot or demo
Data: connect your agent to the real, fragmented data an organisation runs on through Zetaris, and add live web data with Bright Data
Build: vibe code your agent in Cursor with the Meterless prompt library, without spending provider credit on coding
Power: run your product on NVIDIA Nemotron through Meterless, and choose the right model for each step
Give your agent context, memory, and the ability to explain why it reached an answer
Ship, document, and demo your agent to a judging panel against real scoring criteria

Who this course is for

Developers & Agent Builders Engineers and AI builders who want to ship a real, working agent in a week and test it against genuine challenges

Founders & Indie Hackers Solo builders and small teams of up to five who want to turn an idea into a production-oriented agent with mentor support

Students & Career Switchers Anyone aged 16 or older who wants hands-on agent-building experience, from zero knowledge to a functioning agent

What's included

Three challenge tracks plus a wildcard: Solving Fragmented Intelligence, The Agent That Can Explain Why, Reasoning Architecture, and Bring Your Own Project (Tinkerer)

Three live workshops, recorded and available on demand: Data Day (Zetaris and Bright Data), Build Day (vibe coding with Cursor and the Meterless prompt library), and Power Day (Meterless and NVIDIA Nemotron)

A step-by-step setup guide and a quick start for each layer, so your machine is ready before the build window opens

Mentor support and a community help channel for teams, announcements, and coordination

Sponsor technologies and credits: Zetaris, Bright Data, Cursor, Meterless and NVIDIA Nemotron

A prize pool of up to $20,000: $15,000 in cash across the tracks plus $5,000 in in-kind rewards
