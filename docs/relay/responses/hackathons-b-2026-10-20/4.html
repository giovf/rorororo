About The Hackathon

Your chance to shape the future of AI agents and cloud native

This hackathon invites developers, tinkerers, and open source enthusiasts from all backgrounds to get hands-on with building cool MCP servers and AI agents—showing what's possible when we make cloud native more approachable and human-centric through conversations.

Whether you're hacking on MCP, learning agentic AI for the first time, or crafting a tutorial to help others get started, this is your opportunity to learn, contribute, and connect while showcasing the potential of open source technologies like agentgateway, kagent, agentregistry, and more.

This is your chance to show the community what you can do!
