All of the crime by postcode resources on UKCrimeStats
Posted: January 28, 2025 (https://www.ukcrimestats.com/blog/2025/01/28/all-of-the-crime-by-postcode-resources-on-ukcrimestats/) Author: Dan Lewis (https://www.ukcrimestats.com/blog/author/dan/)
If I wanted to know about crime by postcode, or check crime in my postcode, or the crime rate in my postcode, you have come to the best website for it, www.ukcrimestats.com. Look no further, here is everything you can access regarding crime and postcode.
Free to view service . Let’s say the postcode you are interested in is CB22 3HS. Just search on the postcode and it will take you to the page for that postcode https://www.ukcrimestats.com/Postcode/CB223HS . We have 1.7 million pages covering all postcodes. With this free to view page you can see 1 year of data, by crime type, crime within 1 mile, zoom in and out of the map. This page is currently ad supported but if you want to see it without ads, login for a free account for limited access and functionability.
Paid service from £7.49 per month (https://www.ukcrimestats.com/Membership/) . This allows you to see the latest 6 months of data – so today, 28th January 2025, that is June 2024 to November 2024 and all the way back to December 2010. You can also change the radius from 1 mile, to 0.5 or 0.25 miles. This we recommend especially in urban areas as crime changes considerably within just a few streets. See all membership options and details here on our Sign Up page (https://www.ukcrimestats.com/Membership/) . You also get a lot more with analysis and reporting options with membership.
Crime by Postcode Report (https://www.ukcrimestats.com/postcode_reports/) . So you don’t want to trawl through numbers and run queries. No, you just want one authoritative, validated report that does all the work for you. In that case, we recommend buying a postcode report. Here’s a sample report for N1 5TU (https://www.ukcrimestats.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/N15TU_1_2_mile-1-compressed.pdf) . Find out more about our very popular postcode reports here (https://www.ukcrimestats.com/postcode_reports/) . Big discounts for multiple purchases.
All postcodes crime in a big csv file (https://www.ukcrimestats.com/worldpay.php) . So you have some or even a lot of database skills and just want all of the postcodes, their crime totals by radius, crime rate and percentile ranking over the latest 12 months. Easy, head over to our Crime Postcode Datasets page (https://www.ukcrimestats.com/worldpay.php) and buy and download immediately your choice of files – we also have whole calendar years 2019,2020, 2021, 2022,2023 and will soon of course have 2024.
The UKCrimeStats API (https://www.ukcrimestats.com/api/signup.php) . Ok, so you don’t want to build a database, but you have the programming skills to pull data from our API and want use it for your own application or whatever. Go to our API page (https://www.ukcrimestats.com/api/signup.php) , read through the documentation, and you’ll see how easy it is to sign up with a free account and start getting crime postcode data.
YourSites – the Corporate Choice (https://www.ukcrimestats.com/YourSites/) . Your situation is that you are monitoring risk across multiple sites which can be differentiated by postcode. You want to compare them all at once across multiple different reporting criteria. You really don’t want to juggle spreadsheets and endlessly update them. YourSites does it all for you. Developed for Tesco supermarkets to track 1000s of locations simultaneously, you can now use it yourself. Check out the Demo video here (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qH-NzbSOr3A) .
