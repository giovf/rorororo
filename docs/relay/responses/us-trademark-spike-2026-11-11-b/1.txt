Open Data Portal Registration Requirement​
In order to access the Open Data Portal (ODP), starting on June 18, 2026 , you'll need to sign in with a valid USPTO.gov account. Please sign in using the navigation below or create an account using the button in the banner. If you want more information on this initiative, please visit our Register for ODP access page (support/universal-registration) and sign-in today. Additionally, your USPTO.gov account requires multi-factor authentication (MFA). For guidance on MFA setup, please visit the USPTO.gov account FAQs (https://www.uspto.gov/learning-and-resources/account-faqs#subcategory=&category=Multifactor+authentication) .
Also, effective August 18, 2026 , users are required to provide four additional fields of information on their USPTO profile. API keys for accounts that did not complete these fields by October 8, 2026, have been deactivated. To reactivate your key, update these fields in your USPTO.gov account (https://account.uspto.gov/profile) settings by selecting the “Open Data Portal” section at the bottom of the left navigation menu. Next login to ODP and click on the Activate API Key button on the Manage API Key page. See our FAQs (/support/faq) for details.

Register for ODP access
Starting June 18, 2026 all use of the Open Data Portal (ODP) website will require a USPTO.gov account.
The goal of this new initiative is to provide a single, secure account experience for accessing USPTO digital services. This registration will allow the agency to improve the customer profile and lead to better future offerings. And this measure will benefit ODP users by improving ODP site security while limiting costly unregistered bot traffic – keeping ODP safe, fast, and reliable.
Note: Multi-factor authentication (MFA) required
When creating your USPTO.gov account, you will be prompted to enroll in a secure MFA method. This is a required step to maintain access to your account. Be sure to complete MFA enrollment before attempting to sign in to ODP.
For guidance on MFA setup, please visit the USPTO.gov account FAQs (https://www.uspto.gov/learning-and-resources/account-faqs#subcategory=&category=Multifactor+authentication) . For additional help, contact the USPTO Contact Center (https://www.uspto.gov/learning-and-resources/support-centers/uspto-contact-center-ucc) (UCC) at 800-786-9199 (toll free) or 571-272-1000 (local) and select option 3.
What you get with a free USPTO.gov account
You get access to our suite of products, including Patent File Manager, Bulk Data Directories, Final Petition Decisions and more. In these tools you'll be able to:
Search, filter, and explore USPTO open data
View and download detailed information and documents for specific patents and datasets
Customize the data attributes/fields you want to view and download
Run bulk downloads of up to 3,000 records at a time
ID.me verified USPTO.gov accounts get access to our API through a personalized API key
Why are we requiring a USPTO.gov account to access Open Data Portal?
The main objective of this initiative is to keep the ODP system fast and reliable. By signing in with a USPTO.gov account you help protect ODP and USPTO APIs from abuse, supporting fair usage limits and better performance for all users.
Account-based access supports fair usage limits and better performance. It also helps us track demand patterns, improving how we manage our resources so that we can focus on future enhancements to the Open Data Portal experience.
How will you use my USPTO.gov information?
What we will use it for : We use USPTO.gov account information to provide and secure ODP services—such as verifying access, issuing and managing API keys, preventing abuse, and ensuring fair and reliable service. We may also use it to operate and improve ODP, including monitoring performance, troubleshooting issues, and understanding usage trends so we can prioritize improvements.
What we won’t use it for : We won’t use your USPTO.gov account data to sell your personal information, build advertising profiles, or target you with third party advertising. We also won’t use it for unrelated purposes outside providing, protecting, and improving ODP, except as required to meet legal obligations or maintain the security and integrity of USPTO systems.
Why do I have to be ID.me verified in order to get an ODP API key?
Verification enhances our security for online services by verifying the identities of individuals performing actions related to our filing and registering processes. This helps to protect sensitive information while preventing fraud.
Linking your ID.me account to your USPTO.gov account is a one-time registration. Once completed, you will be able to access your API Key anytime.
