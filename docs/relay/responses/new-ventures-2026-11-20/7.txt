How to Apply to Become a Creator
Anyone with a confirmed Superhive (formerly Blender Market) account can open a creator shop in a couple of minutes. There's no application, product proposal or portfolio to send in, and no waiting to be approved: you fill in a short form and your shop is created right away. Before you get started, take a look at Who Can Sell Products on Superhive (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/55-who-can-sell-products-on-blender-market) , which covers who can sell on Superhive (formerly Blender Market) and the rules that apply once you have a shop.
New to selling? Our Getting Started as a Creator (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/387-getting-started-as-a-creator) guide walks you through everything from creating your shop to your first approved product.
Step 1: Create and Confirm Your Account
To open a shop, you need a Superhive (formerly Blender Market) user account with a confirmed email address . If you don't have an account yet, sign up first, then click the link in the confirmation email we send you.
If you aren't signed in when you try to open a shop, you'll be asked to create an account or log in first. If your email address isn't confirmed yet, you'll be asked to confirm it before you can continue.
Step 2: Click Start Selling
Click Start selling at the top right of the header on any Superhive (formerly Blender Market) page, next to the Login / Signup links (or your account menu if you're signed in). This opens the Become a Creator (https://superhivemarket.com/become-a-creator) page, which explains what selling on Superhive looks like and how your first product is reviewed.
The Start selling link only appears in the header on desktop-sized screens. On a phone or tablet, go straight to the Become a Creator (https://superhivemarket.com/become-a-creator) page instead, or use the Sell on Superhive button on the homepage.
[The Start selling link in the site header and the Start selling button on the Become a Creator page]
On the Become a Creator page, click Start selling to open the shop setup form.
Step 3: Fill Out the Shop Setup Form
The form only asks for what we need to set up your shop. There's no proposal, portfolio, sample file, phone number or mailing address.
Shop Name and Shop URL
Your shop name is what customers see at the top of your shop and on your product pages. It can be your own name if you're a freelancer, or your company name.
Your shop URL is the bit at the end of the Superhive (formerly Blender Market) address that identifies your shop. As you type, the form tells you whether the URL is available. You can change it later (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/163-how-to-customize-your-profile) , but doing so changes the address where customers find your shop.
Your Name
Enter your first and last name .
Country and Timezone
Choose the country you're based in and your timezone . Your country should be your country of permanent residence, because it affects how we can pay you. See Can I Be Paid as a Creator on Superhive? (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/273-can-i-be-paid-as-a-creator-on-blender-market)
We can't send payouts to creators in some countries, so we can't set up shops for creators based there. If you choose one of them as your country, the form shows We can't set up shops for creators based in … and no shop is created.
Creator Newsletter
Want to stay up-to-date on news and upcoming changes for Superhive (formerly Blender Market) Creators? Tick the optional Creator Newsletter box. It goes out when we have new features or important information to share—usually once every few weeks.
Creator Terms
To open a shop, you need to accept the Creator Terms of Use (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/44-terms-of-use-creator) (a.k.a. the Creator Agreement).
Step 4: Click Create My Shop
Click Create my shop . That's it—there's nothing to wait for. You'll go straight to your Creator Dashboard, where a green Your shop is ready message welcomes you.
Step 5: Set Up Payouts and Create Your First Product
Your dashboard shows a welcome checklist to help you get going. Two things matter most:
Set up Payouts. You need a working payout method (Stripe, or a PayPal email address we can pay to) before you can submit a product for review. You add it in your Account settings (https://superhivemarket.com/account) ( My Account > Account > Primary Info , in the Payout Method section), and you'll be asked for your current password. See Can I Be Paid as a Creator on Superhive? (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/273-can-i-be-paid-as-a-creator-on-blender-market)
Create your first product. Follow How to Upload Your Products (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/41-how-to-upload-products) , then click Submit for Review . Our team reviews every new creator's first product before it goes live. Reviews typically take 3–5 days.
New creators can have one product in progress at a time until their first product is approved. After that, you can add as many products as you like. Learn more in One Product at a Time for New Creators (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/388-one-product-at-a-time-for-new-creators) .
While you're new, a couple of other things work a little differently: earnings from each sale become payable 30 days after the sale until your first payout has gone through, and you can't email your customers until one of your products is approved. See How and When Creators Are Paid (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/46-how-and-when-creators-get-paid) and How To Email Customers (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/33-how-to-email-customers) .
