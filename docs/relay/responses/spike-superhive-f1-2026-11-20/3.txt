⚡ TweenKit — One-Click Animation
for Blender
Apply. Stack. Mute. Remove.
Create stunning animations in seconds, without losing control of your existing animation. 🎬
TweenKit is a powerful, non-destructive animation toolkit for Blender that combines ready-to-use animation presets, customizable effects, procedural motion, and simulation tools in one intuitive workflow.
✨ 113 animation presets. 14 categories. 37 experimental simulations.
From simple object movements to complex procedural effects, TweenKit helps you bring your ideas to life without spending hours creating animations from scratch.
Your animation. Your rules. Your creativity.
🚀 TweenKit— A New Era of Animation
TweenKit has evolved into a more complete, flexible, and powerful animation toolkit.
Version brings an expanded library, new simulation capabilities, improved effect stacking, a redesigned interface, and significant workflow and stability improvements.
🎬 113 animation presets across 14 categories.
🧪 TweenKit Lab: 37 experimental simulations.
👀 Preview effects before applying them.
🧩 Improved additive effect stacking.
⭐ Favorites, recently used presets, and smarter search.
🎛️ Customizable effect cards with live controls.
🎨 Real mesh deformation and procedural Geometry Nodes effects.
🛠️ Improved stability, compatibility, and usability.
✅ Compatible with Blender 4.5 LTS and later, verified on Blender 4.5 LTS and Blender 5.3.
🎯 Apply. Stack. Mute. Remove.
Animation should be about creativity, not managing endless keyframes.
TweenKit's animation effects live on their own independent NLA tracks , allowing you to experiment without overwriting your existing animation.
✨ Apply — Choose a preset and bring your object to life with one click.
➕ Stack — Combine multiple animation effects on the same object.
🎛️ Customize — Adjust timing, intensity, movement, and effect-specific parameters.
🔇 Mute — Temporarily disable individual effects without deleting them.
❌ Remove — Delete an effect while preserving your original animation.
No unnecessary keyframe hunting. No starting over. Just creative freedom.
💥 113 Ready-to-Use Animation Presets
Explore an extensive library of 113 presets organized into 14 categories , designed for different parts of your Blender workflow.
Whether you're creating motion graphics, product animations, cinematic shots, animated typography, or stylized effects, TweenKit gives you a starting point that remains fully editable.
🎭 Objects & Motion
Create dynamic entrances, movements, rotations, attention-grabbing effects, and seamless loops.
Bounce, slide, spin, hover, shake, stretch, pop, roll, and much more.
📷 Cinematic Cameras
Add movement, energy, and cinematic impact to your shots with camera animation presets.
Create orbits, camera shakes, framing effects, dramatic movements, and more.
💡 Animated Lights
Bring your lighting to life with animated power, flickering, ambient lighting, and dynamic lighting effects.
🔥 Emissive Materials & Glow
Create animated emission effects, glowing pulses, neon-style animations, flickering materials, and more.
Perfect for sci-fi scenes, motion graphics, signs, and stylized environments.
🔤 Animated Typography
Bring text to life with dynamic entrances, exits, motion effects, number animations, and creative typography presets.
🧪 Introducing TweenKit Lab
37 Experimental Simulations. Endless Possibilities.
TweenKit Lab opens the door to a whole new world of creative effects, using Blender's native physics and simulation engines.
Set up simulations directly from TweenKit and customize their behavior through dedicated effect cards.
🎛️ More Control Over Your Simulations
✅ Customizable simulation settings.
✅ Object and collection-based colliders where supported.
✅ Baking controls with progress and cancellation.
✅ Preview, Medium, and Final bake quality for fluid simulations.
✅ Adjustable emission timing, colors, and appearance.
✅ Simulation removal with cleanup of generated helper objects.
🧪 Experimental Feature: TweenKit Lab is an experimental environment. Simulation behavior, appearance, and available controls may continue to evolve in future versions. Some simulations require baking before playback or rendering.
🧠 Non-Destructive by Design
Your existing animation should never get in the way of new ideas.
TweenKit places its animation presets on independent NLA tracks instead of overwriting your original keyframes.
With improved additive stacking, multiple effects can work together on the same object.
Imagine this workflow:
🎬 Start with your existing animation.
➕ Add a Hover effect.
➕ Add a Heartbeat effect.
➕ Add a Spin effect.
🔇 Mute the Spin.
🎛️ Adjust the Heartbeat.
❌ Remove the Hover.
Your original animation remains intact, and the other effects continue working.
Experiment freely. Keep what works. Remove what doesn't.
🎛️ Every Effect Has Its Own Controls
TweenKit is more than a library of fixed animations.
Every applied preset gets its own effect card, displaying the controls relevant to that particular effect.
⏱️ Adjust start and end frames.
🎚️ Change intensity, speed, and animated values.
🔁 Control repeats, stretching, and hold states.
📈 Customize easing and animation curves where supported.
🎨 Adjust colors, emission, deformation, and procedural settings.
🧩 Switch between grouped preset variations.
📋 Duplicate, mute, rebuild, or remove effects individually.
Supported Live controls update the result immediately, while other parameters can be applied through Re-bake or Rebuild.
Need even more control? Open the effect's own keyframes in Blender's Dope Sheet and edit them manually.
👀 Preview Before You Apply
Not sure which animation fits your scene?
Enable Preview mode and try a preset directly on your selected objects before committing to it.
TweenKit plays the effect once and removes the preview automatically, leaving your scene as it was before.
✨ Explore more ideas with less effort.
⭐ A Faster, Cleaner Workflow
TweenKit 2.5.0 introduces a more intuitive interface designed to keep your favorite animation tools within reach.
🎞️ Animated thumbnails: See what presets do before selecting them.
🔍 Smarter search: Find effects by name or descriptive words.
⭐ Favorites: Keep your most-used presets close at hand.
🕒 Recently Used: Quickly access effects you've applied before.
🧩 Grouped presets: Explore multiple variations from a single preset button.
📐 Adaptive interface: A panel that adjusts to the available space in Blender's sidebar.
⚡ Quick Pie Menu: Access common effects with Ctrl+Shift+A.
Browse, preview, apply, and customize — without interrupting your creative workflow.
📐 Stagger, Align & Distribute
Working with multiple objects? TweenKit includes additional tools to help organize your scene and create coordinated animations.
⏱️ Stagger
Offset animation start times across multiple objects to create waves, sequential entrances, and cascading motion effects.
📏 Align
Align selected objects along the X, Y, and Z axes using their bounds or origins.
↔️ Distribute
Evenly distribute multiple objects across selected axes for cleaner layouts and compositions.
Perfect for motion graphics, product presentations, typography, and multi-object animations.

💻 Compatibility & Requirements
Blender: 4.5.0 LTS or later.
Verified versions: Blender 4.5 LTS and Blender 5.3.
Operating systems: Windows, macOS, and Linux.
Dependencies: No additional installations required. TweenKit uses Blender's native tools and engines.
Internet connection: Not required to use the add-on.
Important: TweenKit 2.5.0 requires Blender 4.5 LTS or newer. Some advanced simulations may require additional processing time, memory, disk space, or baking depending on your scene and settings.
🔄 Easy Updates
Keep TweenKit up to date directly from the add-on's preferences.
The Update from ZIP feature allows you to install newer versions using a downloaded ZIP file while preserving your scenes, settings, and shortcuts.
If an update fails to load correctly, TweenKit automatically restores the previous version.
Existing TweenKit effects remain in your Blender projects as native NLA tracks, even when the add-on is disabled or removed.
