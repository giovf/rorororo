Have you ever wanted to use Blender as if you were inside a retro Xbox console? Well now you can!
This Xbox theme for Blender changes the colors of the UI to look like the Original Xbox theme. It includes a Blender theme file and 2 Matcaps (as png); regular green and a Glow/Shine green.

- Also works with Maya Config Lite and Maya Config Pro!
