Fold it. Dress it. Rig it. Render it.
Boxwright is a Blender add-on that builds a folding box as one continuous sheet of board, with real thickness and creases that bend. Thirty box styles, eleven boards measured from real ones, a rig that animates anywhere and ten photo studios, from the Boxwright tab of the sidebar.
[Flat sheets folding into a kraft mailer, a takeout pail and a black petal box]
For anyone who has to show a box before it exists: a packaging mockup for a client, product shots and e-commerce images, an unboxing animation, motion graphics, a preview of a dieline.
Thirty boxes, each from a real one
Pick a section, pick a picture, press Create Box. Every style follows the construction of a real box and its dieline, with its FEFCO or ECMA code where there is one, and presets in real sizes.
[Thumbnails of the thirty box styles]
Basics : Mailer, Tray, Shipping Box, Straight Tuck End, Reverse Tuck End, Sleeve, Envelope, Double-Wall Tray, Five Panel Folder.
Gift : Gable Box, Drawer Box, Telescope Box, Book Box, Petal Box, Shoulder Box, Suitcase Box.
Food : Wedge Box, Takeout Box, Window Cake Box, Clamshell Box, Pizza Box, Gable Top Carton, Brick Carton, Brick Carton with Cap.
Retail : Display Box, Hang Tab Carton, Presentation Folder.
Shapes : Pyramid, Prism Box with 3 to 12 sides, Tapered Tub.
Fold it live, at any size
One sheet means no gaps: the creases bend, so corners close like cardboard and not like loose planes. Fold it flat, assembled or closed with one button, or crease by crease; drawers and telescope lids slide. Type a width, a height, a board thickness or a crease width and the sheet rebuilds; each style knows its limits, so the flaps always meet.
[Screen recording: the Folds and Poses sliders folding a mailer, crease by crease]
[Screen recording: a prism box going from 3 to 12 sides, then taller and narrower]
Board that looks like board
Eleven boards built from scans, mill datasheets and photographs, at their real scale: Kraft Corrugated, White-Top Corrugated, Double-Wall Corrugated, Exposed Flute, Kraft Paperboard, White Card, White-Lined Chipboard, Gray Chipboard, Black Card, Milk Carton and Long-Life Carton with foil inside. One board for the whole box or a different one on each side, and the same colour in Cycles and Eevee.
[Screen recording: the material gallery switching one box through the eleven boards]
[Corrugated cut edge: a wave across the flutes, a straight band along them]
The flutes sit where they really are: a wave on a cut across them, a straight band along them.
[A light sweeping over a milk carton roof: crinkles and waviness bend its reflection]
Crease wrinkles, handling crinkles and waviness on the drink cartons, sized from cartons opened out flat.
Design it flat, see it folded
Export the UV template as SVG or PNG, outside and inside, draw on top of it in any app and load the image back on either face. The artwork keeps the board's grain, and a check grid shows where every panel lands.
[Flat artwork folding into the finished box]
Bake a rig that opens anywhere
Finalize turns the box into a plain mesh with an armature: a control on every crease, with an arrow that shows which way it closes. Edit the mesh by hand and the weights follow. Its drivers need no Python, so the .blend animates on any machine, with or without Boxwright.
[Screen recording: a finalized mailer opened and closed by its rig in Pose Mode]
[The screw cap unscrews and lifts off the carton]
On the Brick Carton with Cap, one slider turns the cap off its thread, 1.3 turns like the real one; then it moves freely.
Ten photo studios, one click
Classic Gray, Catalog White, Dark Premium, Spotlight, Golden Window, Color Pop, Podium, Hero Copy Space, Top Down and Cool Tech. Each sets the backdrop, the lights and a camera that frames your box at any size; Color Pop and Podium take the colour opposite your board, kraft on blue.
[Screen recording: the Photo Studio gallery building eight setups around one box]
[The same box in all ten studio setups]
What you get
30 box styles in five sections, with presets in real sizes
11 boards, one per side if you want, alike in Cycles and Eevee
Live folding, every crease by hand, sliding two-piece boxes
UV templates in SVG and PNG, artwork on both faces, a check grid
Finalize and Build Rig: a mesh and an armature that work without the add-on
10 photo studio setups in a picture gallery
Blender 5.0 to 5.2 LTS, no dependencies, free updates, GPL
Made for mockups, renders and animation. The UV template is an artwork guide, not a production dieline: no millimetre scale and no bleed.
