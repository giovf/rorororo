Traverse builds a concert stage from one sidebar tab: truss, LED walls that play your footage, moving lights with gobos, haze and a crowd. Bake your track's BPM and the whole rig pulses, chases and strobes on the beat. Every setting stays live.
[A concert stage built with Traverse, rendered in Cycles]
6 builders in one tab · LED walls up to 96 x 96 cabinets · Up to 100 fixtures per rig, 8 gobos · Pulse, Chase and Strobe at any BPM · EEVEE and Cycles · Blender 5.0 and newer
How it works
Five steps, top to bottom in the Traverse tab of the 3D Viewport sidebar.
[Add an LED wall, load your footage]
Step 1
Add an LED wall, load your footage
Add LED Wall builds a cabinet wall at the 3D cursor. Type the columns and rows, press Load Media, and your movie or image loops across the cabinets with a real pixel grid.
[Add a truss, hang a light rig]
Step 2
Add a truss, hang a light rig
Add Truss and set its length. With the truss selected, Add Light Rig clamps the fixtures underneath, evenly spaced and all aimed at one target.
[Drop a crowd on the floor]
Step 3
Drop a crowd on the floor
Select a floor mesh and click Add Crowd. Everyone faces the stage, and the panel counts the heads as you change the density.
[Bake your BPM, press play]
Step 4
Bake your BPM, press play
Select the light rig and the LED wall, click Bake BPM and type your tempo. The lights pulse, chase or strobe, the wall pulses, all as plain keyframes.
[Change anything, it stays live]
Step 5
Change anything, it stays live
Curve the wall, stretch the truss, move the target: the stage follows while you drag. Lights on a truss re-space when its length changes.
Three beat effects
Bake one, or switch bar by bar. Every rig keeps Pulse, Chase and Strobe as sliders you can key or drive.
[Pulse]
Pulse
Lights, lenses and LED wall flash on the beat and fade.
[Chase]
Chase
A highlight runs across the fixtures, one sweep per bar.
[Strobe]
Strobe
A hard burst of flicker on every beat.
Every builder, live
Each button builds a rig you keep editing, and the pieces fit each other.
[LED walls that play your footage]
LED walls that play your footage
Set columns, rows and curve, load a movie or an image, and it loops across the cabinets. Pixel pitch draws a real LED grid, so close-ups read as a screen, not a plane.
Up to 96 x 96 cabinets, curved up to 300°
Pixel pitch from 0.8 to 50 mm
MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WebM, MPG, PNG, JPG and EXR
Truss, straight or along any curve
Drag the length and any light rig on it re-spaces, or give it a curve to follow. Corner blocks included.
Zig-Zag, X-Brace or Chords Only
Steel or black finish
Bay length, size and radii
[Truss, straight or along any curve]
[One target. Every head follows.]
One target. Every head follows.
Every fixture in a rig aims at one empty. Drag it or animate it, and the whole rig sweeps together.
Moving Head Spot or Wash Par, up to 100 per rig
Beam angle, edge softness, colour, watts
8 gobos to throw patterns on the floor
A crowd that faces the stage
Scatter an audience on any floor mesh. Everyone turns toward a focus empty, with as much jitter as you like.
Density, scale and colour variation
Exclusion clears a hole around a FOH tower
Your own figures, stood upright for you
[A crowd that faces the stage]
Ready for production
Renders without Traverse
The stage is plain Blender data with the gobos packed, so it renders on any computer or farm.
Haze, from subtle to arena
One click adds a haze box sized to your stage: Subtle, Club or Arena. Previz Mode keeps playback fast.
Follows a real track
Point Audio Trigger or any audio add-on at a rig's Pulse to hit every beat of the song.
Traverse or stock Blender
Everything here can be built by hand. Traverse is the part where it takes minutes, stays editable and moves on the beat.
