What does UK Trademark Scraper do? #what-does-uk-trademark-scraper-do
UK Trademark Scraper searches the UK trade mark register kept by the UK Intellectual Property Office (UK IPO / UKIPO) and returns one row per UK trade mark: number, mark text, status, type, Nice classes, goods and services, owner, filing, registration and renewal dates, opposition window and links. A second mode reads the IPO's weekly Trade Marks Journal , which lists every newly published application with its representative (attorney firm) and postal address. Brand owners, IP attorneys, clearance teams and agencies use it for UK clearance searches, brand watches and prospect lists.
UK trademark search · UKIPO trade mark scraper · UK IPO register API · Trade Marks Journal watch
The whole UK register: 3,674,419 marks on 2026-10-16 (2,325,043 registered, 72,398 pending, 1,265,253 expired, 11,725 ended), by mark text, owner, number, class, status, type, Vienna code and dates.
Goods and services on every mark: the full specification per Nice class, included in the price.
New applications with their representatives: the weekly journal gives each mark's attorney firm with full address and postcode, applicant ids, priority claims, series, colour claims and the mark image.
Past the 10,000-result limit: large searches are split by filing date, so "every mark in class 25 filed this year" is reachable. A completeness line per search compares rows delivered with the register's own total.
Brexit clones labelled: UK009… and UK008… numbers (comparable marks created from EU trade marks and EU-designating international registrations) carry their origin and the EU / WIPO number.
Monitor mode: schedule it and receive only new marks and status or date changes.
No login, no API key, no browser. Pay only for the marks delivered.

How to scrape UK trade marks with UK Trademark Scraper #how-to-scrape-uk-trade-marks-with-uk-trademark-scraper
Create a free Apify account (https://console.apify.com/sign-up) (the free plan includes monthly credit).
Open UK Trademark Scraper and choose What to read : the UK register search or the Trade Marks Journal.
Type a mark (e.g. nike ), an owner (e.g. Diageo ) or a number (e.g. UK00003904654 ), and add any filters.

Clearance tips #clearance-tips
Run the same mark with contains and fuzzy : fuzzy finds spelling variants ("nike" 249 vs 3,365 marks).
Add niceClasses for the classes you will file in; a mark covering any of them is kept.
Set statuses to live to drop expired and ended marks.

Limits & empty runs #limits-and-empty-runs
One register query returns at most 10,000 marks; bigger searches are split by filing date automatically. Marks with no filing date cannot be reached that way and are counted in RUN_SUMMARY .
The register source has no mark images (it serves a placeholder for every UK mark); images come from the journal or the IPO record.
Applicant addresses are not in the journal or the register search; they come with the IPO record.
The journal lists about a year of weekly issues (51 on 2026-10-16), about 3,600 marks each.
The IPO record reads one page per mark, about 1.2 s apart; if the IPO's site shows its security check, the record is skipped for the rest of the run and the rows are still delivered.
A run with 0 rows means nothing matched (see RUN_SUMMARY ); nothing is charged.
Measured speed: 5 marks in 5 s; 100 marks with goods and services in 25 s; a full journal issue (3,641 marks, 1,566 images) in 26 s.
❓ FAQ #faq
How much does UK Trademark Scraper cost? #how-much-does-uk-trademark-scraper-cost
$0.0009 per trade mark, with goods and services, journal images and the optional IPO record included. 100 marks cost $0.09.
How many UK trade marks can UK Trademark Scraper return? #how-many-uk-trade-marks-can-uk-trademark-scraper-return
The whole register is 3.67 million marks. A single query reaches 10,000; larger searches are read in filing-date windows. A full journal issue is about 3,600 marks.
Does UK Trademark Scraper return the owner's address? #does-uk-trademark-scraper-return-the-owners-address
Yes, with includeIpoRecord : the owner's postal address, country of incorporation and, where the IPO shows it, the company registration number. The register search and the journal give the owner's name.
Can UK Trademark Scraper find who represents a trade mark? #can-uk-trademark-scraper-find-who-represents-a-trade-mark
Yes. Journal rows carry the representative firm with full address (46% of new applications have one); the IPO record gives the representative for any UK mark.
Can I watch new UK trade mark filings with UK Trademark Scraper? #can-i-watch-new-uk-trade-mark-filings-with-uk-trademark-scraper
Yes. Schedule journal mode weekly with your words in searchQueries and monitorMode on; each new published mark arrives once, with its opposition deadline.
Does UK Trademark Scraper cover EU trade marks? #does-uk-trademark-scraper-cover-eu-trade-marks
It covers the UK register, including the UK comparable marks created from EU trade marks on 1 January 2021 (UK009…). EU trade marks themselves are in our EUIPO Trademark Scraper.
