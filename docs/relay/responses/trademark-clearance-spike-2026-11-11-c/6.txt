About UKIPO
What is UKIPO?
Intellectual Property Office, Newport, Wales, United Kingdom.
The Intellectual Property Office (UKIPO) is the United Kingdom's national trademark registry, operating as an executive agency of the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT) from its headquarters in Newport, Wales. Since the UK's departure from the European Union on 1 January 2021, UKIPO has been a wholly independent filing jurisdiction, EU trademarks no longer extend to the UK, and a separate UK filing (or Madrid designation of GB) is now required for UK protection.
At the moment of Brexit, UKIPO automatically cloned every existing EU Trade Mark and EU-designated International Registration onto the UK register as a "comparable UK right". These appear under two specific prefixes, UK009 for cloned EUTMs and UK008 for cloned EU designations of International Registrations, and retain the original filing, priority, and seniority dates. Roughly 1.4M comparable rights were created overnight, more than doubling the size of the UK register and creating a distinct numbering cohort that any serious UK trademark workflow has to handle correctly.
UKIPO runs the Trade Marks Act 1994 examination regime, with a strong absolute-grounds review, a two-month opposition window (extendable to three months by filing Form TM7A, a notice of threatened opposition), and 10-year renewable terms. The office has historically exposed register data through the Ipsum service, which is now being replaced by the "One IPO Search" platform and a planned bulk-data API. Signa ingests UKIPO data through the available register endpoints and the published bulk files, normalizing UK national filings, Madrid designations of GB, UK009 comparable EUTMs, and UK008 comparable International Registrations into a single unified schema with the same status vocabulary used across every other office on the platform.
What makes it different
Post-Brexit jurisdiction, UK is a fully independent filing system since 1 January 2021, no longer covered by EUTMs
Comparable UK rights, ~1.4M EUTMs cloned to UK register on Brexit day with UK009 prefix; EU designations of IRs cloned with UK008 prefix
Madrid Protocol member, UK can be designated through WIPO, and UK marks can use Madrid for international filings
Two-month opposition window from advertisement, extendable to three months by filing Form TM7A (notice of threatened opposition)
Absolute grounds examination, UKIPO actively refuses descriptive, non-distinctive, and deceptive marks under Section 3
Relative grounds search-and-notify, UKIPO searches earlier marks and notifies owners of conflicting applications, but does not refuse on that basis
Multi-class single application, one filing can cover all 45 Nice classes (fees scale per class above the first)
Series marks (legacy), up to six fundamentally identical variations in one application; being phased out under the digital transformation programme
10-year term, renewable indefinitely in 10-year blocks; no use-based maintenance declarations (unlike USPTO)

FAQ
UKIPO API questions
Does the UKIPO have a free public trademark API?
Not in the way USPTO or EUIPO do, no. UKIPO has historically offered the Ipsum service for per-mark lookups, which is being retired and replaced by the "One IPO Search" platform. UKIPO has publicly committed to a bulk-data API as part of the 2026 digital transformation programme, but it is not yet a general-purpose REST API anyone can register for. In the meantime, the practical paths are scraping the public search tool, parsing the weekly Trade Marks Journal, or applying for a direct UKIPO data-access agreement. Signa packages all of that into a normalized REST API behind a single key.
What was Ipsum and what replaces it?
Ipsum was UKIPO's long-running online register search tool, it handled lookups for patents, trade marks, and designs by application or registration number. UKIPO is replacing it with "One IPO Search", a unified search experience covering all three IP types with saved searches, notifications, and AI-assisted search planned for the rollout. Programmatic access via APIs is on the One IPO roadmap for 2026.
How do I get a UKIPO API key today?
There is no self-service UKIPO trademark API key as of mid-2026. UKIPO does grant data-access agreements to specific organisations (search-and-monitoring providers, large law firms, statistical researchers) on request. These agreements typically involve a written application, a use-case description, and bespoke delivery terms. For Signa: sign up at app.signa.so, copy your API key from the dashboard, and pass it as Authorization: Bearer. The same key works for UKIPO and every other office Signa covers.
What are the UKIPO rate limits?
There are no published rate limits because there is no published public API. The One IPO Search web tool enforces typical anti-abuse throttling for human use. Signa's API does not pass any UKIPO-side limit through to you, we share the underlying ingestion quota across customers and surface our own per-key rate limits (300 req/min on the free tier, higher on paid plans).
What happened to my EU Trade Mark after Brexit?
On 1 January 2021, UKIPO automatically created a "comparable UK right" for every EUTM that was registered as of 31 December 2020. The comparable UK mark has the last eight digits of the EUTM prefixed with UK009, retains the original EUTM filing, priority, and seniority dates, and is a fully independent UK registration that can be renewed, assigned, licensed, or challenged separately from the original EUTM. No action was required from the owner and no fee was charged. EU designations of International Registrations were cloned the same way but with a UK008 prefix.
How does Signa handle UK comparable rights vs original EUTMs?
Signa stores UK008 and UK009 comparable rights as first-class UKIPO records, they appear on UKIPO searches, have UK statuses, UK renewal deadlines, and a GB jurisdiction. We additionally populate a parent_office reference pointing back to the originating EUTM record on EUIPO, so you can resolve the EU parent in a single follow-up query. Filter offices: "GB" and you get the UK rights; filter offices: "EM" and you get the surviving EU rights; pivot through parent_office and you can show the post-Brexit lineage in your UI without writing custom logic per number prefix.
Do I still need to file at UKIPO if I have an EUTM?
Yes, for any application filed on or after 1 January 2021. EUTMs filed before Brexit were cloned to UK comparable rights automatically, but new EUTMs no longer extend to the UK. To protect a mark in both jurisdictions today you either file a UK national application at UKIPO and an EUTM at EUIPO, or use the Madrid Protocol to designate both GB and EM (the EU designation). Signa indexes all three so you can verify coverage across the post-Brexit landscape in one query.
How fresh is UKIPO data on Signa?
Signa runs a daily UKIPO ingestion that picks up new applications from the Trade Marks Journal and status changes from the register. New records are queryable on the API the same day they are published. Every record includes an updated_at timestamp and a source_data_date so you can verify freshness directly inside the data.
