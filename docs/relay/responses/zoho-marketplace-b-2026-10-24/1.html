Create a company profile

The first step to building and listing an application is to sign up and create a company profile on Zoho Marketplace. The applications you develop can only be submitted or published in Marketplace once you create a company profile.

Why create a company profile?

  • To give you visibility, your company profile will be displayed on the listing page of your application on Marketplace.
  • To get access to news and updates about developer-focused events associated with Zoho Marketplace.
  • To allow us to filter applications by vendor and identify verified partners.

Steps to create a company profile

  • Visit the Zoho Marketplace home page and sign up.
  • Click on the Profile icon on the home page and choose the Partner Console option.
  • Select Company profile on the left panel.
  • Enter the necessary details in each tab and click Next to navigate to the next tab.
  • Click the Finish button once you have filled in all the particulars.
  • Preview the draft of your company profile and use the Edit option to make changes if necessary.
  • Editing Company Profile - Super Admin of the Org can edit the company details and submit it. The edited changes will undergo a review process, and after verification/validation, the respective company profile changes will be updated in Marketplace.
  • Delete a Marketplace vendor account - A Marketplace vendor can request for vendor account deletion using the "Delete Account" option. The request will undergo a review process, and necessary actions will be taken. Upon deletion, the vendor will not be able to access Marketplace Partner Console and cannot submit and publish extension(s)/ app(s) in Zoho Marketplace.
  • To add people involved in the creation of application or extension, go to Company Profile > People > Add New and enter their email address and role.
