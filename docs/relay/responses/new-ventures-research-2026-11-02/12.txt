4. Payments
Revenue you may earn under the Creator Monetization Program are calculated based on our tracking mechanisms. You can see the estimated amount you are earning in your account settings. Estimates may differ from earnings paid out under the program, as earnings paid may be adjusted for refunds or chargebacks, taxes, or fraud. Actual earnings will be reflected in your payout reports from the Payment Agent. Payments from the Poe Creator Monetization Program will come from Quora, Inc.

You acknowledge and agree that funds may take up to 10 days to process. “Payment Agent” means Quora’s payment agent, which is currently Stripe, Inc., and which may be changed by Quora from time to time upon notice to you.
