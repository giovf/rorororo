Creator Policy
Superhive (formerly Blender Market) is a marketplace where you can offer your Blender goods directly to buyers around the world. Our goal is to help you succeed and earn a living with your work. We want to make sure purchasing from your Superhive (formerly Blender Market) shop is a positive experience for you and your customers. Read on to find out more about your rights, as well as what is expected of you, as a Creator.
This policy is a part of our Terms of Use (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/44-terms-of-use-creator) . By opening a Superhive (formerly Blender Market) shop, you're agreeing to this policy and our Terms of Use.

1. Shop Basics
Keeping in mind these basic requirements will set you up for success on Superhive (formerly Blender Market).
a. What can be sold on Superhive (formerly Blender Market)?
Superhive (formerly Blender Market) is a unique marketplace dedicated entirely to Blender products. Buyers come here to purchase items that they might not find anywhere else. Everything listed for sale on Superhive (formerly Blender Market) must be original work.
You agree that:
All products are made or designed by you. You are allowed to collaborate with a partner or partners who assist you with the creation of original work.
You are using your own images or video content that accurately represents the product and its use, not stock photos, images not created by or representing the use of the product, catalogue images, or photos used by other sellers or sites. See also Listing Imagery and Buyer Confusion (#listing-imagery) .
Stand-alone products (products that are not add-ons and have their own binary executable file) meet the following conditions:
The product integrates with Blender, extends its functionality, or directly benefits a Blender-specific workflow.
The features and functionality of the product are not possible (or very infeasible) as an add-on.
The product's license is compatible with the GPL.
b. What Can't be Sold on Superhive (formerly Blender Market)
All listings must offer an item for sale. You may not create a Superhive (formerly Blender Market) listing for the purpose of sharing a referral code, posting a want ad, or similar activity that does not offer a digital item for sale.
Reselling is not allowed on Superhive (formerly Blender Market). Reselling refers to listing an item that you were not involved in designing or making.
Keep in mind that members may flag listings that appear to violate our policies for Superhive (formerly Blender Market)'s review. Superhive (formerly Blender Market) may remove any listings that violate our policies. Superhive (formerly Blender Market) may also suspend or terminate your account for any violations and revoke any earnings or commissions associated with the account.
c. Managing your Superhive (formerly Blender Market) Shop
Your shop represents you and your business to the Blender community. It's important that you, your items and your shop are honestly and accurately represented.
By creating a shop on Superhive (formerly Blender Market), you agree that you will:
Provide honest, accurate information on your Product Page.
Ensure your shop content, such as any text, photos or videos used to represent yourself, your shop or your listings, abide by Superhive (formerly Blender Market)'s policies, including our Anti-Discrimination policy.
Accurately represent your items in listings and listing photos.
Respect the intellectual property of others. Products that contain third-party trademarks, brand names, logos, characters, or other protected IP are not allowed on Superhive. See our Intellectual Property Rights Policy (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/334-intellectual-property-rights-policy) for details on what we accept, what we don't, and how to report a violation.
Not publish listing imagery that is substantially confusable with the listing imagery of an existing product in the same category. See Listing Imagery and Buyer Confusion (#listing-imagery) below.
Not create duplicate shops or take any other action (such as manipulating clicks, carts or sales) for the purpose of shilling, manipulating search or circumventing Superhive (formerly Blender Market)'s policies.
Not coordinate pricing with other sellers.
Listing Imagery and Buyer Confusion
Buyers rely on listing imagery to tell products apart. Imagery that closely mirrors an existing listing makes that harder and damages trust in the marketplace, including for the creator being imitated.
Categories develop visual conventions, and working within them is not a violation. Products of the same type will often share similar subject matter, layout, and presentation.
What is not permitted is imagery that, taken as a whole, a buyer would likely confuse with an existing listing. We look at the combination of elements rather than any single one, including layout, color palette, typography, promotional badge treatment and wording, title structure, and publication timing. Any one in common is usually coincidence. Several together are not.
Where we find a listing confusable with an existing one, we will ordinarily ask the creator to revise the imagery within a set period, and the product may remain for sale during that time. Listings that are not revised, and repeat instances by the same creator, may be removed. We may remove a listing without a revision request where the similarity is severe or appears deliberate.
d. Creator Standards
By listing a product on Superhive (formerly Blender Market) you understand and agree that you are responsible for complying with all applicable laws and regulations for the products, including any required licensing. Superhive (formerly Blender Market) assumes no responsibility for the accuracy, labeling, or content of your listings.
Meeting Service Level Standards
As a Creator, you must provide great customer service and maintain trust with your buyers. Superhive (formerly Blender Market) may reach out to you if your shop fails to meet Superhive (formerly Blender Market)'s Creator standards.
By setting up a shop on Superhive (formerly Blender Market), you agree to:
Have uploaded product files to be deliverable upon completion of the transaction.
Respond to messages in a timely manner.
Honor the commitments you make in your product page.
Resolve disagreements or disputes directly with the buyer in a professional manner. In the unlikely event that you can't reach a resolution, Superhive (formerly Blender Market) can help. Escalate the conversation to Admin and email [email protected] (/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#aad9dfdadac5d8deead9dfdacfd8c2c3dccfc7cbd8c1cfde84c9c5c7) .
Honor the Superhive (formerly Blender Market) Refund Policy (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/22-refund-policy) .
e. Subscription Fees
Creators may be charged for using some of Superhive (formerly Blender Market)'s services. There are fees associated with some of the subscriptions available on Superhive (formerly Blender Market) (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/185-how-merchant-and-transaction-fees-are-calculated) .
2. Being a Member of the Superhive (formerly Blender Market) Community
At Superhive (formerly Blender Market), everyone is expected to treat fellow members of the Superhive (formerly Blender Market) community with respect. As a Creator, you have additional responsibilities to provide a great customer experience.
a. Creating and Uploading Content
As a member of Superhive (formerly Blender Market), you have the opportunity to create and upload a variety of content, like listings, Messages, text, photos, and videos. In order to keep our community safe and respectful, you agree that you will not upload content that is:
Abusive, threatening, defamatory, or harassing;
Graphic, obscene, or vulgar;
In violation of someone else's privacy or intellectual property rights (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/334-intellectual-property-rights-policy) ; or
False, deceptive, or misleading.
b. Communication Standards
Messages
With Superhive (formerly Blender Market) Inbox Messages, you have the ability to communicate directly with your buyers or other Superhive (formerly Blender Market) members. Messages are a great way for buyers to ask you questions about a product.
Messages may not be used for the following activities:
Sending unsolicited advertising or promotions, requests for donations, or spam;
Harassing or abusing another member;
Contacting someone after they have explicitly asked you not to;
Interfering with a transaction or the business of another member;
Exchanging personal contact, financial or other information for the purposes of evading the checkout process on Superhive (formerly Blender Market), including phone number, address, email, social media handles, external URLs, directions to purchase via another site, instructions for money transfer, etc.
Interference
Interference occurs when a member intentionally interferes with another member's shop in order to drive away their business. Interference is strictly prohibited on Superhive (formerly Blender Market). Examples of interference include:
Contacting another member via Superhive (formerly Blender Market) Messages to warn them away from a particular member, shop, or item;
Posting in public areas to demonstrate or discuss a dispute with another member;
Purchasing from a creator for the sole purpose of leaving a negative review;
Maliciously clicking on a competitor's Promoted Listings ads in order to drain that member's advertising budget, also known as "click fraud."
Harassment
Any use of Superhive (formerly Blender Market) Messages to harass other members is strictly prohibited. Similarly, Messages may not be used to support or glorify hatred. If you receive a Message that violates this policy, please let us know right away.
Creator Discord
Creator Discord is a public space provided by Superhive (formerly Blender Market) where creators can connect, and these communication standards must be followed. This should be a helpful and encouraging space for Creators to work together and support each other.
3. Feedback, Disputes and Your Success
a. Reviews
Reviews are a great way for you to build a reputation on Superhive (formerly Blender Market). Customers can leave a review, including a one to five star rating and a review of your product/support.
On any customer review, you can leave a response.
Reviews and your response to reviews may not:
Contain private information;
Contain graphic, mature, or obscene language or imagery;
Contain hateful or derogatory language or imagery;
Contain prohibited medical drug claims;
Contain advertising or spam;
Be about things outside the seller's control, such as personal device limitations, Superhive (formerly Blender Market) or a third party;
Contain threats, harassment, or extortion;
Include shilling or otherwise falsely inflate a shop's review score. Shilling can include compensating a third party through free or significantly reduced items, samples, cash, or other compensation, to purchase and leave a biased, inauthentic, or untruthful positive review; or
Undermine the integrity of the Reviews system.
b. Your Shop and Superhive (formerly Blender Market)'s Terms
In order to keep Superhive (formerly Blender Market) safe and improve our Services, we may take actions that limit the visibility of your shop, or product page, or that impact your payment account. In the event a shop sees unusual order activity, or we otherwise believe that your actions or shop may result in buyer disputes, chargebacks, increased risk of fraud, counterfeiting, or other claims, Superhive (formerly Blender Market) may take actions such as limiting visibility of your account, or placing restrictions or reserves on your payments account, in accordance with our Terms of Service (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/44-terms-of-use-creator) , including this Policy. When appropriate and permitted by law, Superhive (formerly Blender Market) will communicate information to the affected Creator about the issue.
If Superhive (formerly Blender Market) has reason to believe you, your Content, or your use of the services violate our terms, including this Creator Policy, we may deactivate your content or suspend or terminate your account (and any accounts Superhive (formerly Blender Market) determines is related to your account) and your access to the Services. Generally, Superhive (formerly Blender Market) will notify you that Your Content or account has been suspended or terminated, unless you've repeatedly violated our Terms or we have legal or regulatory reasons preventing us from notifying you.
