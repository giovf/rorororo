One Product at a Time for New Creators
When you first open your shop on Superhive (formerly Blender Market), you can have one product in progress at a time until your first product is approved. This keeps our review queue moving so every new creator gets a timely first review. As soon as your first product is approved, the limit is lifted for good.
Already selling? If you were a creator before self-registration launched, or you've already had a product approved, this doesn't apply to you. You can add as many products as you like.
What Counts as "In Progress"
A product is in progress when it is:
a draft you're still working on, or
waiting for review (shown as Pending on My Products) after you clicked Submit for Review .
While you have a product in progress, it holds your one slot.
Where You'll See the Limit
While your slot is taken, the New Product button is disabled. You'll see One product at a time until your first is approved , with a link to the product you're working on. This appears:
on My Products in your Creator Dashboard, and
in the creator menu on the storefront.
If you go to the new-product page directly (for example, from a bookmark), you'll be sent back with an explanation.
What Happens at Each Stage
Your product is…
Slot
What you can do
A draft
Taken
Keep editing it, then click Submit for Review .
Waiting for review
Taken
Editing is paused until our team has reviewed it.
Changes requested
Still taken
The product goes back to draft. Make the changes in the review message and submit it again.
Declined
Freed
Revise this product and resubmit it, or start a new product with New Product .
Deleted
Freed
New Product works again.
Approved
Limit lifted
Add as many products as you like.
Declined products and new products: if you start a new product after a decline, the new product takes your slot. You won't be able to resubmit the declined product while another product is in progress. Decide which one you want to continue with before you start something new.
After Your First Approval
When your first product is approved, you'll see Your first product is approved, so you can now add as many products as you like. The limit never comes back, even if a later product has changes requested or is declined.
Tips for a Smooth First Review
Set up payouts first. You can't submit a product for review until you have a working payout method. See Can I Be Paid as a Creator on Superhive? (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/273-can-i-be-paid-as-a-creator-on-blender-market)
Follow the guidelines. Read the General Product Guidelines (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/253-general-product-guidelines-for-uploading-products) and Why Products Get Rejected (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/186-why-products-get-rejected) before you submit.
Pick your strongest product first. Because you can only work on one product at a time until it's approved, start with the one that's most ready.
For more on what happens during review, see Your First Product Review (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/209-creator-application-product-proposal-and-review) . For the full journey from sign-up to first sale, see Getting Started as a Creator (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/387-getting-started-as-a-creator) .
