What if Blender had one button that bakes displacement maps from multires, easily? And what if that same addon also gave you HD geometry, like in ZBrush, with baking included?
This add-on does both.
HD Geometry: sculpt high-res detail and bake it out. Hover the mesh and press Y to pick the HD region you want to work on. HD bakes are saved with a 0.5 midlevel and the suffix "bump".
Everything is lossless. You can jump between HD regions as much as you want and no detail gets softened or lost along the way. HD data is saved to disk next to your .blend file, so you can save incremental HD versions separately and go back to an earlier one when you want.
Multires displacement baking: bake regular displacement from your multires levels. You can set the midlevel and resolution, and combine several objects into the same map in one bake. These are saved with the suffix "displacement".
All baking goes through Multiresolution. Both bake as 32-bit EXR and support UDIMs.
Since the two maps are saved separately, you can combine them in your shader and decide how much of the HD detail should show, or amplify it if you want more.
