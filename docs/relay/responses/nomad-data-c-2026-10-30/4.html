Diversified
This provider delivers authoritative, entity-resolved, and versioned datasets focused on emerging markets, with coverage spanning trade, sanctions and politically exposed persons, education institutions, procurement, corporate entities, and ESG-related reference data. Datasets feature historical version control, detailed provenance, confidence scores, and explicit data gap tracking, supporting analytics, compliance, sanctions screening, supply chain intelligence, and risk management workflows. Data is available for India, select international markets, and the EU, and is delivered in accessible formats including Excel, CSV, and Parquet.
Electric Vehicle
Corporate provider offering electric and hybrid vehicle battery replacement, reconditioning, installation services, warranty fulfillment, DIY part sales, and battery diagnostics across multiple U.S. states. Available data includes detailed insights on battery types, component availability, pricing, installation locations, service demand trends, customer scheduling, and vehicle compatibility. Datasets support analysis of regional electrified vehicle aftermarket activity, inventory forecasting, parts sourcing, and market opportunities for electric and hybrid vehicle maintenance.
AI Infrastructure
This provider delivers structured intelligence connecting data center capacity, power and interconnection activity, project development timelines, ownership, and investment metrics across the AI and digital infrastructure landscape. The datasets track the evolution of physical infrastructure and power markets at the individual site and project level, integrating company, financial, and private-market data for comprehensive market, investment, and due diligence analysis. Coverage enables investors and enterprises to analyze infrastructure from local assets through to sector and public market trends, with delivery via research platforms, API, and data licensing.
Healthcare
This provider delivers AI-driven oral health data based on a curated set of over 18,000 annotated intra-oral images, including detailed zone-level labels for conditions such as calculus, caries, and inflamed gums. Using deep learning models, the data identifies and scores probable oral pathologies, supporting dental practices in remote patient triage, diagnosis, and post-treatment monitoring. Structured results, such as detection location and per-zone confidence, are made available through a secure, healthcare-compliant digital environment.
Financial
This provider offers extensive financial and real estate datasets focused on the German and European markets, including historical and live data on interest rates, mortgage and loan conditions, bank account fees, and deposit products. Their analytics tools and studies deliver granular insights on market trends, consumer demand, rate developments, and provider benchmarking, facilitating strategic decision-making for banks, insurers, real estate professionals, and publishers. Data access is available via interactive dashboards, API-ready calculators, downloadable reports, and integration-ready widgets, enabling real-time market monitoring, product comparison, and deep historical analysis.
Hospitality
This provider delivers comprehensive, independent cruise fare and ancillary pricing datasets sourced from millions of daily retail price snapshots across major global cruise lines. Coverage includes ship and itinerary details, cabin categories, price-per-person-per-night metrics, booking patterns, onboard add-on pricing, and promotional trends. The data supports institutions and analysts seeking granular insight into cruise market dynamics, competitor benchmarking, fare movement analysis, and consumer pricing behaviors.
Aviation
This provider offers aviation market intelligence and fare monitoring data, delivering insights on lowest available fares, competitor fare movements, pricing signals, and market-level risk and opportunity indicators across global airline routes, channels, and points of sale. Datasets include structured fare tracking, market prioritization scores, revenue risk assessments, demand momentum signals, and execution-ready pricing recommendations, enabling airline commercial teams to monitor market changes, prioritize routes, and optimize pricing strategies. The data supports revenue management, competitive analysis, and controlled pricing actions through explainable, decision-oriented market signals and performance tracking.
AI Training
This provider delivers structured machine-readable data on physical spaces—such as homes, offices, venues, and showrooms—using spatial AI and photorealistic 3D capture to extract detailed information about rooms, objects, materials, dimensions, geometry, and contextual surroundings. Interaction analytics offer insights into visitor behavior and intent, tracking navigation patterns, dwell times, questions, and conversion signals within browser-based virtual tours. The datasets support AI training, virtual space understanding, enhanced property search, commercial analytics, space optimization, and digital archiving of exhibitions with object-level metadata.
Business
This provider delivers comprehensive market intelligence and business data to banks and large retailers, offering detailed insights into competitive pricing, customer behavior, and market trends. Data offerings include in-depth bank rate comparisons, compliance and regulatory audits, customer service and sales evaluations, and sustainability assessments. Real-time analytics support decision-making related to pricing strategies, customer engagement improvement, and maintaining compliance across the US market.
Aviation
This provider delivers privacy-safe, real-time airport and aviation audience intelligence using aggregated travel signals, flight schedules, itinerary flows, seating configurations, and public demographic data for all IATA airports globally. The data includes detailed profiles of passenger populations such as nationality mix, age group, income bracket, traveler type, top interest categories, footfall, dwell time, and travel purpose, with granular insights available at terminal and gate levels. Use cases include airport audience analysis, passenger segmentation, campaign reach optimization, cross-airport comparison, and travel forecasting for stakeholders across the travel, aviation, and advertising ecosystem.
