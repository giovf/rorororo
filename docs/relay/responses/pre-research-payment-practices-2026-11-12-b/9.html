UK Supplier Payment Practices API

Overview

The UK Supplier Payment Practices API exposes the payment performance that large UK companies are legally required to report: average days to pay, the share of invoices paid outside agreed terms, and the payment terms themselves. It covers 9,149 companies keyed on Companies House number, with the full history of reporting periods for each one.

It is built for suppliers assessing whether a prospective customer actually pays on time, and for credit and procurement teams pricing payment risk. The benchmarks endpoint puts any single company in context, returning the market distribution from the 10th through the 90th percentile.

Features

  • 9,149 Companies: Payment practice records keyed on Companies House company number.
  • Reporting History: Every reported period per company, paginated with page and per_page.
  • Market Benchmarks: Percentile distribution for days-to-pay and invoices outside terms, p10 through p90.
  • Name Search: Find a company by name before pulling its profile.
  • History Flag: history on the company endpoint controls whether prior periods are included.
  • Reference Codes: /v1/codes documents the coded values used across responses.

Use Cases

  • Customer Payment Risk: Check how a prospective customer pays before agreeing terms.
  • Credit Decisions: Price payment risk into credit limits using reported performance.
  • Procurement Benchmarking: Compare a company against the market distribution.
  • Cash Flow Forecasting: Use reported days-to-pay to model expected collection timing.

API Documentation

Base URL

All API requests for the UK Supplier Payment Practices API should be made to the following base URL:

https://prod.api.market/api/v1/193market/uk-supplier-payment-practices

Authentication

Authentication is handled via an API key. You must include your key in the x-api-market-key header of every request:

x-api-market-key: YOUR_API_KEY

Replace YOUR_API_KEY with your actual API key.

FAQ

  • What is the company number? The Companies House registration number, for example 04780736. Find it with /v1/companies/search, then use it for the profile and reports endpoints.

  • How should I read the benchmarks? /v1/benchmarks returns the distribution across all covered companies — median, quartiles and the 10th/90th percentiles — so you can see where one company sits rather than judging its days-to-pay in isolation.

  • Why do some companies have several reports? Large UK companies report payment practices each reporting period. The reports endpoint returns that history, most recent first.

  • Does this cover every UK company? No. Only companies above the statutory reporting thresholds are required to report, which is why coverage is 9,149 companies rather than the full register.
