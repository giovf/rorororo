# Wix App Market Partner Agreement

We recommend that you read through our entire [Partner Agreement](https://dev.wix.com/app-market-partner-agreement) before developing. This will help you to understand the terms and conditions of launching your app in the App Market. During app submission in your app's dashboard, you'll need to acknowledge your agreement to the Partner Agreement.
