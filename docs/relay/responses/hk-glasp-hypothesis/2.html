Export Your Glasp Highlights to Notion, Obsidian, Readwise, Roam Research, Logseq, and More

Last updated on  Feb 21, 2025

Your highlights and notes are valuable—so why keep them locked in one place? With Glasp's Export Feature, you can seamlessly transfer your saved knowledge into your favorite note-taking apps like Notion, Obsidian, Readwise, Roam Research, Logseq, and more.

Why Export Your Highlights?

Exporting your highlights gives you:

  • ✅ Better organization – Integrate your highlights with your existing knowledge base.
  • ✅ Advanced note-taking – Combine highlights with personal insights, ideas, and research.
  • ✅ Cross-platform accessibility – Access your saved content across different apps and devices.

How to Export Highlights from Glasp

Exporting your highlights is easy and can be done in just a few steps:

1. Exporting to Notion

  1. Go to Account Settings > Integrations
  2. Select Notion as your export option.
  3. Connect your Notion account and choose the destination database or page.
  4. Click "Export" in your highlighted content and your highlights and notes will appear in Notion!

2. Exporting to Obsidian

  1. Download the Glasp Obsidian Plugin directly from the plugin page. Or open Obsidian, go to Settings > Community Plugins, and search for Glasp. Install and enable the plugin.
  2. In Glasp, navigate to your Account Settings > Access Token and copy your access token.
  3. Open the plugin settings in Obsidian and paste your Glasp access token.
  4. Click "Import Glasp Highlights" in the Obsidian sidebar. Your highlights will be fetched and added to your Obsidian vault.

3. Exporting to Readwise

  1. Open your Glasp profile page.
  2. Click on "Export" and select Readwise.
  3. Connect your Readwise account.
  4. Click "Sync" and your highlights will be automatically imported.

4. Exporting to Roam Research

  1. Choose Markdown (MD) or CSV as your export format.
  2. Download the file and import it into Roam Research.
  3. Your highlights will be structured for easy linking inside Roam!

5. Exporting to Other Apps

Glasp also allows you to export highlights in CSV, Markdown, or JSON formats. These can be used to import highlights into Logseq, Evernote, Apple Notes, and many other apps that support these formats.

Make the Most of Your Highlights

With Glasp’s export feature, your highlights are not just saved—they become a part of your learning system. Whether you're organizing notes in Notion, linking ideas in Obsidian, or reviewing insights in Readwise, exporting gives you the freedom to use your knowledge however you like.

Try it out today and take your note-taking workflow to the next level! 🚀
