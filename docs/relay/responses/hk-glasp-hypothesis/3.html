▼ Can I import highlights from other platforms like Weava, Hypothesis, Medium, Instapaper, Pocket, and Readwise?

Yes. Glasp can import highlights and notes from Kindle, Snipd, Readwise, Matter, Medium, Instapaper, Hypothesis, Weava, Raindrop.io, Diigo, Wallabag, Airtable, and Pocket, plus any CSV file. You can find all importers under Settings, then Integrations, in the Import tab.

Note: Pocket shut down in July 2025, so the Pocket import works with the export file you downloaded before Pocket's deadline.

▼ Can I export my highlights and notes into other note-taking apps?

Yes! You can export your Glasp highlights and notes to your favorite tools, including Notion, Obsidian, Readwise, Roam Research, Tana, Workflowy, Zotero, and Anki. You can find the integrations under Settings, then Integrations, in the Export tab. For tools without a direct integration, such as Logseq, you can copy and paste your highlights, or download them as TXT, CSV, or Markdown files.

▼ How often do integrations sync?

It depends on the integration.

Notion offers daily auto-sync on paid plans, so new highlights are sent automatically once a day.

Workflowy and Tana have a Daily Sync toggle in their modal, under AUTOMATION, once you have connected them. Turn it on and Glasp exports new highlights once a day without you doing anything.

Obsidian works the other way around. The official Glasp plugin pulls your highlights from Glasp, so there is nothing to run on the Glasp side. Click Import Glasp Highlights in the plugin settings whenever you want the latest ones.

Whatever the integration, you can always run the export yourself with Export All Highlights. Re-runs only send the pages that changed since the last export, so you will not end up with duplicates.

▼ What is AI Clone?

AI Clone is an AI version of you, built from the highlights and notes you've saved on Glasp. Because it learns from what you've actually read and found important, it answers in a way that reflects your interests and thinking. You can chat with your own AI Clone from your profile page by clicking Ask AI Clone, and you can also ask other people's AI Clones on their profile pages to learn from what they've read.

▼ Can I turn off or hide my AI Clone?

Yes. Go to Settings, then AI Clone & Hatch, and change Visibility of Your AI Clone from Public to Private. Your AI Clone then stops appearing to other people, and only you can ask it questions.

Your highlights and learning memories are yours either way. On the same page you can download them with Download Your Learning Memories.

▼ Is there a dark mode for Glasp?

Yes! Glasp supports dark mode. To change the theme, go to Settings, then Preferences, and find the Appearance section. You can choose from three options: System (follows your device setting), Light, or Dark. The theme setting is saved on each device.

▼ Can I change the language of Glasp?

Yes. In the Glasp web app, go to Settings, then Preferences, and you'll find two language settings. Interface Language sets the language of the browser extension and the mobile app, which are available in seven languages: English, Spanish, French, Portuguese (Brazil), Vietnamese, Japanese, and Korean. Summary Language sets the language your AI summaries are written in. You can pick from 51 languages, or leave it on Auto to match the language of the page or video.

Both are saved to your account and synced to your Glasp browser extension and mobile app. The extensions also have their own language setting if you'd rather set it there.

📚 Kindle Highlights

▼ How do I export my Kindle highlights with Glasp?

Install the Glasp browser extension, then go to read.amazon.com and sign in with your Amazon account. Click "Notes & Highlights" in the left sidebar, and the Glasp modal will appear at the top right. From there, you can select books and download highlights as TXT, CSV, or Markdown, or import them to Glasp for further export to note-taking apps.

See the full guide: How to Download Highlights and Notes from Kindle

▼ Can I export Kindle highlights to Notion, Obsidian, and other apps?

Yes. Glasp supports direct export to 11+ note-taking apps including Notion, Obsidian, Evernote, Apple Notes, Google Keep, OneNote, Goodnotes, Notability, Capacities, Joplin, and Zotero. Import your highlights to Glasp from Kindle Cloud Reader, then choose your destination app. For a full list of supported apps and step-by-step tutorials, see: How to Download Highlights and Notes from Kindle

▼ Is there a Kindle export limit with Glasp?

No. Amazon's built-in notebook feature limits how much highlighted text you can copy from certain books due to publisher copyright restrictions. Glasp bypasses this limitation, allowing you to export all your highlights without any restrictions. See how it works: How to Copy and Paste Kindle Highlights Beyond the Export Limits

▼ Can I export Kindle highlights as Markdown?

Yes. Select your books in Kindle Cloud Reader, click "Download" on the Glasp modal, and choose "MD" as the format. Your highlights will be downloaded as a Markdown file, ready to import into Obsidian, Notion, or any other Markdown-based app. For a detailed walkthrough, see: How to Export Kindle Highlights as Markdown Using Glasp

▼ How do I set up Daily Kindle Review?

Sign up for a free Glasp account, then import your Kindle highlights from Kindle Cloud Reader using the "Import to Glasp" button. Once imported, go to your Glasp profile settings and enable Daily Kindle Review. Glasp will send you a daily email with random highlights from your books to help you remember what you've read.

Full setup guide: Daily Kindle Review: How to Turn Your Kindle Highlights Into Lasting Knowledge

▼ Does Glasp work with Amazon stores outside the US?

Yes. Glasp supports amazon.com, amazon.co.jp, amazon.co.uk, and 10+ other countries.

▼ Can I import Kindle highlights into ChatGPT or NotebookLM?

Yes. Export your Kindle highlights as a file using Glasp, then upload it to ChatGPT or NotebookLM. You can ask questions about what you've read, generate summaries, or find connections across multiple books.

See the step-by-step tutorials: How to Import Kindle Highlights into ChatGPT and How to Import Kindle Highlights into NotebookLM

▼ Why are my Kindle highlights not showing in Glasp?

This is usually caused by one of the following:

  1. Not signed in to Kindle Cloud Reader: Go to read.amazon.com and make sure you're logged in with the correct Amazon account.
  2. Wrong country selected: Select the Amazon store that matches the account you purchased the books with (e.g., amazon.com, amazon.co.jp).
  3. Glasp extension not enabled: Check that the Glasp extension is installed and enabled in your browser. If you're in Incognito mode, make sure you've enabled Glasp for Incognito.
  4. Books not purchased from Amazon: Highlights from personal documents (PDFs, mobi files) sent to Kindle are not synced to Kindle Cloud Reader. Only highlights from Amazon-purchased books are available.

If the issue persists, try refreshing the page or reinstalling the Glasp extension.

▼ Can I export highlights from books not purchased on Amazon?

Amazon only syncs highlights from purchased books to Kindle Cloud Reader. For personal documents sent to Kindle, highlights are stored locally on the device in My Clippings.txt.

▼ What's the difference between the Free, Pro, and Unlimited plans?

The Free plan gives you unlimited public highlights, basic YouTube summaries (3 per day), PDF uploads and summaries (30 files), AI chat (20 credits per month), and audio transcription (30 minutes per month).

Pro ($12.50/month billed annually, or $15 monthly) adds unlimited private highlights, advanced YouTube summaries, all AI chat models, daily auto-sync to Notion, and much higher limits: 100 PDF uploads, 500 AI chat credits, and 300 minutes of audio transcription per month.

Unlimited ($30/month billed annually, or $36 monthly) raises everything further, with unlimited PDF uploads, 2,000 AI chat credits, 1,500 minutes of audio transcription per month, and priority support.

▼ Does Glasp offer an API for YouTube Summary?

No. Even though the Glasp's API to get the YouTube Summary is easy and robust way to get the transcript, we're not offering it.
