About this app
arrow_forward
Monthly Expenses helps you to take control of your expenses and income in a very simple and intuitive way. It also helps you to create Budgets for different expense categories and better organize your spending.
You can track your expenses in the form of daily, weekly, monthly or annual reports and export the reports in PDF or Excel format.
You can backup your data in Google Drive for better safety and synchronize the same in any other device.
★ Features ★
---------------
★ Add Expense
- The application helps you to add your expense, income and transfer details from one account to another
★ Budget Planner
- You can add daily, weekly, monthly and annual spending budgets for your expense categories
- The budgets are archived and you can compare your previous and current expenses for the expense categories
★ Custom category
- The application comes with all possible expense and income categories that you would possibly need for your day to day spending
- You can add/edit additional expense categories as per your needs and select graphic symbol for your newly added/edited category from our rich set of symbol archive
★ Bill reminder
- The application lets you add all your bills and the bill dates will be identified by the alarm symbol in expense calendar for your date of spending
- The application will also send notification regarding your pending bills even if you do not open the application
★ Schedule Expense
- The application lets you schedule your re-occurring expenses and incomes so that you do not have to add them manually each time
- Just schedule your expense and income and the spending record will be automatically added
★ Expense Report
- You have the option to view your daily expenses or income report by date or by category wise
- You can view detailed report of your expenses - item wise, daily, monthly, yearly or based on a date range
★ Favorite category
- You can set the expense categories that you commonly use as the Favorite spending category
- This helps you to limit your search space while adding an expense
- You can also separately view reports for your Favorite expense categories
★ Upload Expense report
- You can manage your expense reports by storing them in Email, WhatsApp, Google drive, Dropbox etc.
★ Password protection
- Insecure about exposing your financial details ?
- The application helps you to password protect all your information
- You can set password in the application and nobody else would be able to view your spending details
- You have the password recovery option, in case you forget your password
★ Backup database
- Insecure about data loss ?
- The application takes backup of your spending database each day and stores it in your device
- You can also create Backup of your database in Google drive for better safety.
★ Multiple Accounts
- You can add your accounts and cards and get detail expense report on your spending for each of your accounts in daily, monthly, yearly or item wise
