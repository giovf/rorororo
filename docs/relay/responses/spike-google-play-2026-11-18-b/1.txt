About this app
arrow_forward
Know what you REALLY made on every flip.
Marketplaces show you the sale price — not what's left after their fees, your shipping, and what the item cost you at the thrift store. Reseller Profit Tracker is the offline inventory and profit app for resellers who flip thrift, estate, flea-market, wholesale and online finds. Log an item the moment you source it, follow it from shelf to sold, and see your true net profit and ROI on every single flip.
Log a find in seconds — name, brand, size, what you paid, a photo — then move it through your pipeline: SOURCED, LISTED, SOLD, and back out as RETURNED or KEPT. When it sells, record the sale price, the platform fee and your shipping, and the app does the math no marketplace app does for you: sold − cost − fees − shipping + shipping charged − other = your real net profit, plus ROI %.
WHY RESELLERS USE IT
• Real net profit per flip — after fees, shipping and cost, calculated for you
• ROI on every item — see which sourcing actually pays
• 14 marketplace fee presets — eBay, Vinted, Depop, Poshmark, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace, Etsy, Grailed, Whatnot, Carousell, Shpock, Gumtree, OLX and Local/Cash — all editable, add your own
• SOURCED → LISTED → SOLD pipeline — plus RETURNED and KEPT handling that reverses returns correctly
• Multi-platform listings — cross-list one item and track where it actually sold
• Death-pile & aging tracker — surface items sitting 30, 60, 90+ days and the cash tied up in them
• Sales timeline — a clean date-by-date history of everything you've sold
• Sourcing-trip P&L — log a thrift run's finds and expenses, see if the whole trip made money
• Monthly dashboard — net profit, revenue, items sold and inventory-at-cost at a glance
• CSV & PDF export — tidy profit records for your own files or your accountant
• Photos — snap with the camera or pick from your gallery for every item
FREE FEE CALCULATOR — FOREVER Not sure a flip is worth it? Use the built-in marketplace fee calculator, unlimited and free forever. Pick a platform, punch in a sold price and your cost, and instantly see the fee, your net and your ROI before you buy or list — no item, no sign-up needed.
PRIVATE BY DESIGN Everything stays on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no cloud sync — your inventory, costs and profit are yours alone, stored locally on your device. Uninstalling removes all your data.
FREE, WITH OPTIONAL PREMIUM Every feature, sourcing-trip P&L included, works on the free plan. Track up to 30 active items for free (sold and kept items never count against the limit), with bonus slots available. Premium, as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime unlock, removes that cap, removes ads, and exports reports without watching an ad.
IMPORTANT Reseller Profit Tracker is a personal record-keeping tool. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to eBay, Vinted, Depop, Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy or any other marketplace — all names belong to their owners. It does not provide tax, accounting, or financial advice; consult a qualified professional for filing requirements in your country.
