About this app
arrow_forward
Never miss a business opportunity—anywhere in Nepal or even from abroad!
Tendernotice is Nepal’s premier online Bid and Tender Portal, designed to simplify how businesses discover and manage procurement opportunities. We aggregate tender notices published in all major national and local newspapers across Nepal, bringing them together in one easy-to-use digital platform.
Whether you are in Nepal or outside the country, Tendernotice lets contractors, suppliers, and consultants view and manage tender notices anytime, from anywhere—directly from their phone or computer.
Key Features
•	Nationwide Coverage: Access tender notices from all national and local newspapers across Nepal
•	Access from Anywhere: View notices from anywhere in Nepal or abroad, without physical newspaper access
•	Daily Updates: Real-time collection of the latest tender notices
•	Instant Notifications: Email and in-app alerts for tenders relevant to your business
•	Document Access: View and download tender documents directly through the app
•	Online Management: Organize, track, and manage bids and tenders fully online
•	Advanced Search & Date Filters: Filter by category, organization, location, deadline — and view back-dated (previous) notices
Why Tendernotice?
We eliminate location and time barriers in tender discovery. By digitizing newspaper-based notices from all over Nepal, Tendernotice ensures you can track opportunities even if you’re outside the country or checking notices late.
From PPMO / e-GP updates to local government, municipal, and private-sector auctions, we cover it all—nationwide.
Join thousands of professionals who trust Tendernotice to grow their business.
Register today and manage tenders from anywhere.
