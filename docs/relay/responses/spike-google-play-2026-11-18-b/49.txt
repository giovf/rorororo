About this app
arrow_forward
HygiMap shows official food hygiene inspection results for restaurants, cafés, takeaways and food shops near you on a map – in Finland, Denmark, Norway, the UK, France, Canada (Toronto, Ottawa and Surrey / Fraser Health BC) and the United States (NYC, NY State, Suffolk NY, Los Angeles, San Francisco, Oakland / Berkeley / Alameda, Marin, Silicon Valley / Santa Clara, Sacramento, Seattle & King, Kitsap & Island WA, Las Vegas, Chicago, Austin, Arlington, Irving, Pearland, Waco-McLennan & Williamson / Round Rock TX, Boston & Cambridge MA, Detroit, Minneapolis, Columbus, Portage & Cincinnati OH, Pittsburgh / Allegheny PA, Delaware, Fairfax VA, Montgomery & Anne Arundel MD, Raleigh / Wake NC, Louisville KY, Baton Rouge LA, Omaha / Douglas NE, Montgomery AL, Bloomington / Monroe IN, Park City / Summit UT, Dover NH and Boulder CO).
Each country or region is shown on its own official scale, as the authority publishes it. Nothing is converted into a score of our own:
• Finland: Oiva (Excellent, Good, To be corrected, Poor)
• Denmark: Fødevarestyrelsen smiley
• Norway: Mattilsynet smilefjes
• UK: Food Hygiene Rating Scheme (0–5); Scotland: Food Hygiene Information Scheme
• France: Alim'confiance
• NYC, NY State retail stores, LA County, Las Vegas, Wake County, Louisville, Irving, Pearland & Dover: letter grades (A, B, C)
• Toronto, Alameda, Marin, Santa Clara, Sacramento & Columbus: Green / Yellow / Red or Pass / Conditional Pass / Closed placard
• San Francisco, Chicago, Boston, Cambridge, NY State restaurants, Suffolk, Arlington, Waco-McLennan, Williamson, Detroit, Portage, Pittsburgh, Delaware, Fairfax, Montgomery & Anne Arundel (MD), Cincinnati, Baton Rouge, Bloomington & Boulder: Pass / In Compliance, Conditional Pass / Violations, Fail / Closed
• Ottawa: In Compliance / Enforcement Action / Closed
• Surrey / Fraser Health & Omaha / Douglas County: Hazard or Superior / Excellent / Standard / Fair rating
• Seattle & King, Kitsap & Island Counties (WA): 4-tier Food Safety Rating
• Austin, Montgomery (AL) & Park City / Summit: numeric inspection scores (0–100) or critical-violation counts
FEATURES
• Map and list of nearby places with their ratings (sort by distance, rating or inspection date) and area rating distribution
• Direct link to the establishment's official inspection report in every supported region, sub-scores, violation breakdowns, earlier results and inspection trend
• Opening hours, closing-soon alert, next opening time, lunch hours, and wheelchair access, outdoor seating, lunch and recently-inspected filters, and places closed now can be hidden
• Filter by type of place and by rating, and share any place or filtered view as a link
• Favourites (sort by distance, rating or recently added, search and personal notes) with new-inspection alerts that can be moved between devices as a file or a link
• Search restaurants by name in every supported region, find cities, districts and streets, and jump directly to any supported region
• In English, Finnish and Swedish
PRICE
The seven-day trial is free and does not turn into a paid plan by itself. After it, the app requires the one-time purchase HygiMap Pro. No subscription, no ads.
PRIVACY
No sign-in, no ads, no tracking. Your location is sent to the server only to an accuracy of about one hundred metres, and your favourites stay on your device.
HygiMap is an independent app, not an official service of any authority. Ratings: Finnish Food Authority (Oiva); Fødevarestyrelsen; Mattilsynet (CC BY 4.0); Food Standards Agency and Food Standards Scotland (OGL v3.0); Alim'confiance (Licence Ouverte); Canadian and US public health authorities (sources credited in the app). Places and address locations: © OpenStreetMap contributors.
