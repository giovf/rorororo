About this app
arrow_forward
📦 BusinessX – Simple POS, Stock & Profit Manager That Works Offline
Still tracking your shop in a notebook? BusinessX is a simple POS (point of sale) and stock management app for small business owners. Record counter sales, track inventory and see your profit instantly. It works fully offline, and everything syncs across every phone in your shop the moment you're back online.
BusinessX is all about your numbers: what you sold today, what profit you made this month, and what needs restocking. No extra hardware, no setup, no paperwork.
🧾 ONE APP FOR YOUR WHOLE SHOP
Use BusinessX as your POS register, inventory and sales tracker, daily sales record, stock register, profit and loss tracker and inventory management app — on one phone or across your whole team.
🆕 WHAT'S NEW
- Instant multi-device sync – every phone stays updated in real time
- Instant cloud backup – your data is saved as you work
- Owner & staff accounts with limited access
- Manage multiple shops from one account
- Easy restocking
- Paid & unpaid sales tracking
- Export to Excel, CSV or PDF
- Sales trends at a glance
🛒 SIMPLE POS FOR COUNTER SALES
Record a sale in one tap with item auto-detect. Sell multiple items as a bundle and the discount and per-item price are calculated for you. Mark every sale as paid or unpaid and keep track of pending payments. Record returns in seconds. Every sale updates your stock automatically.
👥 OWNER & STAFF ACCESS
Add staff to your shop with their own accounts. You decide what they see: let them record sales while cost prices and profits stay hidden. You keep full control; your team gets only what it needs.
📶 WORKS OFFLINE, SYNCS INSTANTLY
BusinessX works fully offline, so a weak signal never stops a sale. Once you're online, a sale recorded on your staff's phone shows up on yours instantly, and every entry is backed up to the cloud automatically. A lost or new phone never means lost records.
🏬 MULTIPLE SHOPS, ONE APP
Run more than one store? Switch between shops in one tap. Each shop keeps its own inventory, sales and reports.
📦 STOCK MANAGEMENT & INVENTORY
Add items with photos, auto-assigned IDs, cost price, selling price, tax % and quantity. Track stock size-wise for shoes and clothing. Filter by in-stock, low stock or out-of-stock, and restock in a few taps when new supplies arrive.
💰 EXPENSE TRACKING
Log rent, electricity, salaries, restocking costs and other expenses so your profit and loss numbers are always real.
📊 REPORTS, TRENDS & EXPORT
- Daily and monthly sales, profit and trends
- Profit margin % and average monthly sales
- Month-over-month comparison, as far back as you like
- Export sales and inventory to Excel, CSV or PDF
✅ KEY FEATURES AT A GLANCE
✓ Simple POS with one-tap sales & item auto-detect
✓ Works fully offline
✓ Inventory with photos, IDs, prices & tax
✓ Size-wise stock for shoes & clothing
✓ Bundle sales with auto discount

✓ Owner & staff roles with limited access
✓ Multiple shops in one app
✓ Instant multi-device sync
✓ Instant cloud backup
✓ Expense tracker
✓ Daily, monthly & average profit reports
✓ Sales tracker with trends & charts
✓ Export to Excel, CSV & PDF
🏪 PERFECT FOR
Grocery & kirana stores · Shoe shops · Clothing stores & boutiques · Electronics & mobile shops · Stationery shops · Pharmacies · Hardware stores · Cosmetics shops · Any small retail business
💡 WHY BUSINESSX?
Many POS and accounting apps are built for accountants. BusinessX is built for shop owners who just want their numbers — stock, sales and profit — on the phone they already have, even offline. Fast, visual and easy for anyone at your counter. No accounting knowledge needed.
Free to start. Some advanced features require BusinessX Premium.
