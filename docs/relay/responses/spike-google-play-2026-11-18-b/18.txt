About this app
arrow_forward
Track your miles automatically completely free with TripLog! Whether you’re a gig worker, freelancer, employee, or a manager of a team or company of any size, TripLog saves you time, helps you keep the records needed to claim tax relief or allowable business expenses, and makes employee mileage expense claim easier.
Unlike other mileage trackers, TripLog gives individual users truly unlimited automatic trip detection at no cost. Once you’re set up, our app runs seamlessly in the background and captures every drive for you. Just drive and let TripLog do the work!
► Start Tracking Automatically For Free
• Free unlimited automatic tracking - TripLog starts tracking when you start driving and stops when you stop
• Smart trip classification - Swipe to classify drives as business or private, or set up auto-classification hours to automatically categorise trips depending on when you drive
• Expense tracking included - Track income, fuel, parking, tolls, and other allowable/reimbursable business expenses
• Annual reporting access - All TripLog users get one free 7-day Premium Pass each year to create and download annual mileage reports
• Perfect for keeping business mileage records for tax purposes
► Power Up With Premium
• Unlimited reporting - Create detailed, HMRC tax-compliant mileage and expense reports in PDF or CSV formats
• Web dashboard access - Review and manage your mileage, expenses, and reports from your computer
• Enhanced expense tracking - Enjoy OCR receipt capture, auto detect receipt date, supplier, amount, and more
• Additional trip auto-classification options - Set custom work schedules, frequent trip rules, and other automatic classifications
• More ways to stay organised - Create custom activities and mileage rates, in addition to Approved Mileage Allowance Payments (AMAP) rates, plan routes, and more
• Software integrations - Connect TripLog with QuickBooks Online, SAP Concur, and other tools
• Great for individual employee mileage allowance claims
► Enterprise & Business Solutions
Have employees who drive for work? TripLog makes it easy to manage mileage, expenses, time, and claims all in one place.
• Manage all of your employees’ mileage and expenses from one centralised dashboard
• Built-in HMRC Approved Mileage Allowance Payments (AMAP) rates, i.e. 55p per mile for the first 10,000 business miles, then 25p per mile over 10,000 business miles
• Create custom mileage rates, commute deductions, and reimbursement policies
• Quarterly updated HMRC Advisory Fuel Rate (AFR) and its VAT calculation
• Set up approval hierarchies to review and approve employee mileage and expense reports
• Keep employees’ private trip details private
• Integrate with ADP, SAP Concur, UKG, QuickBooks, and more
Whether you’re tracking business mileage for tax purposes, submitting mileage claim, or managing an entire team of drivers, TripLog keeps your records accurate and makes mileage tracking a breeze.
Note: TripLog uses a foreground service to collect location data for real-time, automatic mileage tracking, which is essential for accurate record-keeping. This ensures that trips are accurately recorded even when the app is running in the background or the screen is off, as any interruption would result in missing mileage data. The foreground service runs with a persistent notification to keep you informed while TripLog tracks your mileage.
For those using our optional TripLog Drive™ device, TripLog uses a foreground service to enable continuous data transfer between the device and your phone. This ensures that your mileage data is accurately synced even when your phone is locked or the app is in the background, as any interruption would result in missing trip data. The foreground service runs with a persistent notification to keep you informed while TripLog syncs with your device.
