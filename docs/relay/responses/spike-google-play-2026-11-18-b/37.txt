arrow_forward
Search over 2 million UK companies registered with Companies House.
-Offline information accessibility.
- No need to register
- Access details such as company address, status, account date and etc.
- Access financial information including Balance Sheet, Rate & Kpi, Capital & Reserve, Profit & Loss
- Access details of company officers and filing history
- Store your most frequently viewed companies in your favorites
