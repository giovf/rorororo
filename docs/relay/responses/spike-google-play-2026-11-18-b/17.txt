MileageWise is the easiest way to track business miles, log trips, and uncover £1,000s in potential business mileage allowances, without draining your phone or your time.
Whether you need a business mileage tracker app or just want a smooth way to separate your business and personal travel, this app does it all. Battery-friendly and designed for everyday drivers.

How Distance Is Calculated: Choose Your Method
↔️Server-Based Distance Calculation
How it works: The app logs your arrivals at each stop, and our server calculates the optimal door-to-door distance in the background.
Why it’s great:
- Private by design (no route line)
- You don’t need to worry about the app “waking up”, and it's lighter on battery and data
- Distances match what HMRC can verify in Google Maps
🛣️Route Tracking Distance Calculation
How it works: Records your actual route line and mid-trip waypoints in real time.
Why it’s great:
- See exactly where you drove
- If the starting point isn’t recorded because of coverage or device conditions, the server fills the gap for a complete log
- Backup Mileage Capture adds a safety net
Manual Mode
How it works: One tap to log each arrival, quick, easy, full control.
Why it’s great:
- Maximum privacy
- You choose what to log and when
- Our most battery- and data-friendly option
More Than Just a Mileage Tracker
• Automatically classify trips as business or personal
• Track miles across multiple cars or devices

• Save client locations and companies by name or POI

• Track vehicle-related expenses
• Secure cloud data storage
• Create a mileage logbook with one tap
• Export reports to PDF, Excel, or CSV
