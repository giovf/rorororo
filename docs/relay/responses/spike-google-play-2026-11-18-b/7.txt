About this app
arrow_forward
📊 Profit Tracker – Trading Journal, Trade Log & Performance Analytics
Profit Tracker is the all-in-one trading journal app built for stock traders, forex traders, and crypto traders who want to track performance, hit their goals, and trade with real data — not guesswork.
Whether you're a beginner trader building discipline or an advanced day trader refining your edge, this trading journal, trade tracker, and performance analytics app gives you everything you need in one place.
✨ TRADE JOURNAL & TRADE LOG
Log every trade fast and keep a complete trading diary. Review your trading history as a list or a line chart, and turn your trade log into a tool for real trading discipline and trading psychology.
🎯 TRADING GOALS & TARGETS
Set daily, weekly, monthly, half-year, and yearly trading targets. Track your progress with clear goal charts and know exactly how close you are to hitting your next trading milestone.
📈 PERFORMANCE COMPARE & TRADE ANALYTICS
Compare your trading performance across any time period — today vs. yesterday, week vs. week, month vs. month, half-year vs. half-year, year vs. year. Visualize trends with dual-line performance charts and compare win rate, total trades, wins, and losses at a glance.
🕓 GOAL HISTORY TRACKER
Keep a full history of every trading goal you've set, across every timeframe, so you can see your growth as a trader over time.
🏠 TRADING DASHBOARD
Your home dashboard shows your current balance, target progress, monthly profit and loss, win rate, and recent trades — a complete trading performance summary in one screen.
⚙️ PERSONALIZED TRADING PROFILE
Set up your deposit currency, initial balance, and monthly target. Choose your trading style — investor, swing trader, or day trader — your experience level, and your risk tolerance, so the app adapts to how you actually trade.
🌗 BUILT FOR SERIOUS TRADERS
- Light mode and dark mode
- 14 languages — full app translation, not just menus
- Backup and restore your trading data anytime
- Export your trading journal to CSV, Excel, or JSON
📌 PERFECT FOR
Stock trading • Forex trading • Crypto trading • Day trading • Swing trading • Investing • Trading journal for beginners • Trade tracking for advanced traders • Trading performance analysis • Trading goal setting • Win rate tracking • Profit and loss tracking
Profit Tracker is more than a trading journal — it's a trading performance tracker, trade analytics tool, and trading goal planner built to help you trade smarter, track consistently, and grow as a trader.
⚠️ Disclaimer: Profit Tracker is a personal trading journal and analytics tool. It does not provide financial advice, investment recommendations, or execute trades.
Download Profit Tracker now and start building a real trading journal habit.
