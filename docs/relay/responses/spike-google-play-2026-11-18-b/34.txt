About this app
arrow_forward
Search every company on the UK register, look up the directors behind them, and keep track of what each one has to file and when.
UK Company Search puts the Companies House public register in your pocket. Look up any of the five million companies registered in the UK, check who runs them, see what they have filed, and get their confirmation statement and annual accounts dates.
SEARCH THE UK COMPANY REGISTER
Find any UK company by name or by company number. Search company directors and secretaries by name at the same time — one box searches both, and the building and person icons narrow it to one or the other. Results show each company's status as you scan: active, dissolved, in liquidation, in administration.
CHECK A COMPANY
Open any company for its full public record. Registered office address, incorporation date, company type, jurisdiction, SIC codes, and the flags that matter — registered charges, insolvency history, whether it can still file. See its officers, current and resigned, with their roles and appointment dates. See its recent filing history, from incorporation onwards.
CHECK A DIRECTOR
Search company directors and secretaries by name and see every company they are attached to, current and resigned, with the status of each. It is the quickest way to spot that a director you are about to deal with has companies in liquidation, or a trail of dissolved ones behind them.
TRACK FILING DEADLINES
Save any company and the app keeps its dates current. Confirmation statement and annual accounts deadlines are shown in plain English — due in 19 days, overdue by 4 days — with anything overdue flagged in red and anything due within thirty days in amber. A dissolved company shows nothing outstanding, because it has nothing left to file. Pull down to re-check the register whenever you want.
Save directors too. Officers file nothing themselves, so what you get is every company attached to them and the state each of those companies is in.
NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
There is nothing to sign up for. Search freely, save whatever you like, and your saved list stays on your phone. No email address, no password, no data collected.
WHO USES IT
Company directors watching their own filing dates. Accountants and bookkeepers checking client deadlines. Anyone checking a supplier, landlord or customer before signing something.
ABOUT THE DATA
Company information in this app comes from the Companies House public register and contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Data is live — it is read from the register when you search, not from a copy made months ago.
We are not Companies House. This app is not endorsed by, affiliated with or connected to Companies House or any government department. Companies House makes every final decision about company records and company names.
