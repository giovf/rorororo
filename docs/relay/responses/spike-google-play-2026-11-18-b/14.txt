About this app
arrow_forward
Stop guessing your reselling margins! The eBay & Poshmark Fee Calculator helps online sellers instantly calculate exact net profit, marketplace fees, and return on investment (ROI) before sourcing items.
Whether you are a thrift flipper, shoe reseller, or clothing seller, this simple reseller profit calculator takes the guesswork out of your business. Switch between marketplaces instantly to see where you will make the most money.
Supported Marketplaces:
• eBay Calculator: Accurately backs out standard category final value fees, fixed transaction costs, and store level discounts.
• Poshmark Calculator: Automatically applies the flat $2.95 fee for under $15 or the 20% commission for higher sales.
Core Features:
• Instant Profit Margin: Enter your item cost and target sale price to see your exact take-home payout.
• Shipping Fee Adjustments: Factor in buyer-paid or seller-paid shipping costs easily.
• 100% Offline: No account or internet required—calculate your profits inside thrift stores or garage sales where cellular service is low.
Stop losing money to hidden marketplace fees. Download the ultimate reseller calculator today and flip smarter!
