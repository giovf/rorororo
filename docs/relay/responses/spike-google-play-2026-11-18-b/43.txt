About this app
arrow_forward
Bidding Source Android app is the easiest and smart way for searching the world latest tenders, procurement opportunities, contracts and projects information.
**Source of Information**
All information available on BS application is gathered by BS information gathering agents in different locations of the world from online and offline sources such as countries local newspapers, online news sources. The gathered information is as a raw information in different formats such as hard paper, RSS, html,... and after processing the information by BS staffs it is categorized on the base of Business activities (Business categories) and published on BS application for users.
**Disclaimer of Liability**
Bidding Source does not represent any Governmental Entities or any Buyer and also is not a buyer or tender holder.
The tenders’ information contained in this application is for general information purposes only. You should not rely upon the information on this application as a basis for making any business or any other decisions. Any reliance you place on such information is therefore strictly at your own risk and Bidding Source as a tender information publisher has not any responsibilities about them. Bidding Source tries to publish up to date and accurate tenders’ information but does not guarantee that all of the published information is error-free. For any other information or for any questions regarding participating in any tender ... you should contact directly with the buyer.
Bidding Source will help you to access to all tenders information of your business in your mobile phone or in your personal computer.
By installing Bidding Source Android application you can receive below services:
- Accessing to world tenders and contract awards information.
- Creating unlimited personal search Criteria (Filters) for your account on the base of Categories, Countries and Regions.
- Accessing to your business tenders easily by using your previously created filters.
- Receiving daily notifications as soon as any newly published tender matched with your business.
- Favoriting your interested tenders to the list of Favorite Tenders for easy access in future.
- Sharing tenders in all Social Media and Messaging apps easily.
And other features that you can use after installing the application.
Bidding Source team goals are:
• Enhancing your Business
• Increasing your Business Profits
• Increasing your Market Share
We always respect to our Clients’ business and we are aware that only by building your success, we will succeed. By registering on Bidding Source Android application, receive your business related tenders daily notifications.
