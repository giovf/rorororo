About this app
arrow_forward
UK Companies House Search is a focused read-only utility for reviewing basic UK company registration information. Search by company name or registration number when a live public registry relay is configured. If the live relay is unavailable, the app remains usable with an explicitly labeled offline reference mode so users can test and organize local workflows without mistaking sample data for official records. Save references locally, revisit history, and manage cache and About information. No account is required.
This is an independent third-party utility, not an official Companies House or UK government application. No government affiliation or endorsement is claimed. Its app icon and promotional artwork do not use government seals or the official Companies House identity.
Check UK company records and keep recent references on your device.
