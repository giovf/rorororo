About this app
arrow_forward
Search UK companies, check directors and explore official Companies House records in one place. Find company registration details, ownership information, financial accounts and filing history using public data from the UK company register.
Whether you're checking a company before signing a contract, researching a potential employer or reviewing a new supplier, this UK company checker helps you quickly access the business information you need.
COMPANY SEARCH
- Search UK companies by name or registration number
- Check company status, including active, dissolved, liquidation and administration
- View registered office addresses, incorporation dates, company types and SIC codes
- Access recent searches and previously viewed companies
- Verify publicly registered company information
DIRECTORS AND OFFICERS
- Find current and resigned company directors and secretaries
- Search directors and view their past and present company appointments
- Check disqualified director records
- View appointment dates and other publicly available officer details
- Explore companies associated with individual directors
COMPANY OWNERSHIP
- View persons with significant control (PSC)
- Check publicly reported beneficial ownership information
- Review share ownership and voting rights
- See how company control is recorded in official filings
COMPANY ACCOUNTS AND FILING HISTORY
- Access annual accounts and financial statements
- View confirmation statements and company filing history
- Check accounts and confirmation statement deadlines
- Identify overdue company filings
- Open, download and share available company documents as PDF
CHARGES AND INSOLVENCY
- Check outstanding, partially satisfied and satisfied company charges
- View registered charge holders
- Review insolvency and liquidation records
- Examine company status changes and relevant filings
COMPANY MONITORING
- Save companies to a watchlist
- Receive notifications about new filings and director changes
- Track company status updates and registered charges
- Monitor suppliers, clients and competitors
COMPANY REPORTS
- Generate PDF reports with company information
- Include directors, ownership, charges and filing history
- Choose which sections to include
- View when each record was retrieved
- Save or share company reports
This app is useful for everyday UK company checks, business verification, supplier research, client checks and company due diligence. It also provides convenient access to public company records for accountants, solicitors, investors, freelancers, journalists and anyone researching UK businesses.
No account or registration is required. Saved companies, lists and search history are stored on your device.
Access UK company information on your Android phone or tablet, including company registration details, director appointments, financial filings, ownership records and company status.
DATA SOURCE
This app uses publicly available data from the official Companies House API under the Open Government Licence.
This is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or officially connected to Companies House or the UK Government. Company registration does not guarantee that a business is trustworthy or financially stable.
