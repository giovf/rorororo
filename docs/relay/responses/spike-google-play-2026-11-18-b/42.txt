About this app
arrow_forward
Trademark Class Finder is a useful app which allows you to find the class of any product, goods or service from the NICE Classification (International Classification of Goods and Services) managed by WIPO.
This app is very useful for trademark attorneys, lawyers and agents who constantly need to check the NICE Classification. The best thing about this app is, it also runs offline.
This app is very easy to use. Just enter a few letters or words of any product or service you want to find and it will list all the classes having the specified product or service, including the goods or service description. The search keyword will be highlighted in the search results, allowing you to navigate through the results quickly.
