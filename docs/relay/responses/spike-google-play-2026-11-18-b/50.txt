About this app
arrow_forward
The FREE all-in-one Self Assessment app.
From getting a tax-rebate to submitting your self assessment and making your whole financial future a healthy-one – downloading the app and subscribing to Pie gives you this little lot:
- Real time bill and tax projections: See what you owe or is owed to you, live at any time
- Bookkeeping: With our revolutionary 'Smart Match' feature reconciling your business expenses has never been easier! You can also upload receipts to ensure your tax return is 100% HMRC safe
- Expert advice from humans: Our Tax Assistants are here to help every step of the way, at a time convenient to you. Like an accountant in your pocket
- Direct submission to HMRC: Every detail of your tax return is taken care of, allowing you to submit directly to HMRC
- Invoicing: One place to create, send and track invoices to your clients
- Multiple incomes in one place: Import your income and outgoings across multiple income streams (wages, side hustle, rental income, capital gains, overseas income, dividends & interest, or anything else - Pie has it covered)
It’s your money, keep it.
