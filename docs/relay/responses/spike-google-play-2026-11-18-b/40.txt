arrow_forward
Instantly identify & Analyze trademarks, copyrights, and more, across multiple countries—all from one app!
Unleash innovation without fear of legal hurdles. Our AI-powered app is your ultimate companion for checking trademarks, copyrights, and ensuring compliance with intellectual property rights. Whether it's text, logos, or images, we’ve got you covered!
✅ Trademark Search: Instantly check trademarks across multiple countries.
✅ Copyright Analysis: Analyze text or images for existing copyright claims.
✅ Risk Assessment: Receive detailed reports on potential legal risks.
✅ Fast & Accurate: AI-driven technology for results in seconds.
✅ Global Coverage: Stay compliant with international laws.
Key Features -
Trademark Checker -
Instantly verify if your business name, logo, or slogan is trademarked.
Comprehensive search across global trademark databases.
Save time and avoid potential conflicts by checking trademarks effortlessly.
Copyright Search - Analyze images, text, and designs for existing copyright claims. Ensure your creative content is original and free from infringement.
Stay compliant with copyright laws across multiple regions.

Legal Risk Assessment - Identify and mitigate risks associated with your intellectual property. AI-driven tools provide comprehensive risk evaluation.
