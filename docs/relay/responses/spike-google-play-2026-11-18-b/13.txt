About this app
arrow_forward
Know what an eBay sale may leave in your pocket before you list. FeeTally estimates current US selling fees, payout, seller costs, net profit, and margin in one clear breakdown.
CALCULATE PROFIT
Enter your expected sale price, item cost, shipping charged, and your shipping cost. FeeTally shows the estimated final value fee, per-order fee, total fees, payout, profit, and margin.
FIND YOUR MINIMUM PRICE
Set the profit you want and FeeTally works backward to find the minimum sale price that reaches it. The result is verified to the nearest cent.
HANDLE REAL SELLING COSTS
Choose your seller plan and selling category. Add sales tax, handling, packaging, other costs, promoted listing rate, international sale, and seller performance adjustments when they apply.
SEE THE FEE LOGIC
Every result includes the applied rate tiers, active US fee schedule, assumptions, and a link to the official fee source.
SAVE RESULTS PRIVATELY
Recent calculations and custom names stay on your device so you can review, rename, reuse, or delete them. No eBay account connection is required.
FREE CORE TOOLS
Profit calculations, target-price calculations, and local history are unlimited. The free app may show a small banner. One optional lifetime purchase removes ads; there is no subscription.
FeeTally provides independent estimates for informational use. Actual fees may differ based on listing details, account status, promotions, taxes, policy changes, or unsupported services. Verify important decisions with the official fee source and your seller account. FeeTally is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by eBay. eBay is a trademark of its respective owner.
