About this app
arrow_forward
Track miles automatically and keep a mileage log book without manual work.
Motolog is an automatic mileage tracker for work, business mileage, mileage claims, reimbursement, and everyday vehicle records. It records journeys automatically, helps you separate business and personal drives, and keeps clear mileage reports ready when you need them. Use it as a HMRC mileage tracker for work, a business mileage tracker, or a practical way to keep a searchable mileage logbook that grows with every drive.
Automatic mileage tracking
- Detect trips in the background and save each drive automatically
- Review, edit, tag, or classify journeys later when you need to
- Use time- and location-based rules to organise recurring routes
- Keep a searchable trip history with distance, time, route details, notes, and categories
- Build a mileage log or mileage logbook without paper notes or spreadsheets
Work records and business mileage
- Keep business mileage separate from personal driving
- Record business journeys without adding manual admin to every day
- Create mileage reports for mileage claims, reimbursement, work records, or tax records
- Export records in PDF, XLS, or CSV
- Import existing history from other apps so your records stay together
Fuel, expenses, and vehicle records
- Record fuel purchases and vehicle expenses alongside your trips
- Keep service history and reminders for each vehicle
- Manage multiple vehicles, including cars and motorcycles
- Share selected vehicles with family or colleagues with configurable access levels
Practical every day
Motolog keeps mileage, reports, fuel, and vehicle history in one organised record. It is built for drivers who want accurate records without overcomplicated workflows, whether they drive for work every day or only need mileage reports from time to time. Searchable history, vehicle-level records, and shared access help you find the right details later without rebuilding a claim or report by hand. You stay in control of your journeys, categories, exports, and shared access while the app reduces the manual work of keeping reliable records for tax relief.
Clear records over time
Motolog is useful whether you drive the same commute, visit clients, or manage several vehicles. Your history stays organised by vehicle and category, so records remain easy to search and ready to share.
Disclosure:
If you enable trip tracking, Motolog collects location in the background and monitors activity in the background to detect and record trips. This is used only for trip detection and trip tracking features.
