About this app
arrow_forward
Brought to you by the #1 award-winning app.
Bookipi Expense is a FREE budgeting app with an easy-to-use expense tracker. Plan and record expenses and scan receipts on the go. Use beautiful charts to review your cash flow and plan your budget weekly or monthly.
Automatically track expenses by connecting your bank feeds to your in-app wallets. Your expense transaction line items automatically flow through to the expense tracking app with secure and real-time bank feeds.
Unlike other budgeting and money management apps, Bookipi Expense helps you separate personal finances and business expenses with UNLIMITED WALLETS for free. You can track your business, travel, and personal expenses in one budgeting platform.
Trusted by over 500,000+ small business owners and freelancers in 179 different countries , Bookipi is now delivering the FULL experience. Sync your budgeting data to our invoice maker and receive payments on the same platform.
Track all of your income & expenses with a versatile budget planner app. Monitor your spending and SAVE MONEY TODAY.
KEY FEATURES:
FREE Unlimited Wallets
Track your expenses and earnings for personal, professional, or even per-project purposes. By using multiple wallets for multiple occasions, you’re guaranteed never to lose track of your spending.
Sync Wallet Balance to Bank Feed
Sync your bank to your wallets in real time! Add multiple bank feeds and ditch summarising expenses across your bank accounts. Save time and manage all of your expenses in one app.
Budget Planning
Easily create monthly or weekly budgets for better money management. Receive notifications for when you are nearing your limit.
Spending Limits
Set unique spending limits on each of your unlimited wallets. We'll notify you when your spending is close to your budget limit based on your inputs and real-time bank feeds.
Unique Categorisation
Log and categorise your expense and income entries to make your finances easy to review and organise. Complete with unique icon assigning.
Charts & Expense Breakdowns
Each wallet has a unique daily report of monthly income & expenses. Visualise your spending patterns and plan your finances ahead of time with our charts and expense breakdowns. By analysing your monthly financial activity, find out how you can save more money.
Receipt Storage
Store and manage receipts for easy retrieval. Just take a picture to store it in our secure databases for your easy reference.
Review Transactions
Our money management app helps with record-keeping so you’re more confident in money matters. Search transactions by date and name, and find attached photo receipts.
Data Exporting
Generate instant summaries of your wallets with a tap through our CSV exporting feature.
Automatic Bookipi Invoicing Data Synchronising
Bookipi Invoice users are rewarded with automatic data syncing. Allowing you to keep track of all of your income and expenses in one seamless process.
OTHER OUTSTANDING FEATURES:
- Expense Record Keeping
- Change Currency
- Transfer Expenses Between Wallets
- Transaction Notes
- In-App Chat Support
- Global Currency Selections
- Intuitive UI Design
- Frequently Updated Video Tutorials
Bookipi Expense is the easiest free app to track personal & business expenses in one app. Manage your expenses and plan your spending on the go. Track how much money goes in and out of your accounts and have full control over your finances today!
