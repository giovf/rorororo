About this app
arrow_forward
Know what you really made on every flip.
Resale Ledger is a simple profit tracker for resellers. Log what you buy, record what sells, and the app works out your real profit after marketplace fees, shipping, sales tax and what you paid for the item. No spreadsheets, no account, no subscription.
FEES FILLED IN FOR YOU
Pick your marketplaces once and the seller fee fills in on every sale. Presets cover eBay, Amazon, Vinted, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Etsy, Whatnot and Facebook Marketplace, including per-order fees, minimum fees and flat fees on small sales. Adjust any rule or add your own marketplace.
INVENTORY THAT KNOWS ITS COST
• Add items with cost, quantity, source, SKU and storage location
• Record a sale from the item and its cost carries over
• Buy in bulk? Track "2 of 3 left" and sell several units at once
• See how long each item has been sitting, and sort oldest first
• Know how much money is tied up in unsold stock
SEE WHAT'S WORKING
• Profit for the month and year, with a chart against last year
• Profit, average per sale and ROI by marketplace
• Which thrift stores, estate sales and garage sales pay off, and how fast their finds sell
• Your best flips at a glance
HAULS AND SOURCING TRIPS
• Log each sourcing trip: the stores you hit, what you spent and the miles you drove
• Add what you bought to the haul and split a lot's cost across the items
• See each year's hauls on the Summary screen: spent, miles, time and what they brought back
• Premium: see which hauls, stores and days pay best, after mileage
READY FOR TAX TIME
• Log expenses like shipping supplies, storage and subscriptions by category
• Cost of goods counts when an item sells, the way Schedule C reports it
• Print or save a year report as a PDF: profit and loss, month by month, marketplaces, sources, starting and ending inventory, and top sales
• Export sales and expenses to CSV for your spreadsheet or accountant
• Premium: a mileage log for hauls and errands, using the IRS rate for each year
• Premium: reports for a quarter or any dates, with a Schedule C summary showing which line each number goes on, and a printable mileage log
YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
No sign-up and no cloud account. Back up to a single file and restore it on a new phone in seconds.
MADE FOR ANY SCREEN
Material You design with light and dark themes, optional wallpaper colors, and layouts for phones, foldables and tablets.
FREE, OR GO PREMIUM ONCE
Resale Ledger is free with ads and tracks up to 50 unsold items. Premium is a single one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks unlimited inventory, haul insights, the mileage log and tax-ready reports, plus future Premium features. No subscription.
Whether you flip clothes, toys, vintage, electronics or anything you find on the weekend, Resale Ledger shows you the number that matters: what you keep.
Resale Ledger is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any marketplace named above. Fee presets are a starting point; marketplaces change their fees, so check current rates. Resale Ledger is a record-keeping tool and not tax advice.
