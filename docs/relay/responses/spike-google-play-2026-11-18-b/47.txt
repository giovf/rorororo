About this app
arrow_forward
Want confidence that your charity donations are actually making an impact? At CharityCheck, we're passionate about making charity data accessible and empowering individuals to make informed decisions about their giving.
With CharityCheck, you can delve into regularly updated financial summaries for UK charities (using data from official charity commissions), to help you better trust your favourite charities or discover new charities making an impact.
Features:
• Save your favourite charities for easy access
• Directly compare two charities side-by-side
• See regulatory reports at a glance
• Navigate to charity websites with a single tap
Download CharityCheck today and regain your donation confidence.
