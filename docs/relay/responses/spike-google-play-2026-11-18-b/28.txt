About this app
arrow_forward
Sage Sole Trader is the bookkeeping and tax app for UK sole traders,
landlords, freelancers and the self-employed.
Track income and expenses, scan receipts, connect your bank, send
invoices and stay organised for Self Assessment and Making Tax Digital.
Sage helps you keep digital records up to date, stay on top of your money,
and feel more confident at tax time.
Why sole traders and landlords choose Sage:
• MTD-ready
Keep digital records aligned with Making Tax Digital requirements and
prepare quarterly updates with confidence
• Track income and expenses
Log transactions on the go, including business income, rental income and
expenses, and always know where your money stands
• Connect your bank
Sync your bank feed and import transactions automatically, with less
manual entry
• Scan receipts quickly
Photograph receipts and store them against the right transaction in
seconds
• Simple invoicing
Create, send and track invoices, with a clear view of what’s paid and
unpaid
• Get paid faster
Use Payment Agent to create invoices by voice or text, accept Tap to Pay
payments, send smart reminders and match payments automatically
• Stay tax ready
Use HMRC-aligned categories to keep your bookkeeping clean and
organised all year round
• Manage everything in one place
View, search and categorise transactions across your accounts in one
simple app
Whether you’re a landlord, freelancer, contractor or self-employed
professional, Sage Sole Trader helps you stay organised, compliant and in
control.
No spreadsheets. No accounting jargon. Just simple bookkeeping, invoice
management and tax-ready records for sole traders, landlords and the self-
employed.
Download Sage Sole Trader today.
