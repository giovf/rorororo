About this app
arrow_forward
Mileage Tracker is a private log for your business miles. It records your drives, files each one with a swipe, and builds a report for tax time or an expense claim.
WHY PEOPLE SWITCH ✓ Private: no account and no cloud. Your trips are encrypted on your phone, and the app has no permission to connect to the internet. ✓ Pay once: a lifetime option instead of another recurring bill. ✓ Honest: if your phone blocks tracking, the app says why.
TRACK EVERY DRIVE ✓ Automatic mileage tracking notices when you start and stop driving (optional) ✓ Or tap Start for a manual trip, in the app, a widget or the Quick Settings tile ✓ Add a trip by hand ✓ Set up before you drive. Nothing needs your attention on the road.
FILE A TRIP IN A SECOND ✓ Swipe right for Business, left for Personal, with Undo ✓ Medical, Charity and your own categories, with purpose, client, parking and tolls ✓ Saved places and rules suggest a category. You always decide.
REPORTS IN ONE TAP ✓ Mileage log PDF with dates, places, purpose, category, distance and estimated value ✓ CSV for spreadsheets, and a ZIP with your receipts ✓ One-page expense claim for your employer ✓ Keep the details usually recorded in an IRS mileage log or for HMRC mileage ✓ Published US, UK, Canadian and Australian rates, dated and sourced, plus custom and employer rates
For self-employed people, gig drivers and sales reps.
FREE AND PREMIUM Free: unlimited manual trips, 15 automatic trips a month, 1 vehicle, on-screen totals, a plain CSV of all trips, tile and widget, app lock and encrypted backup. Premium: unlimited automatic trips, PDF, formatted CSV and ZIP reports, unlimited vehicles and saved places, rules, parking and toll receipts, tags, custom rates, merge and split, odometer tracking.
HOW LOCATION IS USED Automatic tracking is optional. If you turn it on, Mileage Tracker collects location data to detect and record your drives, even when the app is closed or not in use. It needs location access "all the time" and activity recognition. A notification shows during every recorded trip. This data stays on your phone. A finished trip keeps only its start and end points, times and distance unless you turn on Keep routes. Manual trips use location only during the trip.
ACCURACY Distance comes from your phone's location and may differ from your odometer. Signal gaps are marked "part estimated". Automatic tracking can miss trips, often due to battery settings. The Tracking health card shows what to fix.
SUBSCRIPTION TERMS Premium is a yearly subscription, or a lifetime purchase. When offered, new subscribers get a 7-day free trial. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel: anytime in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. Lifetime is one payment with no renewal.
Mileage Tracker keeps your records. It does not give tax or legal advice. Check what you can claim with a qualified professional or your employer.
