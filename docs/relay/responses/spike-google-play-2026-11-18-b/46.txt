About this app
arrow_forward
Sanctions & Trade Intelligence (STI) is the ultimate digital companion for maritime, shipping, and logistics professionals.
Simplify your daily operations with a suite of powerful tools designed for accuracy and compliance. Whether you are checking a vessel for sanctions or calculating fuel density, STI provides instant, reliable results.
KEY FEATURES:
1. Sanctions Screening (Powered by Swoop Gateway)
- Perform real-time due diligence checks on entities, vessels, and companies.
- Screen against major global sanctions lists (OFAC, UK, EU, UN) to ensure compliance.
- Instant "Safe" or "Warning" indicators to help you make informed decisions quickly.
2. Maritime Calculators Say goodbye to manual errors with our industry-standard calculators:
- VCF Calculator: Compute Volume Correction Factors (VCF) using standard density and temperature parameters.
- Unit Conversions: Instantly convert between Metric Tonnes (MT) and Kilolitres (KL) for various fuel types.
- Density Conversion: View both Observed Density and Density @ 15°C side-by-side for clarity.
3. Commercial Tools
- Quality Claims: Calculate potential shortage claims or water content deductions in seconds.
- Cost Build-up: Estimate final pricing by adding premiums, insurance, and freight costs to your base price.
- Currency Converter: Live exchange rates to handle multi-currency transactions effortlessly.
WHY CHOOSE STI?
- Professional Accuracy: Built with maritime industry standards in mind.
- User-Friendly Design: Clean, dark-mode interface designed for ease of use in any environment.
- History Log: Automatically saves your recent calculations for quick reference.
Download STI today and navigate your trade with confidence.
Disclaimer: This app is a support tool. Sanctions screening results are for information purposes only and should not replace official legal compliance procedures.
