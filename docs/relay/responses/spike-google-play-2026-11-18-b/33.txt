About this app
arrow_forward
Companies House Check makes the UK company register easy to read. Search more than 5 million companies and their directors, see who is really behind a business, read its accounts, find its office on the map and get an alert the moment something changes.
SEARCH ANY UK COMPANY
• Find companies by name or number, and people by name
• Active, dissolved and disqualified records
• Clear profiles: status, type, age, registered office and industry (SIC)
• Filing deadlines with days left and overdue warnings
FIND IT. VISIT IT.
• See the registered office on the map
• Street View where available
• Directions in Google Maps in one tap
• Discover similar companies nearby
UNDERSTAND THE NUMBERS
• Turnover, profit, net assets, cash and staff from filed accounts
• Five-year trends and charts
• Key ratios: liquidity, working capital, margins, equity and growth
DUE DILIGENCE IN ONE TAP
• 12 checks: filings, late filing history, insolvency, charges, ownership, directors' track record and registered address
• A health score built from register signals
• Shareable PDF company reports
SEE WHO'S BEHIND IT
• Directors and secretaries, current and past
• Persons with significant control and their share bands
• Connections: every other company run by the same people
• Director track record, including dissolved and insolvent companies
WATCH THE REGISTER LIVE
• How many companies were registered today and this week
• The biggest sectors in the UK
• New company leads: save a filtered search and get new matches every day
STAY AHEAD
• Alerts for new filings, director changes, ownership changes, status changes and overdue accounts
• Follow directors and see their new appointments
ORGANISE
• Save companies, build lists, add notes and tags
• Compare up to four companies side by side
• Export to CSV
• Open and share every filed document
CH CHECK PRO
Free: search, company profiles, the map, current officers, filing history, 3 documents a day, 5 saved companies and alerts on 1 company.
Pro unlocks financials, due diligence, ownership, connections, similar companies nearby, all new companies, unlimited alerts and saves, lead alerts, compare, reports and export.
Choose a weekly, monthly or yearly plan, or pay once for Lifetime. Payment is charged to your Google Play account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel them any time in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and does not renew.
Companies House Check is an independent app. It is not affiliated with Companies House or the UK Government. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. The health score is not a credit rating or financial advice.
