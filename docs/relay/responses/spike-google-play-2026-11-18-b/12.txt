About this app
arrow_forward
Profit Calculator for eBay & Amazon is the fastest way to calculate seller fees and see your real profit before you list an item. Built for online sellers, resellers, drop-shippers and side-hustlers who need accurate numbers not guesses.
Stop losing money on listings you thought were profitable. Enter your sale price, item cost and shipping, and instantly see exactly what you’ll keep after marketplace fees.
★ KEY FEATURES ★
▸ eBay fee calculator final value fees, insertion fees and category-based rates
▸ Amazon fee calculator — referral fees and selling-on-Amazon costs
▸ Etsy fee calculator — listing, transaction and payment-processing fees
▸ Real profit, not just fees — see net margin in your local currency
▸ Shipping cost factored in — never forget postage again
▸ Works across 12 marketplaces worldwide
▸ Beautiful dark and light themes
▸ 100% offline — no account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking
▸ Lightning fast — instant results as you type
★ SUPPORTED COUNTRIES & MARKETPLACES ★
• United States (eBay.com, Amazon.com, Etsy)
• United Kingdom (eBay.co.uk, Amazon.co.uk, Etsy)
• Australia (eBay.com.au, Amazon.com.au, Etsy)
• Germany (eBay.de, Amazon.de, Etsy)
• Canada (eBay.ca, Amazon.ca, Etsy)
• France (eBay.fr, Amazon.fr, Etsy)
• Italy (eBay.it, Amazon.it, Etsy)
• Japan (eBay.co.jp, Amazon.co.jp)
• Ireland (eBay.ie, Amazon.ie, Etsy)
• Singapore (eBay.com.sg, Amazon.sg)
• India
• Malaysia
★ PERFECT FOR ★
• eBay sellers checking final value fees before listing
• Amazon FBA and FBM sellers calculating referral fees
• Etsy shop owners pricing handmade items
• Resellers, flippers and thrifters sourcing inventory
• Dropshippers calculating product margins
• Anyone who wants to know real take-home profit, not just sticker price
★ WHY SELLERS CHOOSE PROFIT CALC ★
• Accurate fees — built using current marketplace fee structures
• Multi-country support — switch marketplaces in one tap
• No fluff — just enter, calculate, sell smart
• Privacy first — your numbers stay on your device, always
• Free forever — no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no premium tier
★ PRIVACY YOU CAN TRUST ★
Profit Calc collects zero personal data. The app works completely offline. No analytics, no ads, no third-party tracking SDKs, no account required. Your sale prices, costs and margins never leave your device.
★ HOW IT WORKS ★
1. Choose your country
2. Pick your selling platform (eBay, Amazon or Etsy)
3. Enter sale price, item cost and shipping
4. Instantly see fees, net payout and real profit
Whether you list one item a month or run a full-time ecommerce business, Profit Calc helps you price smarter, source better and protect your margin.
