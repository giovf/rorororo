About this app
arrow_forward
Know what you really keep.
SellerBook 2 brings your sales, costs, expenses and inventory together. Whether you sell on eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop or Facebook Marketplace, see the profit behind every sale and keep your business organized.
■ Less typing, easier records
• Sales from screenshots: Let AI read up to 10 transaction screenshots at once. Review and edit the item names, amounts and other details before saving.
• Expenses from receipts: Scan a receipt with AI to help fill in an expense. Check the details, then save.
• Chat-to-log: Describe a sale or expense in your own words, review the summary and confirm it to create the record.
AI can make mistakes. Always check extracted details and answers.
■ See where your money goes
• Record the sale price, item cost, shipping, platform fees and packing costs together.
• Track business expenses by category, even on the Free plan, and see profit after expenses.
• Review monthly and annual charts of sales, costs and profits.
• Keep sales and expenses organized for your bookkeeping.
■ Your products, in view
• Attach multiple product photos to sales and check transaction status and profit in one list.
• Reuse product details when recording sales.
• Check stock quantities and low-stock indicators for each product.
• Organize customers, selling platforms, categories, shipping methods and packing supplies.
Product templates and product inventory management require Plus or Pro.
■ Ask AI about your business
Ask "What's my profit this month?", "Compare it with last month" or "Which products are low in stock?" Get help exploring sales by platform, product and customer, and checking inventory.
■ Work solo or with a team
Use the same account across multiple devices. Plus and Pro let you invite members with Owner, Leader and Staff roles. Pro supports up to 5 stores.
■ Plans
• Free: 1 store, solo use, up to 300 sales, expense tracking and 10 AI uses/month.
• Plus: $6.49/month or $64.99/year. 1 store, up to 5 members, unlimited sales, product inventory and 200 AI uses/month.
• Pro: $12.99/month or $124.99/year. Up to 5 stores and 30 members, unlimited sales, product inventory and 500 AI uses/month.
Member limits include the owner.
Chat, screenshot analysis and receipt scanning share one monthly AI allowance. Each chat message, screenshot analyzed or receipt scan uses one unit from the allowance, which is shared by the owner's team.
Prices are for the U.S. Google Play store and vary by country or region. Compared with 12 monthly payments, annual plans save about 16% on Plus and about 20% on Pro.
■ Subscription
Subscriptions renew monthly or annually. Manage or cancel them anytime in Google Play subscriptions.
