About this app
arrow_forward
Mileage Tracker UK is not a government app and is not affiliated with HMRC.
Not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to HMRC or the UK Government. Rates are taken from published GOV.UK guidance.
Track your business mileage and see the HMRC mileage allowance for every trip, worked out at the rate that applied on the day you travelled.
FREE, FOREVER, FOR TRACKING AND TOTALS
- Start shift / End shift GPS tracking, or add trips by hand
- Mark each trip business, personal, or leave it unclassified until you decide
- Edit or delete any trip at any time
- See your running total and progress toward the 10,000-mile threshold on the home screen
THE RIGHT HMRC RATE, EVERY TIME
- Cars and vans: 45p a mile for the first 10,000 business miles in the tax year, 25p after, for tax years up to and including 2025-26; 55p then 25p from 6 April 2026
- Motorcycles: 24p a mile. Bicycles: 20p a mile. Passengers: 5p a mile
- The 10,000-mile threshold is tracked correctly across all your cars and vans in the tax year, including a single trip that crosses the threshold partway through
- The tax year runs 6 April to 5 April, and every trip is rated using the date it happened
ONE-OFF UNLOCK (paid, one payment, no subscription)
- HMRC-style mileage log exports as PDF and CSV, by tax year, with a Self Assessment summary line
- Track more than one vehicle
YOUR DATA, YOUR PHONE
- No account, no sign-up
- Location is used only while a shift is running, to record the route and distance of that trip
- Nothing is sent anywhere; the app's only network use is Google Play Billing for the one-off unlock
- Back up your trips and vehicles to a file you control, and restore them any time
IMPORTANT
Mileage Tracker UK is not a government app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to HMRC or any government body. It is an independent tool. Mileage rates change from time to time — always check the current rate on the official sources below, and use your own judgement (or an accountant's) for your Self Assessment return.
OFFICIAL SOURCES (GOV.UK)
- Mileage Allowance Payments (AMAP) rates: "Travel — mileage and fuel rates and allowances" https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rates-and-allowances-travel-mileage-and-fuel-allowances/travel-mileage-and-fuel-rates-and-allowances
- Simplified expenses vehicle flat rates: "Simplified expenses if you're self-employed" https://www.gov.uk/simpler-income-tax-simplified-expenses/vehicles
Log business miles, apply UK mileage rates, export for Self Assessment.
