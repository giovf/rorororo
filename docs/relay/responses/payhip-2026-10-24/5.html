How Do I Get Paid?

Before you can sell through Payhip, you will need to connect to one of our supported payment processors. Payhip now offers a total of 13 payment processors, allowing sellers from all over the world to distribute their products! For details such as how to connect, available countries, supported payment methods, how payouts work, and fees, please refer to the articles below:

  • Connect Your PayPal Account
  • Connect Your Stripe Account
  • Connect Your Mollie Account
  • Connect Your Square Account
  • Connect Your Mercado Pago Account
  • Connect Your Flutterwave Account
  • Connect Your Paystack Account
  • Connect Your Xendit Account
  • Connect Your Midtrans Account
  • Connect Your PayU Account
  • Connect Your Razorpay Account
  • Connect Your Iyzico Account
  • Connect Your PayTabs Account

Please note: In most cases, you are able to connect PayPal plus one other payment processor for card and other applicable payment types.
