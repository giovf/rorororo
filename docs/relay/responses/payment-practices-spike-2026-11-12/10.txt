Details
These statistics show how large UK businesses pay their suppliers, using data reported under The Reporting on Payment Practices and Performance Regulations 2017 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukdsi/2017/9780111153598/contents) .
The statistics include:
average time to pay (days)
proportion of invoices paid late, by number (%)
proportion of invoices paid late, by value (%)
number of reports submitted by businesses
Each release presents:
the annual averages for all reporting businesses
region and sector breakdowns
