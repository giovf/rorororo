Frequently Asked Questions
When would we be added to templates/waterfall setups?
Once the integration is live, we can add you to templates or waterfalls depending on the integration use case. We will make the additions by our own discretion and inform you of any additions or updates.
How do I apply, and what happens after I submit
Kindly click the Submit Proposal button, fill out our questionnaire, and share as much information as possible to enable us to evaluate your request.
How can I learn more about more collaboration opportunities with Clay?
Please fill out our questionnaire, and a member of our partnerships team will reach out to learn more and/or set up a meeting.
How soon will the integration be live? Can we get an ETA?
Integration typically depends on the workload and existing priorities but we typically get this set up within 30-60 days. Once we've begun building it, an engineer will ask any questions in our shared Slack channel. We avoid planning any co-marketing prior to an integration being completed.
What makes Clay users a unique source of product feedback?
Clay users are creative, AI-forward, Tech-savvy professionals who use these products on a day-to-day basis and are equipped to identify what’s a valuable product and what could potentially make it better.
What kind of promotion can I get once becoming a partner?
We offer a variety of mutually-beneficial marketing initiatives such as website inclusion, co-marketing (post-integration), template placements, and waterfall/enrichment workflow inclusions where relevant.
