How to calculate the price for app on your monday account?

Pricing for our monday.com apps is based on two things:

  1. Your monday seat tier

  2. The billing cycle you choose - Monthly or Annual

Even if only a few people actively use the app, monday.com charges according to your total number of seats, since the app integrates across the entire workspace.

✔ Important: Annual plans are charged monthly

If you choose an annual plan, you do not pay the full year upfront.
Instead, monday.com charges you once per month at the discounted annual rate.

Example:
If your annual plan is $31/month, you pay $31 each month, not $372 at once.
