💱 Currency rates scraper

Scrape currency rates from multiple banks and financial institutions and store the results in a structured format. Use data to compare rates, analyze trends, you name it!

🚀 Features

  • ⬇️ Scrape currency rates from various banks with one command
  • 📊 Compare rates across different banks
  • 🏦 Store rates in structured format (JSON, CSV, etc.)
  • 🥩 Raw data for each bank stored separately for further analysis
  • 💱 All rates are in CZK (Czech Koruna) as the base currency

🌅 Use-cases

  • Financial Research: Save and compare rates when investing or trading currencies. Used in crypto invest calculator curzy 💸

  • Travel Planning: Find the best currency exchange rates before your trip.

  • Business Analysis: Analyze trends in currency rates to make informed decisions.

By default, the scraper will fetch rates from all supported banks. To filter banks, remove the unwanted banks from the Banks to scrape array in the input.
