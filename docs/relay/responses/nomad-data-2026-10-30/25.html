Monda and Nomad Data Partner to Make it Easy for DaaS Companies to Join the World’s Largest Data Market

Written by

Lucy Kelly

Product Marketing Manager
Monda

Published on

October 8, 2024

Table of contents

Boston, MA / New York, NY - 8th October 2024 

‍

Nomad Data, the world’s largest data market with thousands of providers, has partnered with Monda, the all-in-one data monetization platform. The partnership will give data providers using Monda visibility amongst companies in Investment Management, Consulting, Insurance, CPG, Pharma, Telco, as well as other industries.

‍

Nomad Data: Enabling Data Access for Everyone

Nomad Data revolutionizes how businesses harness the power of data. The platform provides access to the world’s largest data market, with thousands of providers listed there. Nomad Data enables users to organize and centralize all data knowledge in one place, and unlock critical insights from data hidden across documents in their organization. As a result, teams are equipped to make smarter, faster decisions so that any company can use data to drive business.

‍

Facilitating Data Procurement Across Industries

Nomad Data's platform targets companies in Investment Management, Consulting, Insurance, CPG, Pharma, Telco, as well as other industries. Its users leverage external and internal data to drive strategic decisions, optimize operations, and uncover new insights. Nomad Data enables organizations to access diverse datasets and supports everyone from CDOs to analysts in enhancing operational efficiency and accelerating innovation using the power of data.

‍

“We’re thrilled to join forces with Monda. Together we can enable even more companies to find valuable external data. Nomad Data empowers organizations to make smarter, faster decisions by enabling actionable insights that can truly drive business growth.” 
Brad Schneider, CEO, Nomad Data

‍

Features to Unlock the Value of External and Internal Data 

Access Over 3,500 Providers

Tap into the world's largest market of data providers, offering a diverse range of datasets to suit any business need. 

 

Uncover Existing Data

Identify and leverage data your company already owns or creates to maximize value.

‍

Turn Documents into Data

Unlock the hidden data within your company's largest store of knowledge—documents—and convert it into actionable insights to drive strategic business decisions.

‍

“Partnering with Nomad Data will bring a lot of value to Monda’s data provider customers. The enterprises using Nomad Data to search for datasets are an ideal audience for Monda’s users. We’re looking forward to working with Nomad to expand the ever-growing selection of data providers on their platform with the best-of-class DaaS companies using Monda.”
Thani Shamsi, Founder and CEO, Monda

‍

Monda: The All-in-one Data Monetization Platform

Monda’s data monetization platform has three key differentiators: Firstly, the product is easy-to-use, enabling business users to create data products and publish them on their own data storefront or data marketplaces in clicks. Secondly, Monda’s all-in-one vertical SaaS approach enables companies to run and manage their entire data business on one platform. Thirdly, customers value Monda’s world-class service and support from an international success team and data monetization experts.

‍

The partnership between Monda and Nomad Data will enable Monda users to sign up for priority onboarding to Nomad Data. Users can do this securely from their Monda portal within 2 minutes. Learn more about Nomad Data’s partnership with Monda on their Partner page.

About Monda

Monda is a leading B2B SaaS company in the data-as-a-service industry. Monda’s all-in-one data monetization platform empowers any company to share and monetize their proprietary data safely. The company was founded in 2024 by Thani Shamsi and Richard Hoffmann as a spin-off from Datarade, the largest comparison platform for data providers. With the vision of democratizing data monetization, Monda aims to create a borderless data sharing ecosystem to fuel the AI revolution and accelerate human progress.
