How it works

  1. Simply describe what you’re looking for and we anonymously route your request using machine learning & artificial intelligence to providers who have data that is most applicable to your need.
  2. Each data provider examines your search, ensures their data is a good fit, and responds with detailed information on how they can address your use case.
  3. Evaluate the responses and determine which best suit your need and we’ll put you in direct contact with experts from your chosen data providers.
  4. Our communications hub manages all your discussions – keeping you organized and helping your search move forward efficiently.
