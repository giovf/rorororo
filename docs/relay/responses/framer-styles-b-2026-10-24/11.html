About Shadow Supply

Stop guessing at shadow values. Shadow Supply gives you instant access to 20 handcrafted box shadow presets, organized by style and built for real design work.

What it does

Browse a curated library of premium shadows across 5 categories — Soft, Sharp, Layered, Colored, and Inset. Each preset is carefully crafted to work beautifully on cards, buttons, modals, images, and any other element in your Framer project.

Click any shadow to reveal its complete breakdown: Type, Position, Color, Opacity, X, Y, Blur, and Spread — every value you need to enter it directly into Framer's shadow panel in seconds. No guesswork, no tweaking from scratch.

Features

  • 20 premium shadow presets across 5 categories

  • Per-value copy buttons for fast, accurate entry into Framer

  • Search shadows by name or category

  • Favorite your most-used presets for quick access

  • Clean, dark UI that feels native to the Framer editor

Who it's for

Shadow Supply is built for Framer designers who want professional-looking depth and elevation without spending time dialing in shadow values manually. Whether you're building a landing page, a SaaS dashboard, or a portfolio, the right shadow makes everything feel more polished.

Use cases

  • Adding depth to cards and feature sections

  • Giving buttons and CTAs a lifted, tactile feel

  • Creating layered elevation systems across a design

  • Applying colored glows to accent elements

  • Getting inset shadows on input fields and pressed states
