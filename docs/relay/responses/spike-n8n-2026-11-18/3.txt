n8n Legal
There are a few key legal documents that are central to life at n8n — for us, our
customers, our community, our partners, and anyone using our platform. To make
it easy for you to find the information you need, we’ve gathered them all in one
place and included a short overview of what you can expect in each.
n8n Self-Serve Terms This page contains our terms and conditions applicable to all paid self-serve plans. (/legal/self-serve-terms/)
n8n Master Enterprise Terms This page contains our unified master Terms applicable to n8n Enterprise customers, either on a Cloud or Self-Hosted plan. (/legal/master-enterprise-terms/)
End User License Agreement This End User License Agreement (“EULA”) shall govern and apply to all end customers procured through, or otherwise acquired by, resellers that have entered into a valid and binding agreement with n8n GmbH. (/legal/eula/)
n8n Sustainable Use License This page explains the fair-code licenses for n8n’s source code: the Sustainable Use License and the n8n Enterprise License. (https://docs.n8n.io/n8n-community-license)
n8n AI Terms These Terms govern the use of AI-powered features within n8n Software and Services. (/legal/ai-terms/)
n8n Credit Terms These Credit Terms govern the purchase and use of prepaid credits for n8n usage-based Services like AI Connect or AI Assistant. (/legal/credit-terms/)
Nod Terms of Use These Terms of Use govern the use of Nod, alongside the Master Enterprise Terms or Self-Serve Terms and the n8n AI Terms. (/legal/nod-terms-of-use/)
n8n Customer Acceptable Use Policy This Acceptable Use Policy sets out the permitted and prohibited uses of n8n's Software and services. (/legal/customer-acceptable-use-policy/)
Privacy Policy This page explains our data processing activities on our website: what we collect, how we use it, how long we retain it, what your rights are, etc. (/legal/privacy/)
Privacy Policy for Recruiting This page explains our data processing activities within our recruiting process: what we collect, how we use it, how long we retain it, what your rights are, etc. (/legal/recruiting-privacy-policy/)
Data Processing Agreement This page contains our pre-signed data processing agreement as processor for customers to download, countersign and return. (/legal/data-processing-agreement/)
Security This page gathers n8n’s key security docs: compliance info, SOC reports and general technical and organizational measures (TOM) such as secure development practices, access controls, data encryption etc. (/legal/security/)
n8n Trust Center To give customers full visibility into how we protect their data, n8n provides a dedicated Trust Center that brings together everything related to security, compliance, privacy and reliability. It highlights our policies, certifications, data handling practices and our commitment to operating a secure and dependable platform for teams of any size. (https://trust.n8n.io/)
