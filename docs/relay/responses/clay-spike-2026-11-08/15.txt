Clean, Enrich, and Structure UK & German CRM Accounts with Beauhurst
Fill gaps in UK and German company records, layer in funding data for smarter routing, and map corporate structures to identify true buying centers — all automatically synced back to your CRM.
Start building now with
Beauhurst (https://www.clay.com/integrations/data-provider/beauhurst)
Download the template (https://app.clay.com/shared-table/share_0tbnw9wme9cdeSzDzQG)
What we used
Clay (Paid): Powers the full CRM enrichment workflow — pulls in existing account data, runs Beauhurst's enrichment actions across every row, applies scoring and routing logic, and syncs enriched records back to your CRM automatically.
Beauhurst: Provides three core actions — Enrich Company, Find Company Funding, and Find Company Corporate Structure — that fill gaps in company records, surface funding signals, and map corporate hierarchies. You'll need to bring your own Beauhurst API key to connect the integration inside Clay.
Your CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, or other): The source of your existing account data and the destination for enriched records once the workflow runs.

Get started
Dirty CRM data is one of the biggest blockers to effective outbound and account management — missing fields, outdated funding information, and unclear corporate structures mean reps are working with incomplete context and routing decisions are made on bad data.
This Claybook solves that by applying Beauhurst's three core actions to your existing CRM accounts. Enrich Company fills in missing firmographic details like company registration numbers and LinkedIn URLs so every account is actually actionable. Find Company Funding adds funding data at scale — grants, total fundraising, and investment history — so you can build routing logic, identify growth signals, and trigger re-engagement at the right moment. Find Company Corporate Structure maps parent-subsidiary relationships so you can merge duplicates, route complex accounts to strategic sales, and disqualify records whose parent company puts them outside your ICP. Once enriched, everything syncs automatically back to your CRM on whatever cadence you set.
‍
How it works:
Pull in your CRM data from Salesforce, HubSpot, or any other CRM integration to populate your Clay table with the accounts you want to clean and enrich.
Run Enrich Company to fill gaps in your company records — pulling registration numbers, LinkedIn URLs, and other missing firmographic data directly from Beauhurst's dataset.
Run Find Company Funding to layer in funding history at scale — number of grants, total fundraising, and investment rounds — so you can build routing logic, flag growth signals, and trigger re-engagement workflows.
Run Find Company Corporate Structure to map parent-subsidiary relationships for every account — giving reps clarity on true buying centers, surfacing duplicate records, and flagging accounts whose parent company falls outside your ICP..
Sync enriched data back to your CRM using an Update action to keep Salesforce, HubSpot, or any other CRM automatically up to date on whatever cadence you set.

Information
Follow the step by steps here in Clay
Download the free template
1
Step 1: Copy this template
First, copy this template in Clay.
If you’re already a user, you’ll be directed to your workspace. If not, you’ll be prompted to create a free Clay account.
The interactive demos on the right will walk you through each step of this Claybook.
‍
2
Step 2: Pull in your CRM data
Use your CRM integration to import existing account records into Clay as the starting point for enrichment. Customize your import using the following available inputs:
CRM source (Salesforce, HubSpot, or any other connected CRM)
Account filters (e.g. only pull accounts missing key fields, or accounts within a specific segment or ownership tier)
Fields to import (company name, domain, existing firmographic data, owner, and any other relevant CRM fields)
‍
3
Step 3: Run Enrich Company
Use the Beauhurst: Enrich Company action to fill gaps in your company records with Beauhurst's dataset. Customize your enrichment by mapping the following available identifiers and outputs:
Company name or domain as the identifier
Company registration number
LinkedIn URL
Firmographic details (industry, headcount, location, and more)
‍
4
Step 4: Run Find Company Funding
Use the Beauhurst: Find Company Funding action to layer detailed funding history onto each account. Customize your funding data capture using the following available outputs:
Number of grants received
Total fundraising amount
Individual funding rounds (round type, date, amount, and investors)
Use as a routing trigger — e.g. flag accounts that have raised a Series A or above for enterprise routing
Use as a re-engagement signal — e.g. trigger outreach when a new funding round is detected
‍
5
Step 5: Run Find Company Corporate Structure
Use the Beauhurst: Find Company Corporate Structure action to map parent-subsidiary relationships for each account. Customize your structure mapping using the following available outputs:
Parent company identification
Subsidiary list
Use to merge duplicate records (e.g. same parent company appearing multiple times in your CRM)
Use to route complex accounts to strategic sales based on parent company size or tier
Use to disqualify records whose parent company falls outside your ICP
‍
6
Step 6: Sync enriched data back to your CRM
Use the Update CRM action to automatically push all enriched fields back to your Salesforce, HubSpot, or other CRM instance. Customize your sync using the following available inputs:
Fields to update (registration number, LinkedIn URL, funding data, corporate structure, ICP score)
Update cadence (set the table to rerun on a schedule to keep records continuously up to date)
Conditional update logic (only overwrite empty fields, or overwrite all with latest Beauhurst data)
CRM destination (Salesforce, HubSpot, or any other connected CRM)
‍
Get Started
