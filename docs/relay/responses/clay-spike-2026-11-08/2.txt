Who on my team should use Clay?
Here are a few typically successful team setups we’ve seen with Clay.
At enterprise companies:
GTM leaders decide the criteria for lead enrichment, segmentation, and outreach
A GTM operations team member implements the above criteria into Clay. (Clay is the team’s “enrichment layer,” which syncs to the CRM to keep data live and accurate).
SDRs do not interface directly with Clay. Instead, they benefit from already-curated leads, enriched data, personalizations in their CRMs.
For example, one client’s SDRs simply click a button in Salesforce to fetch enriched data from Clay, including relevant a company summary, relevant contacts, and a draft message.
At startups, a growth lead often implements Clay and runs all of the company’s outreach single-handedly. Once the company reaches some level of volume, these startups hire a sales team member to help with live conversations.
At outbound agencies, one founder often sets up and runs Clay campaigns on behalf of many clients.
What kinds of companies uses Clay?
We serve GTM teams of all sizes, ranging from large enterprise companies to SMBs, startups, solo founders, outbound agencies, and more. We have thousands of users and hundreds of paying customers, including Ramp, Pave, Verkada, and more.
What is Clay?
Clay helps go-to-market teams up level their data enrichment and automate personalized outreach. You can start with a list of leads, either prospected through Clay or from your own CRM, and follow the steps below.
Step 1: Basic Data Enrichment & Cleaning
Clay combines 170+ data enrichment tools into one marketplace, resulting in superior data coverage and quality—on anything from contact information to company firmographics. Our customers have tripled data coverage vs. providers like Zoominfo while cutting costs by a magnitude of ten or twenty.
Step 2: AI Data Enrichment
We enable you to use AI to do research that previously required manual SDR work, including reading and analyzing webpages, PDFs, and Google searches for specific information.
Step 3: Automated Outreach
Once you’ve nailed your data enrichment, you can use AI to craft relevant, compelling messaging based on that data. Everything syncs back to your CRM, allowing your team to go from campaign idea to execution in seconds.

Prospecting and list building
Do I pay credits for list building / prospecting?
Nope! You can build lists from LinkedIn, Google Maps, Github, CRMs, websites, and more for free on Clay. You only pay credits for data enrichment (e.g. finding contact information, firmographic data, etc.)
Data enrichment
What happens if one of your providers doesn't return valid data?
If a provider charges us for an API call, we will also charge Clay credits for that call. However, if a provider refunds us due to the inability to deliver valid data, we will correspondingly refund those credits back to you. Click here (https://university.clay.com/docs/credit-refunds) to view a list of actions that will refund if invalid data is returned.
What prospecting, data enrichment tools, and CRMs can I access with Clay?
For prospecting, we integrate with 10+ sources, including LinkedIn, Google Maps, Github, and more.
For data enrichment, we integrate with 75+ of the market’s top tools, including Hunter, Harmonic, etc. You can also our AI web scraper and enrichment tools, to read and summarize information on websites, PDFs, and more.
For CRMs, we integrate with Hubspot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Close, Loxo, and more.
You’re never limited by our native integrations: you can also use webhooks and Clay’s HTTP API feature to connect to any tool that you have API access to.
For a full list, check out https://www.clay.com/integrations .
How can Clay give me better data coverage and pricing than Zoominfo or other data providers?
How can Clay give me better data coverage and pricing than Zoominfo or other data providers?
We often double or triple data coverage rates at a 1/5th or less of the cost of Zoominfo. A recent enterprise client, seeking to enrich inbound leads with basic company/contact info, had 30% coverage rates with Zoominfo at about ~25 cents per enrichment. They increased their coverage rates to 80% while cutting costs to less than ~1 cent per enrichment with Clay.
The reason why we have better data coverage is simple: we have multiple providers and waterfall them.
The reason we have lower costs…Large data providers like Zoominfo buy and whitelabel small providers, then charging a massive premium for subscriptions. Clay, on the other hand, is very transparent. We tell you exactly which individual providers your data comes from, and we give you access to wholesale rates from them. The price of Clay depends entirely on the enrichment tools you choose to use. In most cases, even with higher-cost tools in your Clay stack, Clay will be more effective and affordable than Zoominfo.
How good is Clay’s data quality?
Clay doesn’t have any native data. Instead, we allow you to get data directly from 100+ external enrichment tools in our central, credit-based marketplace. You can choose the providers you like most on Clay, including Hunter, Apollo, SalesNavigator, and more, and get their data directly into your workflows.
Customers often skyrocket their data quality and coverage using our waterfall feature, which lets you search multiple enrichment tools in order for any given field of information—and only pay once a result is found.
We also enable AI-based enrichment to scrape and summarize websites, PDFs, and more.
Can I connect Clay to something that isn’t on your integrations list?
Yes. You can use our HTTP API feature to connect to any tool that you have API access to. That includes 6Sense enrichment & intent data, G2 intent data, Cognism enrich, Wappalyzer technographics, Zapier workflows, etc.
Do I need other accounts or API keys in order to access tools in Clay?
You don’t need any outside accounts to use Clay. You can pay with Clay credits to access most of our data enrichment tools. Most people find that Clay, an email sending tool, and a CRM are all they need to get started sending campaigns.
If you do already have active subscriptions with other data enrichment tools, you can use them in Clay for free by using your API keys.
Can I access people that aren’t on LinkedIn with Clay?
Yes! We have various sources that you can use to kick off your list building. That includes scraping Google Maps to find local business leads, Github for engineering contributors, and more. You can also scrape any website with our Chrome Extension to kickstart your list building online.
Do you have global coverage?
Yes. We consolidate 75+ different enrichment tools, some with better global or regional coverage than others—you can choose which work best for your situation! If you ever find any gaps, let us know at support@clay.run (mailto:support@clay.run) and we’ll add a provider or help find you a solution.
Does Clay enable lead scoring?
Yes! You can assign your leads scores based on any criteria you’d like in Clay. See this video (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYrtjU_nfP8) for more information.
What types of triggers & signals can Clay help me track?
An example of a trigger you can use in Clay is Finding Jobs from LinkedIn. There are other providers that can help you find news, headcount changes, etc. You can use our AI web scraper to monitor websites for changes as well. We’re actively working on expanding our list of trackable triggers.
How does Clay’s Chrome extension work?
Clay has two Chrome extensions. One of them can help you scrape any website for any list. Another connects to any website to help you pull leads into Clay, automatically enrich them, and send them to your CRM—in one click.
Can I schedule data enrichments so they re-run on their own?
You can’t currently schedule enrichments, but we’re actively working on a solution. If you’re using a table with a CRM source, it’ll be live and updated in a two-way sync every 24 hours. (Just a reminder, you can simply click a button to re-enrich up to 50k records in a single table, even if it’s manual).

Personalized outreach
How do I send emails with Clay?
Clay doesn’t replace your email sequencer. Instead, we integrate with them so you can push finalized outreach from Clay to your sequencer and live campaigns.
You can use our AI integration to draft personalized lines of emails based on the research in your tables. Once you have a set of messages, you can connect them to an email sending tool of your choice.
We integrate natively with Smartlead, Instantly, Salesloft, Outreach, Hubspot and others. You can connect to any tool you want with HTTP API. You can also send messages directly to your CRM and use your CRM’s automations to send emails from there.
CRM integrations
Which CRMs do you integrate with?
We integrate with Hubspot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Close, Loxo, and more.
How do teams use Clay for CRM enrichment?
Clay is the enrichment layer that exists in a 2-way sync with your CRM to keep it always up-to-date. Your marketing operations team can set up all the enrichments you need in Clay. Because these will auto-sync to your CRM, your SDRs can operate simply in your CRM as usual.
For example, SDRs can find a relevant company and click a box to trigger Clay enrichments in the background. Within a few seconds, all the relevant enrichments will be added to your CRM as fields for them to reference.
Pricing and credits
Do you offer a referral bonus?
Yes! Any current user can refer other users by navigating to their workspace settings in the app and clicking "Referrals". When somebody signs up for a paid account using your referral link, both of you will earn 3,000 credits.
Unfortunately, we aren't able to grant any referral credits to those who didn't sign up using a referral link.
Referral credits are not subject to the usual rollover rules, so they stay in your account until you use them.
What are Clay credits and how do they work?
Clay credits are the currency you use to purchase different data points and actions in Clay. The vast majority of actions in Clay are either free, or cost 1-2 credits, but the average cost of a data point or action varies depending on the provider.
For example, finding an email costs 2 Clay credits if you use Prospeo, DropContact, Datagma, or Hunter.io (http://hunter.io/) . However, finding an email from People Data Labs costs 5 credits—simply because we pay more money to get the same data from People Data Labs vs. others.
Some data points, like mobile numbers, are harder to find and are more expensive as a result. Finding a mobile number starts at 2 credits, but it can cost 5 (People Data Labs), 13 (Selligence), 15 (ContactOut), or even 25 (Datagma) depending on the provider you use.
Which actions cost credits (or are free)?
List building/prospecting and data cleaning is free on Clay. Integrations with CRMs or email sending tools are also free on our Pro plan.
We charge credits for data enrichment, because we have to pay data providers to access lead or account research. That includes things like fundraising data, LinkedIn profiles, contact information, AI web scraping, etc.
If you have existing subscriptions to any data enrichment tools, however, you can use them free of cost in Clay by connecting your own API keys.
Do credits roll over?
If you are on a monthly plan , unused credits will roll over and accumulate in your account. The maximum accumulation is capped at 2× your plan’s monthly credit limit .
For example, if your plan includes 50,000 credits per month, your maximum balance would be 100,000.
• If you cancel or downgrade, you can use any excess credits until your current billing cycle ends. After the billing cycle ends, your balance will be reduced to the credit rollover limit of your new plan.
• If you are on an annual plan , you are eligible for a 15% credit rollover of unused credits, provided you renew on the same or a higher-tier plan.
Note: Your rollover and renewal happen on the same day and at the same time you originally subscribed (this timestamp is now shown in your billing details). Processing may take a few hours after that timestamp , so there can be a delay before you see credits updated.
How can I get more credits if I run out?
If you only need more credits for a particular month, you can purchase credit top-ups directly from your billing modal. New credits will be added to your balance instantly and there will be no changes to your plan or billing cycle. If you foresee an ongoing increase in credit usage, you can upgrade to the next tier in your pricing plan. This will reset your billing cycle and you will receive the new credit allotment from the new plan instantly.
Do I get charged for adding new people to my workspace?
Nope! You can add as many people as you’d like to your workspace, completely free.

How can I change my plan?
You can change your plan in your billing section at any time. If you downgrade, you can enjoy your plan's current features until the next payment due date. If you upgrade, your cycle will reset immediately, and the new credit allotment is added on top of your current credit balance (but will still respect our rollover policy and not go over your limit) so that you can use them and available features right away.
If I upgrade to the next plan, do I get to keep existing credits in my account?
When you upgrade, your new credit allotment is added on top of your current balance and your cycle resets immediately, though rollover rules still apply (max 2x your monthly limit).
One exception: moving from an annual Legacy plan to a Modern plan works differently — instead of carrying over, you're credited for the value of your existing credits as a discount on the new plan. Reach out to support and we'll walk you through what that looks like for your account.
How can I cancel my plan?
You can find the option to cancel in the Billing section at any time. You’ll enjoy your plan’s features until the next payment due date; at that point, you’ll be moved to the free plan. You can subscribe to a new plan whenever you’d like by clicking on the Upgrade Plan button that would then replace Billing.
Do you offer any discounts?
We have a 10% discount if you sign up for an annual plan—and you get all your credits upfront! We also have a non-profit discount of 25%. If you qualify, please send your 501c3 documents to support@clay.run (mailto:support@clay.run) .
How is the enterprise plan different than others?
By moving to enterprise you unlock features that:
Make your team more efficient at using Clay (credit use reporting, custom internal templates)
Enrich more leads (larger credit packs, unlimited LinkedIn)
Unlock new use cases (Continuous CRM Enrichment, 1st Party Data from Snowflake).
Free trial
Why is there a 50-row limit for trial and free accounts?
Trial and free accounts are limited to 50 rows per table. This helps new users explore Clay more broadly by trying different data sources, enrichments, and workflows before committing credits to large actions. It also prevents accidentally using up all your credits on a single table before you have had a chance to see everything Clay can do.

Sales taxes
How is sales tax calculated?
On your invoice, sales tax is calculated based on Clay’s determination of the taxability of the product(s) you purchased, the address information provided by you, and the tax rate of the applicable jurisdiction where you are located. If you need to update your address information please contact support@clay.com (mailto:support@company.com) .
‍
Why am I being charged for sales tax?
For customers located in the United States, Clay is required to charge and collect taxes on sales in certain jurisdictions that have laws requiring the taxation of our products AND Clay has either a physical presence or has met certain economic thresholds within that jurisdiction. These taxes vary by jurisdiction but include sales taxes and excise or privilege taxes.
For customers located in the United States where Clay’s products are taxable but Clay does not have a physical presence or has not yet met the economic thresholds, no sales tax will be charged. This however may change in the future due to changes in law or Clay meeting any of the requirements for charging and collecting tax in that jurisdiction.
‍
What happens with the sales tax collected?
The entire amount charged to a customer for sales tax is remitted to the required
jurisdiction and no amounts are retained by Clay.
‍
What if my business is exempt from sales tax?
Certain businesses such as non-profit organizations, educational institutions and other organizations may have tax exemptions in various jurisdictions. If your business has an exemption certificate please contact support@clay.com (mailto:support@clay.com) and provide a copy of the exemption certificate to us.
