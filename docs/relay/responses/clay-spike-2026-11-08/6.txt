March 10, 2026
Beauhurst Integration: Private Company Financials, Funding, and Corporate Structure for UK & Germany
Access deep private company data for the UK and Germany — including funding rounds that never get publicly announced — to identify and act on commercial inflection points before competitors.
Go to "Add Enrichment" > select "Beauhurst" > choose "Enrich Company (UK & Germany)"
Pull verified firmographics including phone number, registration number, LinkedIn URL, activity status, and location to segment accounts and confirm ICP fit
Disqualify inactive companies automatically using live activity status signals
Build accurate account lists for EMEA outbound with data that goes far beyond what public sources provide. Learn more → (https://university.clay.com/docs/beauhurst-integration)
Find Company Funding
Surface private funding activity — grants, stealth rounds, and early-stage raises — before they hit the news.
Go to "Add Enrichment" > select "Beauhurst" > choose "Find Company Funding (UK & Germany)"
Retrieve number of funding rounds, total amount raised, grant funding received, and latest valuation per company
Set minimum thresholds to qualify accounts and trigger re-engagement sequences based on recent raises
Roughly 70% of UK fundraising is never publicly announced — Beauhurst gives you visibility on all of it. Try it out → (https://university.clay.com/claybooks/clean-enrich-and-structure-uk-german-crm-accounts-with-beauhurst)
Find Company Corporate Structure
Map the full entity tree for any UK or Germany company domain to understand buying center hierarchies and whitespace.
Go to "Add Enrichment" > select "Beauhurst" > choose "Find Company Corporate Structure (UK & Germany)"
Retrieve ultimate parent, intermediate parent, and all child entities for a given domain
Use structure data to merge CRM duplicates, route complex accounts to strategic sales, and find intro paths through existing customers
