Linked Pro for Zoho CRM

Enrich your data for better prospecting

Linked Pro is an advanced Zoho CRM extension designed to elevate your prospecting and data enrichment efforts. With Linked Pro, you can seamlessly search for LinkedIn profiles of your leads, contacts, and accounts using the provided name, location, and company details. The extension delivers precise and relevant search results at your fingertips, simplifying your data management. Furthermore, Linked Pro empowers you to get a comprehensive view of a company by allowing you to explore its employees directly within the accounts module. The bulk export feature lets you effortlessly transfer multiple profiles to Zoho CRM in a single operation, streamlining your workflow.

New to Linked Pro are powerful features like sending LinkedIn connection requests directly from Zoho CRM, initiating and monitoring LinkedIn direct messages within the CRM, and managing your LinkedIn connection list right alongside your Zoho CRM contacts. These enhancements make Linked Pro an indispensable tool for professionals looking to maximize their LinkedIn integration within Zoho CRM, empowering you to connect, engage, and grow your network like never before.

Key Features:

  • Precise Search Results: Obtain the most relevant results based on name, location, and company, ensuring you find the exact information you need quickly and efficiently.
  • Comprehensive Company Employee Access: Easily locate and view all employees of a specific company by searching within the account’s module.
  • LinkedIn Connection Requests: Expand your network effortlessly by sending connection requests directly from Zoho CRM.
    LinkedIn Connection List Management: Access and manage your LinkedIn connection list directly from Zoho CRM, keeping all your key contacts organized in one place.
  • Direct Messaging & Conversation Monitoring: Stay on top of all interactions by initiating and monitoring LinkedIn direct messages within the CRM.
