About 3D PDF Flipbook

3D PDF Flipbook Viewer turns any PDF into an interactive 3D flipbook directly in Framer. Create realistic page-turning experiences for magazines, catalogs, brochures, ebooks, portfolios, presentations, menus, reports, and other PDF documents.

Upload your PDF and display it as a polished 3D page flip viewer with smooth, realistic page-turn animations.

3D PDF Flipbook Viewer

Transform static PDF files into an engaging online flipbook that visitors can browse naturally page by page. Instead of embedding a basic PDF viewer, present your document as an interactive book with a realistic 3D page-turn effect.

Customizable

Adjust the appearance and behavior of your flipbook to match your Framer website. Create anything from a clean document viewer to an immersive digital magazine or product catalog.

Built for Framer

Add your PDF flipbook directly to your Framer website and create interactive document experiences without sending visitors to an external PDF reader.
