About FrameLoc - Native Framer Translation

Translate your Framer site into any language. Pay once, depend on nobody.

FrameLoc is a native localization plugin built for Framer. It reads every text layer of your pages, lets you translate them inside the plugin, and saves everything as code files in your own project. Visitors load translations from your site, not from a third-party server: no monthly fees, and no outside service to slow your pages down.

Why FrameLoc:

• One-time payment per project. No subscription, no per-language billing, no page limits.

• Native. Translations live in your Framer project. Export them any time as CSV, XLIFF or XLSX.

• Unlimited languages and pages, including regional variants and right-to-left.

• Pages, CMS items and components.

• Type translations, paste them from any chat AI, or optionally connect Google or Azure.

• Rules: keep texts untranslated, pin terms, exclude pages.

• Customizable language switcher included.

• Multilingual SEO with language URLs (FrameLoc Plus).

Plans and pricing: https://frameloc.app/pricing
