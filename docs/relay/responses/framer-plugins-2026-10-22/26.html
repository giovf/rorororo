About Text Effects Kit

Text Effects Kit is a collection of animated text effects, typography animations, kinetic text, typing effects, text reveals, pointer interactions, and creative text styles made for Framer.

Add polished text animations and typography effects to headings, hero sections, landing pages, portfolios, buttons, titles, and interactive website content — directly inside Framer.

Animated Text Effects for Framer

Explore effects including Iridescent, Limelight, Prisma, Jitter, Magnetic, Glass, Blueprint, Vapor, Stardust, Laser, Split Flap, Rubber Band, Ransom Note, Soft Blur, Pixel effects and many more.

Multiple Effect Categories

Browse a large collection of effects organized into:

  • Text Effects — creative visual treatments and animated typography

  • Reveal Effects — animated entrances and text reveals

  • Typing Effects — typewriter and character-by-character animations

  • Kinetic Text — moving, shifting and dynamic typography

  • Pointer Effects — text interactions driven by cursor movement

  • Text Styles — distinctive typography treatments and visual styles
