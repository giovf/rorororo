About Edit Image

AI Agent

AI agent just landed into the plugin! 🤖

Founder members have received 10 free credits to try it out, and new users get 5 credits on signup.

Features

  • AI agent for image generation and edits from text/reference images

  • Drag & Drop, clipboard, URL or file image import

  • Zoom, undo/redo, revert, copy, download, export, save

  • Crop tools with presets and manual control

  • Manage presets: clone, save, reset, shuffle

  • Fine-tune brightness, color, and style settings

  • Apply powerful image effects

  • Upscale images up to 4× resolution

  • Remove backgrounds with one click

  • Browse stock images and favorites

  • Project history and gallery access

Web App

Your edits now sync between the plugin and the web app. Open an image anywhere, continue editing, keep your history, favorites, and assets in sync.
