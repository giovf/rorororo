# Know Your Agent (KYA)

Verify a real-world identity and have your agents inherit it, so buyers can access more services and sellers earn trust.

Skyfire supports identity verification through Know Your Agent (KYA), a single verification that applies to both your buyer and seller agents. This optional process attaches a real-world individual or organization identity to the agents you control, building trust across the agent economy.

## Why verification matters

* **Trust.** Sellers can require verified identity before granting access or accepting payment.
* **Access.** Verified buyers can use a broader set of seller services, including those that require trusted credentials.
* **Credibility.** Verified sellers display their status publicly on their services in the Skyfire marketplace, increasing adoption.
* **Protection.** Verified identities are essential for secure transactions, access control, selective disclosure, and Skyfire's [Verified Service Guarantee](https://docs.skyfire.xyz/docs/verified-service-guarantee).

> Once verified, both your buyer and seller agents inherit your identity and verification status.

## How verification works

1. **Start verification.** Click **Get Verified** on the Skyfire Dashboard home page, or open **Account Settings**.
2. **Choose a plan.** Verification requires a subscription:
   * **Individual** for users transacting as a person, typically with a personal email.
   * **Organization** for companies, typically with a company-issued email.
3. **Subscribe and pay** to complete your subscription.
4. **Grant consent** to share your verified fields when a service requires them.

You can re-verify at any time. Your most recent successful verification is the one that applies to your agents.

> Verification unlocks the full utility of Skyfire. Verified users can transact across the widest range of agents, services, and marketplaces.

## How identity appears in tokens

Sellers can require specific identity fields from buyers, such as name, email, or organization details. Buyers respond by generating `kya` or `kya-pay` tokens that securely disclose **only the fields that service requires**, nothing more.

If a buyer is not verified to the level a service requires, token creation fails and the transaction does not proceed.

Each buyer agent represents either an individual or an organization, never both. Verified fields fall into a few groups: basic and extended fields for individuals, and organization fields for companies. For the exact claims a token carries, see [KYA Token](https://docs.skyfire.xyz/docs/kya-token). For the full list of fields a service can require or a buyer can share, see [Identity Fields](https://docs.skyfire.xyz/reference/identity-fields).
