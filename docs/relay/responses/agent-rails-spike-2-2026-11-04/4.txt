# Settlement of Payments

* Tokens have an expiry of between 10 seconds and 24 hours.
* Beyond this, seller services have a 24 hour grace period to charge tokens they receive.
* Full settlement of a token takes place after the expiry of the grace period within a 3 hour window.
* Settlement of payments can therefore take up to 51 hours.
* However, when seller services make charges on tokens, these charges become eligible to settle sooner if they total more than $1.00.
* As soon as the cumulative charges on a token total more than $1.00 they settle within the 3 hour window.
