About HM Revenue & Customs
HM Revenue & Customs is the United Kingdom's tax, payments, and customs authority. It publishes one official exchange rate table per calendar month — released ahead of the month it applies to — and that single rate is what UK customs declarations and VAT invoices must use for the whole month, regardless of how the market moves in between.
Official website: www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-revenue-customs (https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-revenue-customs)
Official rate publication: Monthly exchange rates for customs and VAT (https://www.trade-tariff.service.gov.uk/exchange_rates)

Why use the official HM Revenue & Customs rate?
When an auditor or tax office in United Kingdom asks which rate you used, "the GBP mid-market rate at 14:32" is not an answer — the official rate published by HM Revenue & Customs for that date is. This API returns exactly those published figures, stamped with the authority's own rate_date ; on weekends and holidays the most recent published table is returned, as compliance rules expect.
Pairs the authority doesn't print directly are resolved from its table (inverse or cross via GBP) and explicitly flagged "derived": true — so what the authority printed and what was computed are never confused.
Endpoints
GET /api/v1/central-bank/hmrc/latest — latest table or single pair (all plans)
GET /api/v1/central-bank/hmrc/&lbrace;YYYY-MM-DD&rbrace; — the table for any date (paid plans)
GET /api/v1/central-bank/hmrc/history — time series, up to 5,000 rows (paid plans)
GET /api/v1/central-bank/hmrc/availability — publication calendar: the dates the bank actually published (all plans)
Full parameter reference in the API documentation (/docs/#central-bank) . Every request counts toward your plan's monthly quota — rates change once a month, so caching a published table locally makes a small quota go far.

Is there an API for HM Revenue & Customs exchange rates?
Yes — this one. Official monthly GBP rates as clean JSON, latest table on every plan including free.
How often does HM Revenue & Customs update its rates?
New tables land monthly — one official table per calendar month, published ahead of the month it applies to — the API picks each one up shortly after HM Revenue & Customs publishes it (latest: 2026-11-01).
Is this the official published table?
Yes — the rows are HM Revenue & Customs's published figures, untouched, with the original publication date attached. Pairs the authority didn't print are computed and flagged derived .
