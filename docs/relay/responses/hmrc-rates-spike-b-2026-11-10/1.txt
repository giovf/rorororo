Frequently Asked Questions
What is the uptime of the AllRatesToday API?
We target 99.99% availability. Our API runs on Cloudflare's global edge network for maximum reliability and low latency worldwide. You can check real-time status on our Status Page (https://status.allratestoday.com/) .
Where does the exchange rate data come from?
Live rates come from institutional foreign exchange market data: true mid-market, with no spread added by us. Official rates are ingested from each central bank's or tax authority's own publication and served unmodified with its publication date — see the full list of institutions (/central-bank-rates-api/institutions/) and data sources (/data-sources/) .
Do you offer official central bank exchange rates?
Yes! In addition to real-time mid-market rates, we publish official reference rates from 123 central banks (/central-bank-rates-api/) — including the US Federal Reserve, European Central Bank, Bank of England, People's Bank of China, and Bank of Japan. The latest published rates are available on every plan, including Free. Historical central bank rates and time-series data are included in all paid plans.
How often are the exchange rates updated?
Market rates are refreshed every 60 seconds on every plan, including Free — many providers update hourly or daily. Official central-bank and tax-authority rates update when each institution publishes, and every response carries the publication date.
I need more than 1,000,000 requests per month!
No problem! Large covers 100,000 requests a month and Business 1,000,000. Beyond that, contact us (/contact) and we'll put together a custom plan that works for your needs.
Do you offer yearly billing?
Yes! You can save 17% by switching to yearly billing — that's 2 months free on any paid plan. Use the toggle at the top of this page to see yearly prices.
Can I cancel my plan at any time?
Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription in less than 30 seconds from your dashboard. No contracts, no hidden fees, no hassle.
Can I use the free plan in production?
The free API key is designed for personal and non-commercial use. For production applications, upgrade to a paid plan which includes commercial use rights and higher limits.
What happens if I exceed my request limit?
You'll receive a 429 status code with rate limit headers until your quota resets at the start of the next month — there are no overage charges, so you are never billed more than your plan price. We email you at 90% and 100% of your quota so you can upgrade in time.
Not sure which plan suits your needs?
Our team is here to help you find the right fit for your project.
