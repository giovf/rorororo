Details on how to get support, what topics we provide support for, and other options to find the answers you need.

Your success is our success

We’re committed to giving you an exceptional experience on the Bubble platform, whether you’re on a paid or free plan. With this in mind, we’ve built a team specialized in our product — and backed by our values — and invested in a bot powered by AI to get you the support you need quickly and efficiently. While our goal is to assist and unblock you on most topics when building and running an application on Bubble, there are a few things that fall outside our scope of support.

Topics we support

We can assist you with questions about the Bubble platform, like:

  • Account and billing questions

  • FAQs about the company or platform

  • Questions about core Bubble platform features (e.g., the app editor and plugin editor)

  • Platform-level bug report investigations (if you are experiencing unexpected behavior with Bubble’s platform, please let us know by filling out this form)

Topics we don't directly support

Certain topics are not supported by our in-house team and AI bot, like features that are not native to our platform or developed by third parties. These topics include:

  • Custom code

  • Building and/or troubleshooting custom plugins

  • Third-party API integrations

  • Plugins or templates that are published by third-party developers

  • Designing and building apps for you

Support hours

Bubble support is available to you 24/7/365. Our AI bot and our Support team are available 24/7. Certain topics, like bug report investigations, may require input from specialized teams who work from Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM ET, excluding US holidays. For questions routed to a specialized team, we strive to respond to everyone as quickly as possible. Please note that certain complex questions and bug reports may require more investigation time and take longer to resolve.

How you can reach us

You can use the AI bot to get fast and reliable answers by clicking on the widget in the bottom left-hand corner of the Support center. If at any point you’d like to be connected with our Support team, the AI bot will ensure your message makes it to the correct team.

Note: The Support team can only offer support via email and chat at this time, as it’s the most efficient way to share information across several teams and assist with questions quickly. If you need more hands-on strategic and technical guidance as you scale your app, please contact sales here to learn more about our Technical Success Management team.

We're here to help

Please feel free to reach out! We’ll always do our best to help so that you can keep building and scaling on the platform. Your success is our success.
