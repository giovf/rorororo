Check when large businesses pay their suppliers
You can search for a report that tells you:
the average time it takes for a large business to pay its suppliers
the proportion of payments (for example, invoice payments) that it does not pay on time
Start now (https://check-payment-practices.service.gov.uk/search)
Before you start
A large business is a company or limited liability partnership that has at least two of the following:
£54 million in turnover
£27 million on its balance sheet
250 employees
A large business must publish its reports at least twice a year.
You can download all published reports (https://check-payment-practices.service.gov.uk/export) .
Other ways to apply
Apply by email
Email Payment Practices Reporting to apply.
Payment Practices Reporting
paymentpracticesreporting@businessandtrade.gov.uk (mailto:paymentpracticesreporting@businessandtrade.gov.uk)
