About Clean my Framer

Framer Crystal Clean is the maintenance tool every Framer project needs.

As your site grows, unused components pile up, hidden layers stay forgotten, images stay uncompressed, and variant libraries bloat, slowing down your workflow and your site.

Clean my Framer gives you a clear view of what's cluttering your project and lets you remove it instantly.

Here’s what you get:

  • Image optimization : Compress frame background images and CMS collection images to WebP. Set your compression level (80–100%) and apply it across every matching frame or CMS field in one click.

  • Clean hidden layers : Find and delete layers that are hidden across all breakpoints (Desktop, Phone, Tablet) — no more invisible dead weight.

  • Clean unused resources : Identify and remove unused components, text styles, and color styles that accumulate over time.

  • Check used resources : Get a full audit of which resources are actually being used in your project.

  • Clean unused variants : Remove variant combinations you no longer need and keep your component library tight.
