About Noteframe

Manage tasks, collect client feedback, & collaborate with your team without ever leaving Framer. Noteframe is the only project management tool built specifically for Framer projects.

How It Works

  1. Open the plugin inside Framer.

  2. Create tasks, set deadlines, & assign priorities.

  3. Invite your team & assign tasks to the right people.

  4. Collect client feedback directly on your live Framer site.

Key Features

Manage tasks, feedback & client projects - without leaving Framer.

  • Task management - Create tasks, set deadlines, & assign priorities so nothing slips through the cracks.

  • Client feedback - Let clients & stakeholders leave comments on your live Framer site without touching the project.

  • Team collaboration - Invite team members, assign tasks, & tag people in comments to keep everyone aligned.

  • Flexible task views - Switch between Kanban board & list view. Filter by due date, priority, & more.

  • Notifications - Tag team members & get notified so you always know what's moving & what's not.

  • Free for personal use - Free forever for solo use. $3.99/month per team member for teams.
