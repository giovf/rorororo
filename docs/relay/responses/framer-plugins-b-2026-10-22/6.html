About FramerForms

FramerForms supercharges Framer's native forms with everything they're missing. Multi-step flows, file uploads, conditional logic, & reCAPTCHA. 25+ input types - all built & styled directly on your canvas, without iframes or third-party widgets. Forms that look like the rest of your site, because they're built the same way.

How It Works

  1. Open the plugin & click 'New Form' - choose Single or Multi-Step.

  2. Add your inputs by clicking to drop them in.

  3. Style everything on the canvas.

  4. Connect your destination - email, Google Sheets, Zapier, or Make.

  5. Publish. Your form is live, responsive, and working.

Key Features

Multi-step forms

Guide users through pages with previous/next navigation, like Typeform but inside Framer.

File uploads

Collect documents & images directly through your form.

Conditional logic

Show or hide fields based on a user's previous answers.

Calculators

Build live pricing estimators, ROI calculators, and quote builders.

Quizzes

Scored quizzes with custom result cards and redirect logic.

Hidden fields

Capture UTM tags, referrer URLs, & page URLs in the background without cluttering the form.

25+ input types

Sliders, country selectors, star ratings, phone inputs, & more.

reCAPTCHA

Built-in spam protection to keep your submissions clean.

Full design control

Style every element to match your site. It lives on the canvas like anything else.
