Minimum Payout Thresholds
In order for a creator's earnings to be paid out, the total amount of accrued earnings must be equal to or greater than $25 USD .
When payouts are processed, creators with accrued earnings which are less than the minimum payout threshold will not receive a payment. Instead, their balance will carry forward until their earnings are greater than the minimum payout threshold, at which time they will be included in the next round of payouts.
Only earnings that are ready to be paid count toward the threshold. If you're a new creator, sales that are still inside the 30-day hold before your first payout aren't payable yet, and neither are earnings from an order with an open dispute. The Estimated Next Payout on your Payouts page uses the same payable amount. See Payout Holds and Pauses (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/386-payout-holds-and-pauses) for details.
Once per year, we will process a single payout of all creator earnings which are more than twelve months old and have not yet met the minimum threshold.
