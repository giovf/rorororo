Getting Started as a Creator: From Sign-Up to Your First Sale
Selling on Superhive (formerly Blender Market) starts with a few quick steps. There's no application and no waiting to be vetted: you open your shop in a couple of minutes, set up how you'll be paid, and send your first product to our team for review. This guide walks you through the whole journey and links to the detailed articles for each step.

1. Create and Confirm Your Account
You need a Superhive (formerly Blender Market) account with a confirmed email address. Sign up, then click the link in the confirmation email. If you try to open a shop while signed out, you'll be asked to create an account or log in; if your email isn't confirmed yet, you'll be asked to confirm it first.
Check that you can sell from where you live: see Who Can Sell Products on Superhive (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/55-who-can-sell-products-on-blender-market) .
2. Click Start Selling
Click Start selling at the top right of the site header (it appears on desktop-sized screens; on a phone or tablet, go straight to the Become a Creator page). On the Become a Creator (https://superhivemarket.com/become-a-creator) page, click Start selling again to open the shop setup form.
3. Fill Out the Shop Setup Form
The form asks for:
Shop name and shop URL (the form tells you whether the URL is free as you type)
Your first and last name
Your country and timezone
An optional Creator Newsletter sign-up
Acceptance of the Creator Terms of Use (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/44-terms-of-use-creator)
No proposal, portfolio, sample file, phone number or address is needed. Click Create my shop when you're done.
We can't set up shops for creators based in countries we can't send payouts to. If you choose one of them, you'll see We can't set up shops for creators based in … and no shop is created.
For a field-by-field walkthrough, see How to Become a Creator (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/182-how-to-apply-to-become-a-creator) .
4. Meet Your Creator Dashboard
You'll land straight in your Creator Dashboard with a green Your shop is ready message and a welcome checklist of things to do next.
This is a good moment to customize your profile (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/163-how-to-customize-your-profile) —add an avatar, banner, description and links so customers know who you are. For a tour of the dashboard, see Get to Know Your Creator Dashboard (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/173-get-to-know-your-creator-dashboard) .
5. Set Up Payouts
Follow the Set up Payouts step on your checklist. You need a working payout method before you can submit any product for review:
Stripe : complete the Stripe Connect setup from your Account settings (https://superhivemarket.com/account) ( My Account > Account > Primary Info , in the Payout Method section).
PayPal : add the email address of a PayPal account that can receive payouts.
You'll be asked for your current password when you add or change payout details. To find out which option works in your country, see Can I Be Paid as a Creator on Superhive? (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/273-can-i-be-paid-as-a-creator-on-blender-market) If you can't be paid through Stripe or PayPal, email [email protected] (/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#88fbfdf8f8e7fafcc8fbfdf8edfae0e1feede5e9fae3edfca6ebe7e5) and we'll help you get set up.
Whenever you save payout details, including the first time, payouts to those details start 7 days after the change, and we email your account address with a list of what changed. If you didn't make the change, click This wasn't me in that email. Once the 7 days are up, your earnings go out with the next scheduled payout. See How and When Creators Are Paid (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/46-how-and-when-creators-get-paid) .
6. Create Your First Product
Before you start, read the General Product Guidelines (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/253-general-product-guidelines-for-uploading-products) . Then go to Products in your Creator Dashboard, click New Product and follow How to Upload Your Products (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/41-how-to-upload-products) .
As a new creator, you can have one product in progress at a time until your first product is approved. While that product is a draft or waiting for review, New Product is disabled. See One Product at a Time for New Creators (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/388-one-product-at-a-time-for-new-creators) .
Every file you upload is checked for malware. Files show Scanning… at first and Scanned once they've passed (or Not fully scanned if a file passed but was too large or complex for a full scan). A file that's held shows Under review until our team has looked at it.
7. Submit It for Review
When every item on your product's checklist is complete, the Checklist card on your product's page shows a Publish on date and a Submit for Review button. Pick when you'd like the product to go live if it's approved, then click Submit for Review . (If the button isn't there, check that you've set up payouts.) Our team reviews every new creator's first product, and reviews typically take 3–5 days. While it's waiting, editing is paused.
Changes requested : your product goes back to Draft and you get a message in your Inbox with notes for each section to fix. Make the changes and submit again.
Declined : you'll get a message explaining why. You can revise the product and resubmit it, or start a new one.
Read more in Your First Product Review (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/209-creator-application-product-proposal-and-review) and Checking Your Product Status (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/195-checking-your-product-status) .
8. Approved: the Limit Is Lifted
When your first product is approved, you'll see Your first product is approved, so you can now add as many products as you like. Your product goes live on the Publish on date you chose, or shortly after approval if that date has passed. The one-product limit is gone for good, and you can now email your customers (up to 3 emails in any 24 hours, at least 1 hour apart). See How To Email Customers (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/33-how-to-email-customers) .
9. Your First Sales and Payouts
Congratulations on your first sale! As a new creator, each sale's earnings become payable 30 days after the sale. The Payouts page in your Creator Dashboard explains this and shows how much is still inside the 30-day window. Once your first payout has gone through, you're paid on the normal schedule. See How and When Creators Are Paid (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/46-how-and-when-creators-get-paid) .
Ready to grow? Check out Tips for Success (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/174-tips-for-success) and Creator Resources (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/192-creator-resources) .
