Available Commission Payout Methods
The best way to get paid for your commissions is through Stripe (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/273-can-i-be-paid-as-a-creator-on-blender-market) or PayPal (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/273-can-i-be-paid-as-a-creator-on-blender-market) . Check those lists to see if your region is included.
You need a working payout method before you can submit a product for review: either a completed Stripe Connect account, or PayPal with a valid PayPal email. Until then, your product's checklist stays one item short and there's no Submit for Review button. You can set this up from the Set up Payouts step on your creator dashboard, or in your account settings (https://superhivemarket.com/account) under Payout Method .
If you've found you are in a region without service from either of those platforms, we do have the ability to utilize WISE (formerly TransferWise) to pay to a limited number of countries:
Benin
Mali
Botswana
Nepal
Burkina Faso
Niger
Guinea-Bissau
Senegal
Hong Kong
Zambia
If you found your country on the list above, great! You can create your shop (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/182-how-to-apply-to-become-a-creator) and then follow the steps below.
Setting up WISE payouts
Once you've created your shop, there are a few additional steps needed to get you set up to receive your earnings.
Send an email to [email protected] (/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#8ffcfaffffe0fdfbcffcfaffeafde7e6f9eae2eefde4eafba1ece0e2) with the subject line, "New Creator - WISE Onboarding"
In the body of the email, include the following information:
Full Name on Account
Full Mailing Address
Postal Code
Bank Name
IBAN or BBAN, SWIFT
Account Currency
Occasionally, WISE will ask us for additional verification information. If they do, we’ll work with you to get that resolved.
You need a payout method on your account before you can submit a product for review. If you can't be paid through Stripe or PayPal, email [email protected] (/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#b0c3c5c0c0dfc2c4f0c3c5c0d5c2d8d9c6d5ddd1c2dbd5c49ed3dfdd) and we'll help you get set up.
WISE payouts are only available on the monthly payout schedule and are not available to be paid semi-monthly.
