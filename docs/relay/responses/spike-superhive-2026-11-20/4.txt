Who Can Sell Products on Superhive
In order to become a creator and sell your products on Superhive (formerly Blender Market), you must meet the following requirements:
You need to be at least 16 years old.
You will need a Superhive (formerly Blender Market) user account with a confirmed email address .
You need to be based in a country where we can set up shops. We can't send payouts to creators in some countries, so we can't open shops for creators based there.
You need to be able to receive payouts in your region (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/273-can-i-be-paid-as-a-creator-on-blender-market) , and you must add a working payout method before you submit your first product for review.
When you sign up for an account, you must use valid information.
You must agree to our Creator Terms of Use (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/44-terms-of-use-creator) .
There's no application to fill out and no waiting for approval. If you meet these requirements, you can open your shop right away.
Want to sell your products on Superhive (formerly Blender Market)? Head over to our Become a Creator (https://superhivemarket.com/become-a-creator) page and click Start selling to set up your shop. See How to Become a Creator (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/182-how-to-apply-to-become-a-creator) for a step-by-step walkthrough.
What Happens After I Open My Shop?
Your shop is created as soon as you submit the form. Before your products can go on sale:
Set up payouts. You can't submit a product for review until you have a working payout method.
Get your first product approved. Our team reviews every new creator's first product. Until it's approved, you can have one product in progress at a time. See One Product at a Time for New Creators (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/388-one-product-at-a-time-for-new-creators) .
Expect a payout hold at first. As a new creator, each sale's earnings become payable 30 days after the sale until your first payout has gone through. See How and When Creators Are Paid (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/46-how-and-when-creators-get-paid) .
What Are The Rules Once I'm Selling?
To ensure that Superhive (formerly Blender Market) remains a great marketplace full of high-quality products, satisfied customers, and Creators who are paid in a timely manner, we require that you abide by the following rules:
All uploads must be of your own creation—if you are selling or using products not created by you, you must have built on the original products and have adequate licensing that allows for sale and redistribution of those products.
You must be able to receive payments via our available payout options (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/273-can-i-be-paid-as-a-creator-on-blender-market) .
You must follow all requirements listed in the General Product Guidelines (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/253-general-product-guidelines-for-uploading-products) .
You must offer a reasonable level of support for your products.
These rules are part of our Creator Policy (//support.superhivemarket.com/article/277-creator-seller-policy) .
