Market Statistics
Superhive was launched on June 10th, 2014 with the goal of empowering artists and developers to grow professionally and to give back to Blender’s development and community. In pursuit of this, we believe it’s important to maintain transparency and make public the key figures around the marketplace. Below you will find some of the key stats that give insight into the heartbeat of Superhive.
