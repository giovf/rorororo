Algolia Realtime Search

A powerful Search API delivering relevant results from the first keystroke Starting at ~$0/hour.

Install Add-on

Blazing fast search-as-you-type

99% of queries just return in under 10ms, enabling you to create a stunning search-as-you-type experience.

Integrate in minutes

Pick your API client, push your data and start searching. You even get typo-tolerance out-the-box with no need of tedious configuration. A couple of settings are then enough to fit with your business specifics.

Developer support

Whether you need help, have ideas or just want to say “hello”, we’ll be there. We are developers too.

Full featured dashboard

An intuitive graphical interface for all operations and statistics: monitor performance, explore your data, change your settings, etc.

Outsmart your search

Sales, social, CRM outputs define your business. You can easily feed them into our engine to best answer your users’ queries. Algolia powered websites and apps get smarter with the increasing number of data defining their content, without ever compromising with simplicity.

First-class data security

Generate API keys that restrict access to a specific index, and set limits such as the maximum query rate for an IP address, or the key expiration time.

Offload your servers

Use our Javascript client to directly search from your web application. Not only will your users get a better response time by avoiding to go through your servers, but it will also offload your servers of unnecessary tasks.

Designed to search records, not pages

A perfect solution for SQL and NoSQL databases, with a transparent ranking algorithm optimized for semi-structured data. No matter your object types or your number of attributes, just push your data and start searching.
