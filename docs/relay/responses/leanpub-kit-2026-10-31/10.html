Upgrade to an Author Membership

Leanpub is a powerful platform for serious authors. With an Author Membership, you can take advantage of professional features to help you write, publish, and sell your books.

Unlimited Previews and Publishes

Preview or publish an unlimited number of times per month.

Before using Leanpub, the idea of previewing or publishing a book more than 20 times in a month would sound painful. But once you get used to just clicking a button to do it, you'll never want to go back.

Custom Themes and Print-Ready PDFs

Your book isn't the same as anyone else's. Your theme doesn't need to be, either.

Frequently Asked Questions about Author Memberships and Writing on Leanpub

This is the FAQ for Leanpub Author Memberships. If you're looking to learn more about Reader Memberships, please see the Reader Memberships page.

If you can't find what you're looking for below, chances are the answer is in our amazing Help Center which has hundreds of carefully-written articles and a number of lovingly-created videos answering the questions we've been asked over the years. Or, email our support team and one of us will get back to you.

    • What are Leanpub Memberships?

      Every Leanpub user can read and write on Leanpub. You can buy books and courses, build your Leanpub library, take courses, and even create your own books or courses. You can create books by writing in your browser or by uploading your own files, and you can create courses by writing in your browser. Leanpub also offers optional paid Memberships that give you additional capabilities. Author Memberships provide premium publishing and writing features. Reader Memberships provide access to about 2,000 “Free With Membership” books, plus book and course credits you can use toward purchasing any Leanpub books or courses.

    • Do Leanpub Users Have an Author Membership or Reader Membership by Default?

      Leanpub users do not have an Author Membership or a Reader Membership by default. Membership always refers to a paid subscription that adds extra benefits on top of what every Leanpub user can already do. This approach keeps things simple: all users can read, buy, and write on Leanpub, and Memberships are optional paid upgrades that unlock more value. If you don’t have a Membership, you still have full access to the standard reading and writing capabilities of Leanpub. If you choose to subscribe, you get a clear set of extra features tied to your Membership type.

    • What Can I Do as an Author Without an Author Membership?

      Lots! You can write in your web browser in our Minimal editor, Visual editor or Source editor, or upload PDF and EPUB files with our upload writing mode — three books and one course. You can preview or publish your books or courses up to 5 times per month. You can sell your books or courses on Leanpub and get paid the same amazing royalty rate as authors with an Author Membership. You can also create coupons, team discounts, bundles and tracks, and use all the other great marketing features we offer.

    • Why would I subscribe to an Author Membership?

      Leanpub Author Memberships give you more books and courses, more previews and publishes, and premium authoring features like custom themes, print-ready PDF export, GitHub and Dropbox writing modes, Google Analytics, InDesign export, GhostAI and more. Different Author Membership levels include different premium features. We have five different Author Memberships, so there's one for you! To learn more about Author memberships, visit the Author Memberships page under the Authors menu.

    • What royalty rate does Leanpub pay?

      We pay 80% on books and courses priced at $7.99 and up. Below $7.99, we pay 80% minus 50 cents. So, you earn $3.50 from a $5 sale, $8 from a $10 sale, $16 from a $20 sale, and $24 from a $30 sale. You'd be surprised how many people buy $20 and $30 ebooks, and $89 or $129 courses!

    • How do I write my book in my web browser?

      There are two ways to write your book in your web browser on Leanpub: in plain text, or using our visual editor. If you know what Markdown is, choose plain text. If you don't, but you're reasonably technical, choose plain text. Markdown is really simple to learn. You can learn everything you need in 5 minutes. If you're not reasonably technical, then consider choosing our visual editor.

    • Do you recommend writing on your computer or in your web browser?

      If you have a favorite text editor, write on your computer. If you don't, write in your web browser. Our in-browser writing experience used to be terrible, but our in-browser Source editor and visual editor are both really good now.

    • If I'm writing on my computer, do you recommend GitHub or Dropbox for syncing?

      If you know what Git is, use GitHub. If you don't, use Dropbox. Git and GitHub are amazing if you're a programmer. If you're not, well, have we mentioned Dropbox or our amazing in-browser Source editor? :)

    • What's Markua, and how is it related to Markdown?

      If you write your book in plain text, you can format it in Markua. Markua is basically Markdown, minus inline HTML, plus some optional extensions for adding things like index entries and other things which some books need and which Markdown doesn't natively support. Markua has an open specification based on the CommonMark spec for Markdown.

    • Do I need to use any of the Markua extensions?

      No. You can write your book in standard Markdown as originally defined by John Gruber, and things will work great, with one exception: don't use inline HTML, as it's not supported in Markua.

    • If I already have an ebook, what should I do?

      You just upload your ebook, and click the publish button. You can upload PDF and EPUB files. We do not modify your files in any way.

    • If I already have a Lifetime Standard Membership or Lifetime Pro Membership and I upgrade to a monthly or annual Max Author Membership, what will happen if I cancel my Max Author Membership later?

      We'll put you back on your Lifetime Standard Membership or Lifetime Pro Membership. It's lifetime, after all.

    • Does Leanpub use DRM?

      No, Leanpub does not and has never used DRM. Not only do we not add DRM to any ebooks we've generated, we also do not support DRM on uploaded ebooks. We believe that authors should worry more about obscurity than piracy.

    • Does Leanpub use watermarks?

      No. They irritate legitimate readers. We believe that authors should worry more about obscurity than piracy.

    • I have more questions, what should I do?

      Seriously, our Help Center is amazing. We put way more thought and effort into it than you would typically expect. Check it out, and if you can't find the answer, email us!
