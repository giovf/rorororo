This EYFS Fine Motor Foundational Skills Tracker is a simple and effective assessment spreadsheet designed to help Nursery and Reception practitioners track children’s progress in key fine motor and early writing skills throughout the year.
This easy-to-use EYFS tracking tool allows you to monitor development, identify next steps, and record interventions clearly and consistently. It is ideal for supporting EYFS assessment, fine motor progression, and mark making development across continuous provision.
The tracker focuses on essential foundational skills, including:
Pencil grip
Pre-writing shapes
One-handed tool use (Nursery to Reception)
Name writing
Letter formation
How it works:
Simply enter children’s names down the left-hand side of the spreadsheet. For each skill, type:
Y = Yes (skill achieved) → cell turns dark green
N = Not yet → cell turns red
S = Supported (achieved after intervention) → cell turns light green
Teachers can also right-click cells to add comments and notes, allowing you to record:
Observations
Evidence of learning
Interventions used
Progress over time
When a child achieves a skill after support, simply replace N with S to clearly show progress and intervention impact.
This EYFS Fine Motor Tracker is a highly practical tool for monitoring fine motor development, supporting intervention planning, and keeping assessment clear, visual and easy to manage. It helps practitioners track exactly what foundational skills children have in terms of fine motor control and early writing readiness.
