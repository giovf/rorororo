Ireland CRO Companies Scraper

Scrape Ireland's Companies Registration Office (CRO) by company name or number. Get registered name, CRO number, type, status, incorporation date, registered address and email. No API key. Export to CSV, JSON, Excel, or XML.

Ireland's official company register, the CRO, holds every company incorporated in the Republic of Ireland. This Actor searches it by company name or CRO number and returns each match as a flat row. No login, no API key, no rate limits to manage.

What it does

This Actor searches the Ireland CRO register by company name or number and returns each company as a flat row with its registered details.

  • 🔎 Name search: enter a company name or fragment, get every matching company.
  • 🔢 Number lookup: enter a CRO company number, get that exact company.
  • 📦 Batch export: collect up to 1,000,000 companies per run, export to CSV, JSON, Excel, or XML.

Results export to CSV, JSON, Excel, or XML, or straight from the API.

Troubleshooting

Why am I getting no results?

Check the spelling of the company name or number. Try a shorter name fragment. Also confirm the company is registered in the Republic of Ireland, not Northern Ireland.

Why did the run stop before my maximum?

The Actor stops when it has collected the number of companies you set in 'Maximum companies', or when there are no more matches for your query.

Why is the registered email empty for some companies?

Not all companies on the CRO register have a registered email. The field is only returned when the CRO has it on file.

Can I search by director name or address?

No. This Actor only searches by company name or CRO number. For other search types, use the official CRO website or a different tool.

FAQ

QuestionAnswer
What is the Ireland CRO?The Companies Registration Office (CRO) is the official register of companies incorporated in the Republic of Ireland. It holds details like registered name, CRO number, type, status, incorporation date, and registered address.
Do I need an API key or login?No. This Actor reads the public CRO register directly. You only need an Apify account to run it.
Can I search by company name?Yes. Enter a full name or a fragment in the 'Company name or number' field. The Actor returns all companies whose name contains that text.
Can I search by CRO number?Yes. Enter the exact CRO number in the same field. The Actor returns that one company.
What data does each company row include?Each row includes the registered name, CRO number, company type, status, incorporation date, registered address, and registered email, when available on the register.
How many companies can I collect?You can set the 'Maximum companies' field to any number from 1 to 1,000,000. The Actor stops after collecting that many matches.
What export formats are supported?You can export the results to CSV, JSON, Excel, or XML from the Apify dataset.
Is this the official CRO data?Yes. The Actor reads the public CRO register, the same data you would see on the official CRO website.
Can I run this on a schedule?Yes. You can set up a schedule in Apify to run the Actor daily, weekly, or at any interval, and get fresh data each time.
What if a company is not found?If the CRO register has no company matching your query, the Actor returns an empty dataset. Check the spelling or try a shorter name fragment.
