Connect your firm with the EUIPO
The EUIPO Application Programming Interfaces (APIs) have been developed to let our customers integrate their systems to manage their intellectual property rights.
Explore our APIs (product)
1 Explore
Start by having a look at our APIs. You will find what the we offer you, with all the information about the features and benefits.
2 Register, build & test
Once you have decided which APIs you want to include in your application, you will need to register for an account and request access to our sandbox environment. This will allow you to test your application(s) using our test data
3 Apply
When your applications have successfully been tested, you will need to apply for access to production.
