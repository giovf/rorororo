™️ TMview Trademark Search & Watch #tm-tmview-trademark-search-and-watch
🎯 Search 140+ million trade marks from the EUIPO, WIPO, USPTO and ~80 national offices in one run, and get alerted the moment someone files a mark that looks like yours.
Get each mark's name, office, application and registration numbers, status, type, Nice classes, owner, filing, registration and expiry dates, opposition window and image. Optionally add the full goods and services wording, applicants, representatives, oppositions and publications. Covers the EU trade mark, international (Madrid) registrations, the United States, the UK, every EU member state, China, Japan, Korea, Brazil, Mexico, India, Australia, Canada and many more.
🚀 What Does It Do? #what-does-it-do
This scraper searches TMview, the trade mark database run by the EU Intellectual Property Office together with ~80 national and regional offices. You get structured, ready-to-use data without clicking through search pages. Type a brand name or pick your filters, and hit Start.
💡 Three ways to use it:
🔍 Clearance search : check a name against every register before you launch a brand, with exact, contains, word or fuzzy matching.
👀 Trade mark watch : schedule it daily or weekly with Watch mode. Each run returns only the new or changed marks, such as a fresh conflicting filing, a status change, a registration or a renewal. Alerts can go to Slack, Discord, Telegram or any webhook, and each alert includes the opposition deadline.
📋 Portfolio and market monitoring : pull every mark an owner holds (e.g. Apple Inc. ), or every new filing in a class and office. For example, all EU clothing filings (class 25) from the last 30 days.

✨ Features #features
🌍 ~80 offices in one search : EUIPO, WIPO, USPTO, UKIPO, DPMA, INPI, the Polish, Spanish and Italian offices, China, Japan, Korea, Brazil, India and more
🔤 Six ways to match a name : contains, whole word, begins with, ends with, exact, and fuzzy for similar spellings (adidas ↔ adidaz)
🎛️ Precise filters : office, Nice class, status, mark type, owner, filing and registration dates, and "open to opposition right now"
👀 Watch mode : returns only marks that are new or changed since the last run, so daily monitoring stays cheap
⏰ Opposition deadlines : every mark shows its opposition window and whether you can still oppose today
🔔 Instant alerts : Slack, Discord, Telegram or your own webhook
📄 Full details on demand : goods and services wording, applicants with country and city, representatives, oppositions with the earlier mark, publications, priorities and the link to the office's own record
🖼️ Mark images : image and thumbnail links for figurative and combined marks
🔓 Beyond the 10 000 limit : big searches are split automatically by office, filing date and class, so you get all of them
🔗 Paste TMview links : trade mark pages or whole search-result pages work as input
⚡ Fast & Scalable : from a single clearance check to tens of thousands of marks per run
🧹 Deduplication : each mark appears once, along with the names that matched it
📤 Export Anywhere : download results as JSON, CSV or Excel, or push them to Google Sheets, Zapier, Make or your CRM

❓ FAQ #faq
🤔 How do I set up a trade mark watch?
Add your brand names, choose Fuzzy matching, pick the offices and Nice classes you care about, and turn on Watch mode . Then schedule the task weekly. The first run saves today's picture, and every later run returns only new filings or changes. Each one comes with its opposition deadline.
🤔 Which countries are covered?
About 80 registers: the EU trade mark (EUIPO), international Madrid registrations (WIPO), every EU member state, the UK, Switzerland, Norway, the United States, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, China, Japan, South Korea, India, Australia, New Zealand, Türkiye and many more.
🤔 Can I get more than 10 000 marks?
Yes. Set Max marks per search to 0 and big searches are split automatically by office, filing date and class, so nothing is cut off.
🤔 Can I find marks that look like mine but are spelled differently?
Yes. Fuzzy matching finds similar spellings, and Contains finds your name inside longer marks.
🤔 Can I export the data?
Yes. Export as JSON, CSV, Excel, XML, HTML or RSS, or push the data directly to Google Sheets, Zapier, Make or any webhook/API endpoint.
🤔 How often should I run this?
For a watch, weekly is the usual rhythm, because opposition periods last one to three months. Daily works too. You can schedule automatic runs on Apify with a few clicks.
🤔 Does it work with proxies?
You do not need to set one. A run starts from its own address at a polite pace, and if TMview's firewall does not answer that address it moves to Apify Proxy on its own after three refusals. That is the default and the recommended setting: leave Switch to proxies when blocked on. You can also turn on Apify Proxy in Proxy to use it from the first request.
If you switch the fallback off and set no proxy, the run has only its own address. When TMview refuses it, the run stops with an error after three refusals rather than retrying until it times out.
🤔 Can I read one trade mark from its TMview link?
Yes. Put the link ( https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/#/tmview/detail/<ST13> ) in TMview links . The record always comes with its full detail, including the goods and services wording in the office's language, for every office TMview covers. Filters such as offices or classes are not needed next to a link and are not used when links are the only thing you give.
🤔 Can I limit the retries?
Yes, with Retries per request ( maxRequestRetries , 0–10, default 5). Every refused or timed-out request is written to the log with its URL.

⚖️ Legal & Ethical Use #legal-and-ethical-use
This scraper collects only publicly available register information from TMview. It does not log in or bypass any access control. For applicants who are private individuals, it keeps only the name, country and city, never the street address. TMview is not an official register and its information has no legal effect, so always confirm critical decisions with the office's own record. Please use the data responsibly and in compliance with applicable laws.
