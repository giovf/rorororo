Cookie Consent & GDPR overview

  • Transparent Cookie Notice: Inform users about cookies used on your site clearly and legally
  • Privacy Policy Link Integration: Add a direct link for full legal transparency
  • Multi-Region Compliance: Support GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA, LGPD, and more in one app
  • Easy Setup & No Coding: Get your cookie banner live in minutes with a simple, no-code installation
Make your website GDPR, RGPD, CCPA, PIPEDA, APPI, LGPD, APA, NZPA, and US Laws compliant in minutes with a cookie consent solution. Let visitors accept, reject, or manage cookies in line with privacy regulations. Display a legal cookie banner, cookie notice, or privacy popup that informs visitors about your site’s use of cookies and helps you collect valid consent — no coding required. Choose from ready-made cookie bar templates and easily set their position to match your website’s style. Simple and clean design options work great on all devices, with responsive cookie banners that look perfect on mobile and desktop. Protect your business, build trust, and stay legally compliant — all with one simple cookie compliance app.
