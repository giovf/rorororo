Task Manager for Zoho CRM

Seamlessly manage your tasks inside Zoho CRM

Developed by Mav Consulting Group LLC

Built for: CRM Developed by: Mav Consulting Group LLC
  • Overview
  • Screenshots
  • Ratings & Reviews
  • Pricing
  • Vendor

This task management widget enables Zoho CRM users to efficiently create, assign, and track tasks across key modules—including Leads, Contacts, Accounts, Deals, and Vendors—without needing to switch platforms. Designed to simplify coordination and improve visibility, this tool supports better collaboration and accountability within your CRM environment.
