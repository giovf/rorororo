Arka lays out a house inside your outline and furnishes every room.
Generate: pick an outline with its entrance and windows, click Generate, and in a few seconds the rooms are laid out inside it. ◀ ▶ shows other layouts.
Auto furnish: pick a room's type, and Arka chooses furniture that fits its size and places it. Or make your own list and click Furnish.
Live: move a wall and the room is furnished again.
Draw walls, doors and windows yourself: any closed loop of walls is a room. Add a roof, or more storeys with stairs.
Free trial, then a subscription
Every install comes with a free trial: 30 furnishes, 3 Auto furnishes and 10 houses, with no account and no card. After the trial Arka is a subscription: $10 a month for 500 furnishes, 30 Auto furnishes and 100 houses, bought and activated inside the add-on (Lemon Squeezy, 14-day money-back).
Requirements
Blender 4.2.5 or newer (Windows, macOS, Linux) and online access: plans and furnishing run on Arka's server. The add-on sends the room outlines, doors, windows and furniture lists, nothing else from your scene.
