INTRODUCING
Stylizer VFX
Create editable stylized effects in Blender.
Choose from 74 presets, preview instantly, and customize only the controls you need. Build elemental attacks, cartoon impacts, smoke curls, spell effects, pickups and more with procedural mesh layers.
74 PRESETS 36 ELEMENTAL ATTACKS GEOMETRY NODES BLENDER 4.2+
FROM PRESET TO EFFECT IN SECONDS
Get from a preset to a working effect without building a node graph from scratch.
Choose an effect, click Preview , then tune duration, speed or emitter. Detailed shape, motion, look and export controls stay collapsed until you actually need them.
QUICK PREVIEW
Preview directly from the top of the panel without opening the deeper layer controls.
EDITABLE LAYERS
Adjust count, distribution, size, stretch, direction, seed, color, emission and lifetime curves.
PROCEDURAL
Geometry Nodes evaluates the effects from one normalized lifetime with no simulation cache required.
36 ELEMENTAL ATTACKS
Nine elemental families, each with a projectile, slash, circular slash and piercing slash.
Dark Earth Electric Fire Ice Nature Sound Void Water
Fire uses split tongues and embers, ice uses serrated edges and shards, while the other families add electrical strokes, rocks, foliage, wavelets, vortices and droplets.
COMBAT-READY MOTION
Quick onset, short dissolve, swept ribbons and bright leading cores keep attacks readable and responsive.
DETERMINISTIC SCRUBBING
Scrub forward or backward and get the same result from the same frame and seed.
USE YOUR OWN MODELS
Select a model and emit effects directly from its surface while keeping the model visible.
Or create an independent effect object for slashes, projectiles, impacts and other standalone VFX.
Object Origin Mesh Surface Vertices Collection
12 PROCEDURAL SHAPE SOURCES
Build effects from a flexible library of procedural sources and stylized profiles.
Puffs Ribbons Shards Rings Sparks Debris Runes Lightning Scatter Sparkles Beams Crowns
SOFT CARTOON
A stylized artistic response for soft, readable VFX.
CEL
Banded shading for stronger graphic separation.
UNLIT
Ideal for glowing attacks, spell effects and bright stylized elements.
BLOOM IN ONE CLICK
The Bloom action adds compositor glow to help bright cores and emission-heavy effects pop in rendered view and final output.
EXPORT YOUR EFFECTS
Bake Stylizer VFX for use outside Blender with multiple export workflows.
PNG / EXR Flipbooks VAT Alembic glTF
Destination-engine setup notes are included for Unity, Unreal and Godot workflows.
INCLUDED
74 editable presets, including 36 elemental attacks.
Twelve procedural shape sources with cartoon and elemental profiles.
Centered noir thumbnail wheel with category navigation and preset search.
Preview beside the preset picker with no automatic playback while browsing.
Origin, mesh surface, vertex and collection emitters.
Soft Cartoon, Cel and Unlit looks.
Dissolve, emission and Bloom setup.
Full, Half and Quarter viewport particle counts.
Flipbook, VAT, Alembic and limited glTF export.
Editable demo scenes and rendered previews.
Documentation, FAQ and engine setup notes.
COMPATIBILITY
Blender 4.2 and later. Checked on Blender 4.2 and 5.2. Supports EEVEE and Cycles.
NO SIMULATION CACHE
Core operation requires no external Python packages, smoke simulations, volumetrics or simulation caches.
BEFORE YOU BUY
Custom models can act as emitters but cannot replace the built-in particle source meshes.
Sound effects are not included.
Bloom is a compositor effect and requires a compatible rendered display.
glTF exports supported geometry animation with a simple tint, not the full procedural Blender material.
Engine setup notes are included, but engine-specific shaders and import configuration remain the user's responsibility.
Performance depends on effect density, transparency, viewport resolution and the rest of the scene.
