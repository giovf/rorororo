Glass Studio — Glass Made Simple
Create and fine-tune glass in Blender with 37 ready-to-use glass and mirror presets, rendered thumbnails and searchable libraries. Adjust colour, refraction, roughness, transmission, dispersion and coating through live radial controls, then add procedural frost, scratches, fingerprints, dust, moisture and weathering.
Explore repeatable variations with locked settings, preview materials in a dedicated studio, choose render quality presets and check geometry with built-in repair tools.
Save, rename, import and export custom materials with textures, and personalise the movable, resizable HUD with themes and keyboard shortcuts. Supports Cycles and EEVEE, with shader features depending on your Blender version.
Ready-to-Use Presets
Choose from 37 glass and mirror materials, each with a rendered preview.
Live Glass Controls
Adjust colour, roughness, refraction, transmission and more directly in the viewport.
Surface Details
Add procedural frost, scratches, fingerprints, dust, moisture and weathering.
Material Variations
Generate repeatable variations and lock the settings you want to keep.
Preview Studio
Preview your glass in a dedicated lighting setup without changing your project scene.
Render Quality
Choose Preview, Balanced or Final quality settings for Cycles.
Geometry Checks
Check your mesh for common glass issues, with tools for thickness, scale and normals.
Your Material Library
Save your own materials, browse previews and find presets quickly by name.
Import and Export
Share custom material libraries with their shader nodes, textures and thumbnails.
Theme the UI
Pick a built-in theme or create, save and share your own colours.
Flexible HUD
Move, resize or collapse the HUD to fit your workspace.
Keyboard Shortcuts
Open and hide the HUD quickly with customisable shortcuts.
Cycles and EEVEE
Set up glass for your render engine with one-click engine synchronisation. Limited support for EEVEE!
