CINEMATIC PRODUCT STUDIO
Build. Style. Animate. Done.
Create polished product animations in Blender without rebuilding the same studio setup every time.
Prepare → Frame → Light → Animate → Render
✦ ───────── THE TOOLKIT ───────── ✦
54
Product Animations
Luxury drifts, spins, reveals, drops, flips, orbits, fly-bys and stylised motion.
25
Camera Animations
Push-ins, pull-backs, cinematic orbits and camera-driven reveals.
52
Lighting Presets
Beauty, glass, jewellery, packaging, tech and more.
30
Compositing Looks
Clean commercial, beauty-soft, dark editorial, metallic, glow and premium finishes.
✦ ───── CAMERAS THAT FIT YOUR PRODUCT ───── ✦
Choose from Hero 3/4, Front, Low Angle, Top Down and Macro shots. Automatic framing adapts to your product, with lens controls, close-up padding and dedicated depth-of-field controls.
✧ ───────── STUDIO BACKGROUNDS ───────── ✧
Build a Cyclorama, 360 Studio or Image Background directly from the panel. Cycloramas adapt to your product size, with optional background lighting and editable gradients for campaign-style setups.
✦ ───────── LIGHT IT YOUR WAY ───────── ✦
Choose lighting by product type or by the look you want — from soft beauty and hard strips to halos, dispersion and dark luxury contours.
Auto-scales to your product. No rebuilding the rig every time.
◆ ───────── MADE TO BE ART-DIRECTED ───────── ◆
CPS handles the setup — you keep the control.
Move lights, change cameras, tweak animation, adjust materials, reposition depth of field or add your own effects.
It speeds up Blender. It doesn’t replace it.

✓ Cyclorama + 360 Studio
✓ Image Backgrounds
