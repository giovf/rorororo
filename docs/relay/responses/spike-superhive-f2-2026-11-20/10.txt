Never Fix Broken Game Engine Imports Again
Nothing kills creative momentum faster than exporting an asset from Blender, only to discover flipped normals, exploded rig weights, unapplied scale, or overlapping lightmap UVs inside Unreal, Unity, or Godot.
uGamePro is an all-in-one pre-flight scanner and auto-fixer built directly into Blender’s N-panel. Instead of hunting through menus or finding out what broke after you import into your engine, uGamePro catches hidden technical errors and fixes them safely in seconds.
What uGamePro Catches & Fixes Automatically
Every game engine has strict technical rules. uGamePro runs dozens of targeted checks across six core pipeline stages—giving you clear, plain-English explanations and one-click fixes:
Geometry Sanitizer: Detects and fixes flipped normals, non-manifold edges, loose vertices, zero-area faces, unapplied transforms/scale, and inconsistent object naming.
UV & Texel Density Inspector: Flags overlapping UVs, out-of-bounds UV islands, missing UV channels (like UE5 Lightmap UVs), and mismatched texel density across modular kits.
Texture & Material Validator: Checks for missing texture files, incorrect color spaces (Non-Color vs. sRGB), unpacked maps, unsupported shader nodes, and non-power-of-two resolutions.
Rigging & Weight Doctor: Catches unnormalized vertex weights, weights exceeding 4-bone engine limits, broken bone hierarchies, and invalid constraints before they deform in-engine.
Animation & NLA Cleaner: Identifies unbaked constraints, orphaned NLA strips, redundant keyframes, and shape key naming conflicts so root motion and animations play cleanly.
Smart Engine Export: Enforces clean collection hierarchies, naming conventions, and target-engine axis/scale rules for Unreal Engine 5 , Unity , and Godot .
Built for Solo Devs, Freelancers & Studios
Learn As You Work: Not sure why an issue breaks your engine import? Every warning includes a built-in How-To Guide and Platform Comparison explaining how Unreal, Unity, and Godot handle that exact rule.
Safe, Non-Destructive Control: Review issues per object with clean foldouts. Fix everything at once or apply targeted fixes one by one.
Modular or Full Suite: Buy only the single module you need, or grab the Full uGamePro Suite to automate your entire asset pipeline from mesh to export.
Compatibility & Support
Blender Versions: 4.5 – 5.2+ (Fully Blender Extension compatible)
Target Engines: Unreal Engine (UE4/UE5), Unity, Godot, and custom GLTF/FBX pipelines
