Every Geometry Nodes bake in one panel.
See what's baked, how many frames and how much space it takes. Bake everything, only the missingones, or one at a time. Bake nodes and Simulation zones, Blender 4.2 LTS to 5.2 LTS.
Blender 4.2 – 5.2 Bake nodes + Simulations Windows · macOS · Linux
Where did that bake go?
A big Geometry Nodes scene has bakes everywhere: a Bake node three groups deep, a simulation on another object,two more modifiers you forgot about. Which ones are baked? Which still recalculate every frame and slowthe viewport down? And why is your .blend suddenly 2 GB?
Blender shows each bake only inside its own modifier, and its Bake All button skips Bake nodes entirely.
Oh My Bake puts every bake of the scene in one list , with its status and size, and bakes them all with oneclick. Simulation zones and Bake nodes, at any depth.
What you get
The whole scene at a glance. Every object whose Geometry Nodes hold a Bake node (even nested inside groups) or a Simulation zone, with its status: baked or not, how many frames, how big.
Bake All. Really all. Bake nodes and simulations, every object, in one click. Or Bake Missing for only what isn't baked yet, Bake Selected , one object, or one bake.
Know what it weighs. The total size of all bakes, split into on disk and packed inside the .blend , plus the size of every single bake.
Pack and unpack (Blender 4.3+). Move bakes into the .blend to send one file, or out to a folder to keep the .blend small.
A faster viewport. The monitor icon of each Geometry Nodes modifier is right in the list. Hide Unbaked switches off every modifier that would recalculate, in one click.
Find it again. Each bake shows the group and node it lives in. Click an object or modifier to select it and open it in the Properties editor.
Stays responsive. Several bakes run one after another with progress in the status bar, and Stop ends it after the current bake.
Why I made it
I needed something better than what Blender gives you. In big Geometry Nodes scenes I kept losing track ofwhich bakes were done and how much space they took, and Blender's own Bake All doesn't even touch Bake nodes.So I made one panel that shows all of them and bakes all of them.
Before every release it is installed into a clean Blender 4.2, 4.5 and 5.2 and checked by an automated testsuite on Windows and Linux.
Perfect for
Motion design · scattering and environments · simulations · procedural assets · render-farm prep · anyonewhose .blend grew mysteriously large
