⭐ Spaceship Realism Toolkit — Product Description (Blender Market Ready)
Spaceship Realism Toolkit is a complete realism workflow for sci‑fi and hardsurface artists working in Blender.
Designed for speed, clarity, and professional results, this toolkit brings lighting, camera setup, rendering, weathering, and material enhancement together into one seamless system — all controlled through a powerful radial menu .
Compatible with Blender 3.0 up to 5.2 (tested) .
🚀 A Next‑Generation Radial Menu for Blender
The core of the toolkit is a custom GPU‑accelerated radial menu (Alt+Q) that gives you instant access to every major realism tool.
No more searching through panels or menus — everything you need is one gesture away.
The radial menu includes:
Lights – Key, Rim, Fill, Rim Color presets, Space World
Camera – Auto camera placement, DOF toggle, lens presets
Style – Cinematic, Standard, Product looks
Render – Quality presets, resolution presets, GPU/CPU toggle, glare, transparency
Weathering – 11 procedural generators with live sliders
Parts – Engine glow, glass, color presets
Tools – Live update, restore materials, restore parts
One‑Click Setup – Full realism setup in a single action
Smooth animations, hover highlights, dynamic sliders, and context‑sensitive options make the menu feel fast, modern, and intuitive.

Dust

Includes a Restore Materials option to revert to the original look at any time.
