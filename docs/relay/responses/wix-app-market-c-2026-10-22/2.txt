# Set Up Your Payout Account

Before you can publish a paid app, you must become a Wix partner and then set up a Tipalti account to receive payouts. This is a one-time setup at the account level, not the app level.

If your app's business model isn't free, a blocker appears during the [app review process](https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/launch-your-app/app-distribution/submit-your-first-app-version.md) requiring you to complete this setup. Until payout account setup is complete, you can't publish the app.

> **Note:** If you don't see the blocker, there's a chance you already have a payout account set up.

<blockquote class="important">

__Important:__
You need the **Manage Earnings** permission to complete this flow. Account owners have this access by default. To give a teammate access, see [Grant a teammate access to payouts](#grant-a-teammate-access-to-payouts).

</blockquote>

## Set up your payout account

To set up your payout account:

1. In your account dashboard, navigate to **Earnings** > **[Payouts](https://manage.wix.com/studio/revenues/payouts)**.
1. Click **Join the Partner Program**. The Wix Partners onboarding flow guides you through registering your company as a Wix partner.
2. Set up your Tipalti account to configure how you receive payouts.
3. Return to your app and continue the publish flow.

## Grant a teammate access to payouts

If you want a teammate to set up, view, or manage payouts, create a custom role with the **Manage Earnings** permission and assign it to them.

### Step 1 | Create a custom role

1. Go to **Team Management** and [create a custom role](https://manage.wix.com/studio/collaboration/create-role).
1. Enter a **Role Title** and optional **Description**.
1. Expand **Studio Workspace**, then select **Manage Earnings**.

    This permission allows teammates to set up a payout account, view payouts from templates, apps, and revenue share, and manage payout settings.

    ![Manage Earnings permission](https://wixmp-833713b177cebf373f611808.wixmp.com/images/2a58cd9798e69e23a7525192631aeb60.png)

1. Click **Save**.

### Step 2 | Invite the teammate and assign the role

1. Go to **Team Management** > **Invite Teammates**.
1. Enter the teammate's email address.
1. Under **Site access**, select **All sites and folders**.

    If you grant access to specific sites only, **Earnings** won't appear for the teammate.

    ![Site access for payout role](https://wixmp-833713b177cebf373f611808.wixmp.com/images/847130c64b0b4082972491e9ccc54bbf.png)

1. Under **Select roles**, select the custom role you created.
1. Click **Send Invite**.

The teammate receives an email invitation. Once they accept, they can set up and manage payouts in **Earnings** > **[Payouts](https://manage.wix.com/studio/revenues/payouts)**.
