Set up a limited company: step by step (/set-up-limited-company)
Search for a trade mark
Find details of trade marks to:
check if a similar trade mark to your brand already exists in the UK
find out who owns a trade mark
Search by trade mark number (https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk/ipo-tmcase/start) .
Search by owner (https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk/ipo-tmowner/start) .
Search by keyword, phrase or image (https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk/ipo-tmtext/start) .
Search by flag, emblem or hallmark (https://6ter.wipo.int/simple-search) using the Article 6ter Express Database.
For trade marks in Jersey and Guernsey, search the Jersey trade mark register (http://www.jgreffe-online.gov.je/trademarksdb/searchform.asp) or the Guernsey trade mark register (http://ipo.guernseyregistry.com/article/107508/View-the-Trade-Mark-Register) .
Check the trade marks journal
To find trade mark applications accepted in the last week, check:
Intellectual Property Office’s online trade marks journal (/check-trade-marks-journal) - for UK trade marks and international trade marks that include the UK
the Guernsey online trade marks journal (http://ipo.guernseyregistry.com/article/4929/Current-Journal%20-%20for%20Guernsey) - for Guernsey trade marks

Set up a limited company: step by step (/set-up-limited-company)
Step 1 :
Check if setting up a limited company is right for you
Check what a private limited company is (/limited-company-formation?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
How you set up your business depends on what sort of work you do. It can also affect the way you pay tax and get funding.
Most businesses register as a limited company or a sole trader.
Find out about the different ways to set up a business (/set-up-business?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
Find local advice and free support for your business on business.gov.uk (https://www.business.gov.uk)
Step 2 :
Choose your limited company type
Check whether you'll be limited by shares or guarantee (/limited-company-formation/limited-company-types?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
Step 3 :
Choose directors and a company secretary
You must appoint a director but you do not have to appoint a company secretary.
Find out what directors are responsible for (/running-a-limited-company?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
Check who can be a director or company secretary (/limited-company-formation/appoint-directors-and-company-secretaries?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
Step 4 :
Decide who the shareholders are
You need at least one shareholder, who can be a director.
Check how to choose shareholders for a company limited by shares (/limited-company-formation/shareholders?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
Find out how dividends from shares are taxed (/tax-on-dividends?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
or
Decide who your guarantors are
You need at least one guarantor who can be a director.
Check how to choose guarantors for a company limited by guarantee (/limited-company-formation/guarantors?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
and
Identify people with significant control (PSC) over your company
You need to identify people with significant control (PSC) - for example, anyone with more than 25% of the shares or voting rights.
Find out what counts as a PSC (/guidance/people-with-significant-control-pscs?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
You'll confirm these when you register your company with Companies House.
Step 5 :
Prepare documents agreeing how to run your company
You need to prepare documents agreeing how to run your company. These documents include the :
'memorandum of association'
'articles of association'
'statement of capital'
'statement of guarantee'
Find out how to create these documents (/limited-company-formation/documents?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
Step 6 :
Check what records you'll need to keep
Check the company and accounting records you must keep (/running-a-limited-company/company-and-accounting-records?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
Step 7 :
Choose a name
Check the rules for company names (/limited-company-formation/choose-company-name?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
Check if the name you want is available (https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company-name-availability)
You are currently viewing: Check existing trade marks (#content)
Step 8 :
Register your company with Companies House
You'll need to register an official address and choose a SIC code - this identifies what your company does.
Check the rules for registered office addresses and email addresses (/limited-company-formation/company-address?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
Check what your SIC code is (/government/publications/standard-industrial-classification-of-economic-activities-sic?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
Register your company with Companies House (/limited-company-formation/register-your-company?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
Step 9 :
Find out what to do after you've registered
There are some things you may need to do once you've registered a limited company.
Add Corporation Tax services to your business tax account (/limited-company-formation/add-corporation-tax-services-to-business-tax-account?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
Check the next steps for your limited company (/next-steps-for-your-business?step-by-step-nav=37e4c035-b25c-4289-b85c-c6d36d11a763)
