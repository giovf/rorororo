Phrases to Verify
terms
Required
#terms
Provide a list of words or phrases to check against the USPTO trademark database (e.g. ['star wars', 'disney', 'just do it']). Maximum 100 phrases.
Type: array
USPTO Classes Filter
classes
Optional
#classes
Comma-separated list of product classes to filter searches (e.g. '25' for clothing, '9' for electronics, '18' for leather/bags). Default is '25,9,18,20'.
Type: string
Default: 25,9,18,20
