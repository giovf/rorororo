Use Case Application
Financial
This provider offers daily-refreshed datasets on U.S. retail deposit rates across thousands of banks and credit unions, including product-level details on CDs, savings accounts, and money market accounts, as well as branch-level local competitor and market share insights. The data covers rate histories, promotional offers, peer benchmarks with regulatory performance metrics, and county-level market share statistics, supporting granular geographic analysis and institutional comparison. Financial professionals use this data to monitor deposit pricing changes, analyze competitive landscapes, evaluate local and national market trends, and optimize deposit strategy.
Software Company
This provider offers a private dataset of pre-generative-AI, human-developed production software spanning 22 commercial projects and 100+ code repositories across healthcare, education, logistics, retail, recruitment, analytics, and other industries. The dataset includes source code and extensive Git history, including commits, contributors, merge requests, and dated development activity across Node.js/React, React Native, Android/iOS, APIs, databases, and supporting services.
Political
This provider offers a comprehensive U.S. Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request database, aggregating, cleaning, and enriching over 250,000 request logs from more than 40 federal agencies, with high-frequency updates for near real-time insights. The dataset includes requester identities, request subjects and summaries, submission dates, normalized agency identifiers, and complete request texts—supporting advanced search and filtering across recent and historical government transparency activity. Clients leverage these datasets for predictive intelligence on regulatory focus, potential litigation, corporate or market movements, media activity, compliance trend analysis, and competition or opposition research.
Government
This provider offers structured government and public-sector data covering 75M+ records across 40+ countries, with deep coverage of China, Asia, and the Middle East. Data includes policy, regulatory, procurement, enforcement, and other official government activity, enriched with entity tagging, sentiment, importance, and policy-development signals and available via API.
Human Capital
This provider delivers global human capital and workforce data, including compensation benchmarks, organizational structure insights, role standards, skills intelligence, employee feedback, and talent analytics. Offerings cover research-backed benchmarking for leadership roles, pay equity, employee rewards, hiring fit, development potential, succession planning, and workforce planning, leveraging billions of data points from organizations in over 150 countries. Insights support talent recruitment, retention, compensation and benefits analysis, organizational transformation, and workforce strategy across a range of industries and markets.
Aviation
This provider delivers comprehensive, standardized aviation data powering global airline pricing, merchandising, offer management, and settlement processes. Datasets include fare and rule information, ancillary and branded product attributes, airline amenity content, marketplace activity, historical and real-time pricing, and tax and revenue accounting data—all accessible via APIs, automated feeds, or customizable subscriptions. The data enables fare management, dynamic offer creation, market analysis, competitive benchmarking, transparent product displays, and integration into airline retail, distribution, and analytics platforms worldwide.
Healthcare
This provider delivers a comprehensive database of pharmaceutical product availability and global manufacturing opportunities, including structured data on finished dosage forms, APIs, intermediates, and explicit details such as dosage, market coverage, and offer types for licensing, distribution, supply, and tech transfer. The platform also features a verified directory of contract manufacturers and suppliers, with detailed profiles covering regulatory compliance, technical capabilities, therapeutic focus, facility certifications, manufacturing scale, and sector specialization across human and veterinary products. Data is designed for partner discovery, outsourcing, and market intelligence initiatives, supporting pharmaceutical and biotechnology teams in sourcing, due diligence, and regulatory evaluation worldwide.
AI Training
This provider develops controlled, rights-aware datasets for robotics, embodied AI, vision-language-action models, and human activity understanding, offering video, image, audio, text, and preference data sourced from verified human contributors. Data types include household and workplace egocentric video, multi-camera footage, motion traces, speech recordings, photos, and annotated outputs such as image labels, masks, and audio transcriptions. The data is used for model training, evaluation, and quality review, supporting custom collection, annotation, and delivery for advanced applications in AI and robotics.
Insurance
This provider delivers comprehensive property and casualty insurance industry data, including in-depth directories of independent agencies, MGAs, wholesalers, and carriers, featuring attributes such as employee count, appointed carriers, agency management systems, premium volumes, revenue, targeted industries, and decision-maker contact information. Data offerings support sales and marketing efforts, prospect targeting, and competitive analysis by enabling segmentation by business type, geography, size, and specialty focus areas.
Luxury Goods
This provider delivers proprietary datasets focused on the global fashion, luxury, and beauty sectors, with coverage including consumer demand, brand positioning, cluster-level consumer attention, and emerging trends. Data offerings include monthly market signals, regional market comparisons across the US, France, Italy, and Korea, and portfolio diagnostics that map brand mix to demand clusters and regional alignment. These insights enable organizations to analyze market structure, track brand momentum, identify shifts in consumer interest, and benchmark market performance across geographies.
