Data Relationship Manager (DRMTM)

Track all interactions and relationships around data, internal and external, just like a CRM.

Get StartedWatch Demo
Benefits
Centralize all data relationships in one system
Make Nomad Data your system of record for everything data, with records for each vendor, dataset, and contact.
Keep track of every interaction around data
Record each conversation, evaluation, purchase, and internal note, ensuring retention of institutional knowledge over time.
Manage all data subscriptions in one place
Monitor the progress of data evaluations across teams to avoid duplicative spend and be alerted to pending renewals as well as opt-out dates.
AI-powered Search
Take advantage of generative AI search against all your firm’s data-related activity and documentation. Our chatbot-style search is trained on nearly a decade of connections between use cases and data products and augments your firm's data knowledge with the dynamically-updated profiles of over
5,600
data providers in our Connect network.

‍Prefer to guide your knowledge workers through their data journey? Use our built-in ticketing system to capture, respond to, and log requests all in one place.

Automate offline processes with Workflows

Upload all your data paperwork and we'll tell you where you can save money. Our system extracts pricing details from your contracts automatically so you don’t have to enter your data purchases manually. From there we’ll let you know where you may be able to save money by eliminating duplicative data spend, and since we work with the largest collection of data providers in one place (over
5,600
and growing) we can suggest more cost-effective substitutes.
Define any process as a series of automated tasks, assigning specific responsibilities to relevant team members, both internal and external. Update fields, contact stakeholders, and assign tasks all within your DRM, ensuring critical workflows are completed and fully documented. Onboard new vendors, run diligence, or execute paperwork with ease.

Connect with hundreds of third-party systems

Keep all your data knowledge in sync through easy integrations with hundreds of third-party systems from CRMs, to procurement platforms, to cloud databases. Choose from our library of pre-built connections or provide API documentation and we can build a custom low-code connection in short order.

Our system extracts pricing details from your contracts automatically so you don’t have to enter your data purchases manually. From there we’ll let you know where you may be able to save money by eliminating duplicative data spend, and since we work with the largest collection of data providers in one place (over 5,000 and growing) we can suggest more cost-effective substitutes.

Auto-populate your DRM

Upload all your data contracts and our system extracts relevant information (e.g. pricing, term, contacts, etc.) to populate your DRM with Data Provider, Dataset, Contact, and License records. Use our email plugins or manually BCC a custom address and our system automatically creates an Activity in your DRM.

Auto-renew warning

Never miss an opt-out date. Upload all your data contracts or manually enter your data purchases to be notified of every upcoming contract renewal and auto-renew opt-out notice period.
