Payment thresholds, methods, and time frames
Feedback
Summarize this article for me
In this article
The estimated time to transfer payments, and Microsoft Marketplace storefronts you can receive payments from, can vary depending on your country/region (#payment-methods-in-countriesregions) and payout account type. This article outlines the payment methods supported in each country/region.
For any given country and region, we pay using ACH/SEPA or wire transfer. Some countries and regions also support PayPal as a payment method.
Note
For more information about when Microsoft issues payouts for Microsoft Marketplace after a transaction has occurred, see Payout policy details (../payout-policy-details) . To see your transaction history, see Earnings (../earnings/earnings-overview) .
Number of days for payments to reach payout account
If the Marketplace purchase was made with an Enterprise agreement (SalesChannel is Enterprise in Revenue report), Microsoft issues any payout payments due for a given month by the 15th of that month.
For Microsoft Cloud Agreement (MCA) transactions, refer to the Payout Schedules and Processes (/en-us/partner-center/marketplace-offers/payout-policy-details) page for specific guidance on when payments are expected to be sent, based on the type of MCA transaction.
In both EA and MCA scenarios, note that payments might not reach your account immediately. The timing of receipt depends on the payment method associated with your account, as follows.
Payment method
Approximate days to reach account
PayPal
One business day
ACH/SEPA
Two to three business days
Wire transfer
Seven to 10 business days
Note
Due to changes in foreign exchange rates, the exact sales amount might vary slightly from one currency to another. Exchange rates are calculated monthly. Based on when your transaction took place, the appropriate exchange rate is applied. The exchange rate and the date rate for which it was in force are indicated on your payout report in the columns exchangeRate and exchangeRateDate, respectively.
