About this Skill
When to use it
Use this Skill when someone asks you to map the leadership of a specific account, such as building an exec-team chart, showing who reports to whom, or flagging which leaders are new in their role. It returns a self-contained org chart page with labeled, editable reporting lines and a tray for people it cannot place. Avoid using it for finding one person's email, building prospect lists across many companies, scoring an account list, or writing to a CRM or sequence.
What it delivers
You get one interactive org chart page that groups C-Suite and VP leaders into function lanes, plus a by-level view and a Needs review tray for anyone with no evidence of a manager. The page labels every reporting line as inferred with a confidence level, and you can drag tiles to correct lines yourself; it never states a line as confirmed fact.

Inputs & outputs
What someone provides before the Skill runs, and the reviewable artifacts it returns.
Inputs
01 Target account
Company name or website domain to build the chart for
confirmed against Clay's company records before any people search runs
02 Branding choice
Choose yes or no to style the page in your own brand
if yes, supply your company website so its colors, fonts and logo can be read
03 Build settings
How many leader records to pull on the first build (default 30, capped at 50)
how far back the news section should search (default 3 months)
04 Workspace and lookup setup
Confirm which Clay workspace runs the search
pick a function from your workspace for mobile-number lookups, or skip it for email lookup only
05 the requested amount
re-run the Step 5 query with a limit of current total plus the requested amount, and deduping drops
the people already charted. Default to 20 more if no number is given, and say it uses Clay credits
before running it.
Outputs
01 Build summary reply A short written summary of leaders found, exclusions, workspace searched, and how many people still need review, so you know the chart is ready and what it left out.
02 Leadership coverage summary A quick count strip showing how many leaders, VP versus C-Suite, contacts found, people needing review, and lines you've manually set.
03 Org chart tiles The visual chart itself, with each leader's title, function lane, and reporting line plus the reason and confidence behind that line, ready for you to review or drag into place.
04 Recent news cards Dated, linked news items naming the charted leaders, so you can see what's currently happening with the team before reaching out.

How the Skill runs
Follow the steps in order. Each row includes the full instruction.
Step Workflow Type
01
Check Clay connection and tools
Confirms the Clay search tools and workspace connection work, names which workspace it will search, and stops if anything required is missing.
Step
02
Ask which company
Asks you to name the target company or, better, its website domain if you haven't already, and waits for your answer.
Step
03
Confirm spend and branding
Explains how many leaders it will search for and that the search uses credits, asks whether to apply your brand, and the run stops and waits for your answer before any search happens.
Step
04
Extract seller's brand
Reads your company website's colors, fonts, and logo to style the chart, only when you chose branding, and falls back to the default look if the site can't be read.
Step
05
Resolve the target company
Matches the name or domain to one Clay company record, and the run stops and waits for you to pick when several plausible companies match.
Step
06
Search current leaders
Searches Clay for current C-Suite and VP leaders at the resolved company and checks your workspace for a function that can look up mobile numbers.
Agent
07
Classify leaders and infer lines
Filters out non-qualifying titles, groups remaining leaders by level and function, and infers each reporting line from title seniority with a stated confidence.
Step
08
Find recent leader news
Searches the web for recent, dated news mentioning the charted leaders and keeps only verified, linked items inside your chosen news window.
Agent
09
Build and deliver the page
Renders the interactive chart, publishes it privately when possible, and replies with counts, exclusions, and a reminder that titles come from Clay while reporting lines are inferences, not confirmed facts.
Step
10
Add more people later
Pulls additional leaders into an existing chart, merges them with prior data, refreshes the news section, and republishes to the same page link.
