About this Skill
When to use it
Use this Skill when you want to know where each target account sits in its own security compliance cycle, using only trust pages, GRC job postings, and certification announcements the account publishes itself. It gives you a dated, evidence-backed buying-window stage per framework instead of a stale certification headline. Avoid using it for general signal monitoring, job-posting trend counts, ICP scoring, tech-stack detection, or event-based account sourcing.
What it delivers
You get a per-account, per-framework compliance stage digest with the date and quoted evidence behind each placement, plus a coverage and spend summary. Use it to decide which accounts to approach now versus nurture, while treating unplaced accounts as missing evidence rather than a negative signal.

How someone would prompt this
We sell a compliance automation platform. Check these 200 Series A and B software companies and tell me which are preparing for or currently inside a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 audit, so I know who to prioritize for outreach this quarter.
Inputs & outputs
What someone provides before the Skill runs, and the reviewable artifacts it returns.
Inputs
01 Target accounts & frameworks
A list of target company domains to place
the compliance frameworks you sell into (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.)
02 Sales mapping & role terms
Which stages mean reach out, nurture, or skip for your offer
the job titles that count as compliance hiring
03 Timing windows
How many days a job posting still counts
how many months a certification counts as recent
04 Budget & delivery
The maximum Clay credits this run may spend
where results go, conversation or a named CSV path
Outputs
01 Compliance stage digest A table listing each account, framework, stage, dating evidence, quoted proof line, and your next-move action, so you know which accounts to prioritize now.
02 Coverage and spend line A short summary showing how many accounts it placed, flagged unreadable, or found no evidence for, plus the credits the run actually spent, so you can confirm coverage and cost.

ledgerline.example	SOC 2	preparing	job posting, 19 Aug 2026 (48 days old, window 90)	"GRC Analyst: you will own our first SOC 2 Type II audit"	reach out now
quillstack.example	SOC 2	in-observation	trust page, read 6 Oct 2026	"Type I report available on request. Type II observation period underway, report expected Q1 2027"	nurture, continuous monitoring angle
harborgrid.example	ISO 27001	recently-certified	own announcement, 2 Jun 2026 (4 months old, window 12)	"Harborgrid is now ISO 27001 certified", on the company's own blog	skip
tallyforge.example	SOC 2	certified-undated	nothing dated it	a SOC 2 badge on its security page, no report date beside it, no announcement found in 12 months	ask about audit timing in discovery; do not lead with it
corvel.example	none	unreadable	nothing	homepage timed out twice; every trust page candidate failed to connect	check by hand, or drop
mintpaper.example	SOC 2	no-evidence	all sources read, 6 Oct 2026	no trust page; no compliance postings in 90 days; no announcement in 12 months	nothing visible yet
The stages are the seven in Step 2 and no others. "Unreadable" means the skill could not look;
"no-evidence" means it looked and found nothing. The next move column is copied from the installer's
own mapping, not decided by the skill.

This lets you spot which accounts deserve a rep's attention right now.
