Sorry, there is a problem with the service
The Planning Data Platform (https://www.planning.data.gov.uk/) has been unavailable since Friday 6 November 2026 because of a technical issue.
Check this page for further updates.
Updates
11 November 2026
We expect to restore public access to the Planning Data Platform including the Planning Data API and navigation to Extract (https://extract.ai.gov.uk/) to minimise continued disruption as soon as possible.
However, some services including Check and Provide (https://provide.planning.data.gov.uk/) and Submit a site for a local plan (https://mhclgdigital.blog.gov.uk/2026/10/06/a-faster-start-to-local-plan-making-introducing-the-submit-a-site-for-a-local-plan-tool/) will take longer to reappear.
9 November 2026
The disruption may impact MHCLG services including:
Planning Data Platform (https://www.planning.data.gov.uk/)
Planning Data API, and any third party software services using this data
Check and Provide (https://provide.planning.data.gov.uk/)
Planning Data Design (https://design.planning.data.gov.uk/)
Submit a site for a local plan (https://mhclgdigital.blog.gov.uk/2026/10/06/a-faster-start-to-local-plan-making-introducing-the-submit-a-site-for-a-local-plan-tool/)
Users of Extract (https://extract.ai.gov.uk/) can still access the tool directly.
MHCLG’s Digital Planning Programme are working hard to restore services as soon as possible.
6 November 2026
Planning Data Platform was taken offline.
