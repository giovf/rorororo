Popular questions
Find answers to your questions right here, and don't hesitate to Contact us (/help/contact-us/?option=Endole+App+Enquiry) if you couldn't find what you're looking for.
What do Endole credit checks cover?
Endole Credit Checks offer real-time insight into a company's Credit Risk. All Credit Checks provide current and previous Credit Scores & Limits, summarised and detailed 6 years' County Court Judgement (CCJ) insights - including complete 6 years' Gazette and Charge history.
Do subscription allowances roll over?
Subscription allowances do not roll over and are reset at the date of renewal. For subscriptions paid annually, the entire year's allowances is made available from the moment of signing up.
How many users can access this software?
There are no restrictions on the number of users. However, for security reasons, a subscription can only be utilised on one device at any one time. If you require multiple users, please contact us for a bespoke solution.
Will pricing change on renewal?
Subscriptions are billed recurringly, either monthly or annually, until cancellation. On every billing date, you will receive a notification from us with the details of your subscription including how to access your VAT invoices. Prices will never change on renewal.
Can I subscribe to a bespoke subscription?
We understand the allowances on our subscriptions will not cater to all needs. If you're looking for more subscriptions or a custom subscription to match your requirements, please feel free to call us on 0121 285 6781 or send us an email and a member of our versatile sales team will be more than happy to help.
What happens if I go over my subscription limits?
We'll notify you before you get to the end of your allowance. Additional purchases would be charged at the normal pay as you go price. If your current allowance doesn't meet your needs, you can always contact us to upgrade your account.
Are my details confidential?
We take our users' confidentiality very seriously. All purchases on Endole are anonymous, this means that we do not reveal any information about any user using Endole to a third party without the consent of the user or a legal obligation.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your subscription at any time from within your Endole Account. Alternatively, you can let us know through Live Chat or Email and a member of our support team will cancel your Endole subscription on your behalf.
How soon can I access my Business Credit Report?
We update our UK data continuously, meaning all information is up-to-the-minute. Once purchased, you'll have instant access to your business check.
