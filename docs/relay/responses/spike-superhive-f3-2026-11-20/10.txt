Velocity Blur makes fast-moving and spinning objects look more believable in Blender. It is designed for wheels, propellers, rotors and mechanical parts where static-looking rotation can make a render feel unnatural.
With a simple workflow for linear motion, spin motion, radial motion and combined linear + spin motion, you can set up convincing motion blur quickly and keep control over shutter, samples and rotation angle.
Velocity Blur is especially useful for vehicle, aircraft and machinery renders where you want more dynamic, natural-looking motion without rebuilding the setup manually every time.
Main Features
Motion blur for wheels, propellers, rotors and other spinning objects
Linear motion
Spin motion
Radial motion
Combined linear + spin motion
Quick presets for common use cases
Vehicle + Wheels setup
Propeller and aircraft-related setup tools
Adjustable shutter, samples and motion settings
Apply and render workflow from the add-on panel
Blender 4.5+ support
Made For
Cars and other vehicles
Wheels
Aircraft and propellers
Rotors
Machinery
Gears and shafts
Other fast-spinning mechanical parts
Velocity Blur is made for artists who want a faster way to add believable motion to renders without building the setup manually every time.
