Stack Motion Pro
Stack Motion Pro — The Modular Animation Toolkit for Blender
Tired of spending hours setting tedious keyframes, wrestling with graph editor curves, and rebuilding the same animation setups from scratch? Stack Motion Pro includes 741 total animations : Once you have purchased all the animation packs.
Stack Motion Pro transforms Blender into a rapid motion design studio. Apply, layer, and orchestrate polished 3D animation presets onto any object or text with a single click. Every animation is 100% non-destructive, customizable, and stackable — allowing you to combine multiple effects seamlessly to create unique, complex choreography in seconds.
Key Features & Selling Points:
⚡ One-Click Animation Presets:
Instantly animate 3D objects, models, cameras, and character-by-character text titles without touching the timeline.
🥞 Layered & Stackable Architecture:
Stack multiple animations on the same object (e.g., combine Scale Pop, Fly In, and a secondary Wobble). Tweak, mute, or remove individual layers at any time without breaking original transforms.
✍️ Dynamic 3D Typography:
Generate editable, animated 3D text with character-level timing, spring bounces, kinetic hops, reveals, and broadcast-ready typography.
🎯 Selection-Aware Multi-Object Choreography:
Animate entire arrays, grids, circular rings, or product assemblies with automatic spatial sorting, radial ripples, and staggered cascades.
📦 Modular Expansion Ecosystem:
Expand your creative library across 13 specialised packages — including Cinematic Motion, Product Showcase, Kinetic Physics, Broadcast Titles, Camera Motion, and Organic Motion.
🔄 Clean & Non-Destructive:
Full Blender NLA integration under the hood. Remove or adjust any effect and your models snap back to their exact original positions and transforms.
🛡️ Blender 3.6 LTS – 5.x+ Ready:
Engineered for performance and full compatibility with modern Blender versions.

You can pause and play any animation applied to objects to view the stacked effect, you can also click edit to adjust the timeline and graph to tweak the animation by hand. If you wanted to remove an applied animation you can just click delete and all other animations remain untouched.
