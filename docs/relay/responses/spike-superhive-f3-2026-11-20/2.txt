Designing a moving sculpture should not mean guessing whether the parts actually connect.
Entropy Designer gives Blender makers one guided workflow for editable gear trains, radial multi-output systems, timing drives, planetary joints, curve-authored links, and printable housings.
Start from a kinetic-sculpture, robot-joint, or direct-drive scenario; tune practical dimensions; attach your own form; then check alignment, connections, and motion before animation or export.
Version 2.3 adds integer timing-belt selection, solved center distance, wrap, and tooth-engagement validation.
The turbine and generator remain optional modules. Build the mechanism, understand the compromises, and prototype with evidence—not a decorative mock-up.
