VERTEXMODELER / ONECLICK ASSET
One click.
Asset + preview.
Save your selected asset to your Blender library and automatically generate its transparent preview. All in one export.
SELECT IT. SAVE IT. USE IT AGAIN.
From your scene to your library.
Keep the chair, lamp or complete set you want to reuse. Save it directly from the project you are working on.
01 / AUTOMATIC PREVIEWS
The thumbnail comes with it.
Automatic framing and a transparent background give your asset a clear preview, without a separate thumbnail workflow.
02 / SMART ASSET TYPES
One object. Or the whole set.
One selected object suggests Object. Multiple objects suggest Collection. Change the choice whenever you need, or export a material separately.
03 / YOUR PREFERRED VIEW
Show its best angle.
Choose Front Left, Front or Front Right, each with a gentle downward view. Adjust orientation and margins; set preview quality in Preferences.
04 / READY TO REUSE
Build a library as you work.
Save to an existing catalog or create a new one. Find and reuse your saved work through Blender’s Asset Browser in future projects.
A SIMPLE START
Register a writable local asset library once. Select your objects in Object Mode, open OneClick Asset: Export Asset , check the destination and confirm. The asset and preview are saved together.
Export uses copies, preserving your source objects. Preview rendering takes time; faster quality settings reduce detail.
Your next project starts here.
Keep the work worth keeping. Let OneClick Asset handle the asset and its preview.
WINDOWS x64 / BLENDER 4.0–5.2
VertexModeler · OneClick Asset 1.0.2
Requires a writable local asset library. Exported assets use the file format of your Blender version. This is an asset creation tool; demonstration models are not included.
