About

🚀 Hack2Build: Payments x402 edition

Join us for an incredible 2.5 weeks long hackathon where developers, product designers, and entrepreneurs come together to build the next generation of AI+Blockchain interactions using x402 and ERC8004 on Avalanche.

🎯 What We're Looking For:

  • Innovation: Breakthrough ideas that push the boundaries of what's possible
  • Technical Excellence: Well-architected, secure, and scalable solutions
  • User Experience: Applications that are intuitive and accessible to everyone
  • Real-World Impact: Solutions that solve actual problems and create value

🏆 Prize Pool: $25,000 + Founder Support

  • $25,000 to be distributed among the most promising teams
  • Interview with the InfraBuidl(AI) grant committee
  • Business and Technical Mentorships
  • Partners: Additional incentives from partners

✉️ Submission:

Review the deliverable details

  • 1st Deliverable - Prototype. (Dec 1 - 11:59 pm ET)
  • 2nd Deliverable - MVP. (Dec 8 - 11:59 pm ET)
  • Live Pitch. (Dec 12)

🤝 Community & Support

Our team of mentors, technical experts, and community members will be available throughout the hackathon to help you succeed. Don't hesitate to reach out for guidance, technical support, or just to chat about your ideas!

Ready to build the future? Let's make it happen together! 🚀
