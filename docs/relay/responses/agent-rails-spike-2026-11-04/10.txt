Who pays what
Three lanes, no surprises
Buyers pay nothing
$0
Agents buying from Nevermined merchants
Agents pay the listed price, whether by delegated card, credits, or checkout. No buyer-side fee, ever.
Buyers pay
2%
Buying external services
Managed access to external services: one integration, one wallet, spend mandates enforced, consolidated accounting. The fee rides on top of the buyer's total and the merchant receives 100% of its quoted price. The same 2% on every rail, with no tiers and no minimums. Browsing the catalog is free, and always will be. How the subscription works (https://nevermined.ai/docs/products/catalog/router/subscription)
Merchants pay
1-2%
Selling with Nevermined
1% on stablecoin rails, 2% on card rails, taken from settlement. Payment-processing costs pass through at cost. Buyers pay nothing on top when they buy from you: one fee per flow, never both sides.
This page is what Nevermined costs you. What your agents charge is up to you: per call, per token, per outcome, or cost plus margin. Publish any model and we meter, enforce, and settle it.
