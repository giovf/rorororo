Agentic commerce
Build agents that pay, or sell products, services, and APIs to agents.

Set up agent
Agentic commerce lets AI agents transact for the people they serve. Buyers can browse products, get recommendations, and complete purchases without leaving the conversation. Agents can pay with a customer-controlled wallet and retrieve financial insights from the accounts customers choose to share.
Stripe provides integrations for agent builders and sellers. If you build agents, you can add payments and commerce to them. If you sell products, services, or APIs, you can make them available to agents. Availability, launch stage, and supported countries vary by integration. See each integration’s guide for details.
Build an agent
You can give your agent a wallet to pay online, embed commerce into your AI interface, or complete payments in a browser with WebMCP.
Link Agent Wallet Let your agent pay online and access your customer’s financial data. (/agentic-commerce/agents/link-agent-wallet)
Embed commerce into your AI interface Let your AI interface browse seller catalogs, collect payment information, and complete purchases for buyers. (/agentic-commerce/agents/embedded)
Use WebMCP to complete Stripe payments in a browser Build browser agents that discover and use structured tools in Stripe payment UIs. (/agentic-commerce/agents/webmcp)
Sell to agents
You can sell products by letting Stripe manage checkout or building your own, accept service bookings and payments, or charge for tool calls and HTTP requests with machine payments.
Retail and e-commerce Sell products through agents. Let Stripe manage checkout, or build your own. (/agentic-commerce/sellers/use-cases/retail)
Service bookings Let agents find, book, and pay for your services. (/agentic-commerce/sellers/use-cases/booking)
Tool calls and HTTP requests Charge for tool calls and HTTP requests with machine payments, such as the Machine Payments Protocol (MPP) and x402. (/payments/machine)
