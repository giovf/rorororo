How can I open a store?
Mariel Crespo Garcia
8 months ago
Updated
Creative Fabrica is a large platform with premium designers from all over the world. Below you can learn about the process of applying to open a store on Creative Fabrica.
What are the steps to create a store?
✅Tip - Add your account email
address to your portfolio or store pages before you submit your application.
We use this information to validate ownership of the store/portfolio read here (https://help.creativefabrica.com/hc/en-us/articles/4411193014674-Adding-your-email-or-account-ID-to-your-portfolio) .
for more details.
Before applying to open a store you must have a Creative Fabrica account. If you don't have an account yet, you can create one here. (https://www.creativefabrica.com/my-account/)
On your Creative Fabrica account page (https://www.creativefabrica.com/my-account/) find the sell your designs on Creative Fabrica section and click Open a store .
This will open a form where you will be asked to complete a form using the following information
Your full name
The name of your store
Links to any existing stores
Links to your portfolio
Your reason for wanting to be a designer at Creative Fabrica
We highly recommend that your portfolio showcases your designs, their popularity and portfolio history so that we are able to evaluate if your store is a good fit for our marketplace. Below are some examples of portfolios or stores that you can link to:
Portfolio Examples
Behance
Etsy
Creative Market
Instagram
Design Bundles
Adobe Stock
Dribble
Cults3d
What do look for in a store application?
✅Tip - Make sure your account email
address or the last two digits of your Creative Fabrica account ID appear on
your portfolio or existing store pages. This way we can be sure that they belong
to you, and increase the chance your application will be approved quickly.
When evaluating your application we look for high quality designs that are suitable for our marketplace and audience and e vidence that the portfolios or existing stores belong to the person making the application.
What happens if my request is rejected?
If your request is rejected, you can submit a new store request. The next steps depend on the reason your request was rejected.
My store request was rejected because you weren't able to verify my existing store/portfolio
If we weren't able to verify that you are the owner of a store or portfolio you submitted in your application we will reject your request, and ask you to add your email or account ID to your store/profile.
Add the information to your store or portfolio, then reapply right away and we will check your new application.
My request was rejected for quality reasons
If your store is rejected due to quality reasons or an insufficient number of designs, you will only be able to reapply in 2 months.
Please note that your request is automatically reviewed. If you have any questions about your application, feel free to reach out to us at designers@creativefabrica.com (mailto:designers@creativefabrica.com) .
