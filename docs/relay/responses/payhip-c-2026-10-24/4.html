Refund Request

If you would like to request a refund for a product you purchased from a Payhip seller, you’ll need to contact the seller directly. In this article, we'll show you how to get in touch with the seller and what to expect during the process.

  • Troubleshooting before requesting a refund
  • How to contact the seller
  • Expectations on response time
  • Frequently Asked Questions

Payhip does not hold funds and cannot issue refunds directly.

Troubleshooting before requesting a refund

Before requesting a refund, you may want to:

  • Try downloading the product again
  • Use a different browser or device
  • Double-check any instructions provided by the seller

In many cases, issues can be resolved without needing a refund.

Each seller sets and manages their own refund policy. Some sellers may offer refunds, while others may not, especially if the product has already been downloaded or accessed.

How to contact the seller

You can contact the seller in the following ways:

  • Via email: Check your receipt email and use the seller’s email address listed at the bottom.
  • Through their store: Visit the seller’s store and look for a contact page (usually in the header or footer).

For more information, please visit this article: How to Contact the Seller.

Expectations on response time

Many sellers on Payhip are individual creators or small businesses, so response times may vary. Most sellers typically reply within 24 to 72 hours. We recommend allowing some time before following up.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if the product description was misleading?

If you feel the product description didn't accurately reflect what you received, reach out to the seller and explain the situation. Depending on their refund policy, they may be able to clarify things or offer a refund. Most sellers are willing to work towards a resolution.

What if I purchased the wrong product by mistake?

If you've accidentally purchased the wrong product, contact the seller as soon as possible. Whether a refund can be issued will depend on their refund policy, so it's worth reaching out promptly.

What if I was charged twice?

If you've been charged more than once for the same order, get in touch with the seller and include both receipts so they can review the transactions on their end. If you're unable to reach the seller or the issue isn't resolved, you can also raise it directly with your bank or payment provider.

Can I report a seller?

If you believe a seller is acting in bad faith, misrepresenting their products, or violating Payhip's policies, please get in touch with our support team at contact@payhip.com. Include as much detail as you can about the situation, and we'll review it and take the appropriate steps from there.
