Pricing

One price per record. No subscription.

You pay once for a company record and you get the KYB report plus the underlying official registry filings together. The price depends only on what the register itself charges us, so it varies by jurisdiction.

155 jurisdictions are on sale today. See the full coverage list, which names the band and the source register for each one.

What is included in the price

  • The KYB report: company data, controlling parties, and shareholders, with beneficial ownership derived by tracing the ownership chain.
  • The standard set of official registry documents for that jurisdiction.
  • Delivery to your account, and a downloadable invoice.

What is not charged for

  • Company search is free, up to a daily limit.
  • There is no subscription, no minimum, and no platform fee.

Prices are in US dollars and are charged once, at purchase. The Terms of Service govern every order.
