Downloading open data

Attention! From November 1, 2024, open data files do not contain personal identification number information due to data protection reasons.

Public data of companies, non-profit organizations, foundations, national and municipal agencies can be downloaded in machine-readable format (JSON and XML). Additionally, for certain data sets, some data is available as a CSV file, such as general data.

Downloading open data requires acceptance of the license terms.
There are a total of eight downloadable Business Register data sets, which are updated once a day, and with open data it is important to point out that the files only contain public data of the legal entities currently in Business Register (only with statuses: entered into the register, in liquidation, in bankruptcy).

Basic data

Basic data
Basic data
Basic data

Dataset includes the basic data of legal persons:

  • Name of the legal person
  • Registry code
  • Legal form and subtype
  • VAT number
  • Current status
  • Date of first entry
  • Address

Formats:

  • XML
  • CSV

General data

General data
General data
General data

General data contains all the main information about the company, in addition to Basic data the file also includes:

  • Internal ID of the legal person
  • Information on accounting obligation
  • Capital
  • Shares without nominal value
  • Legal succession
  • List of annual reports
  • List of articles of association
  • Date of first entry in Enterprise register
  • Contacts
  • Areas of activity
  • List of registry cards
  • Immovables entered to the registry card (only Building Associations have this information)

Formats:

  • XML
  • JSON

Registry cards

Registry cards
Registry cards

Registry card dataset returns a list of a companies registry cards.

  • Name
  • Registry code
  • Entry number
  • Date of entry
  • Entry type

Formats:

  • XML
  • JSON

Persons on registry card

Persons on registry card
Persons on registry card

This dataset returns a list of persons entered on the companies registry card.

  • Name of the legal entity
  • Registry code
  • Role of the person
  • Name of the person
  • Entry date and end date
  • Specifications in the right of representation as text

Formats:

  • XML
  • JSON

Persons not on registry card

Persons not on registry card
Persons not on registry card

This dataset returns a list of persons outside of the registry card

Formats:

  • XML
  • JSON

Shareholders

Shareholders
Shareholders

The open data set of shareholders includes the data of persons in the register of shareholders:

  • Registry code of the company
  • Company name
  • Person ID
  • The role of the person
  • Person's name
  • Shareholders contribution and participation
  • Contribution type
  • Start and end date
  • Special representation rights as text

File formats:

  • XML
  • JSON

Beneficial owners

Beneficial owners
Beneficial owners

Set of data that returns list of current beneficial owners.

  • Name of the legal entity
  • Registry code
  • Person ID
  • Name of the beneficial owner;
  • Country of residence of the beneficial owner;
  • The manner in which the verification of the identity of the beneficial owner is carried out.

Formats:

  • XML
  • JSON

Commercial pledge

Commercial pledge
Commercial pledge

Commercial pledge data set contains all the current commercial pledge information associated with the company.

  • Company name
  • Registry code
  • Commercial pledge number
  • Commercial pledge amount
  • Commercial pledge status
  • Commercial pledge entry date
  • The name and code of the pledgee
  • Remarks

Formats:

  • XML
  • JSON

Rulings

Rulings
Rulings

A list of all the rulings assigned to the legal person.

  • Name of the legal person
  • Registry code
  • Ruling number
  • Date of ruling
  • Additional deadline
  • Type or ruling
  • Status of ruling
  • Ruling force status
  • Date of entry into force
  • Entry type

Formats:

  • XML
  • JSON

Annual reports data

1. General information of reports (until 30.09.2026)
2. Sales revenue distribution by EMTAK (until 30.09.2026)
3. Sales revenue by geographical region (until 30.09.2026)
4.1 2019 reports: key indicators (until 30.09.2026)
4.1 2020 reports: key indicators (until 30.09.2026)
4.1 2021 reports: key indicators (until 30.09.2026)
4.1 2022 reports: key indicators (until 30.09.2026)
4.1 2023 reports: key indicators (until 30.09.2026)
4.1 2024 reports: key indicators (until 30.09.2026)
4.1 2025 reports: key indicators (until 30.09.2026)

Datasets contain general statistics of the submitted annual reports for the three previous financial years and include key indicators and data. (To get whole reports, the services of e-Business Register should be used). Data is displayed for each main period of the report (data for the reference period of the reports is not included in files)

Files are initially updated once a month

The data is presented in four types CSV documents, connected to each other based on the report_id attribute, where:

1) General information describing the submission of reports, which contains a list of the submitted reports with its important information (company name, registry code, legal type, status, when the report is submitted, for which period, which company category, whether audited and if so, if known, also the type of decision and identification(s) of possible modification etc, auditor company.

NB! There is no precise content about the audit result if the report is submitted in PDF format only

2) Distribution of non-consolidated sales revenue based on EMTAK (only for commercial entities)

3) Distribution of non-consolidated sales revenue by geographical regions (only for legal entities that have filled out the corresponding note when filling in sales revenue, there is no data for consolidated reports as non-consolidated note cannot be submitted)

4) Key indicators from the non-consolidated (even if a consolidated report is submitted) reports:
Cash and cash equivalents, Total current assets, Total non-current assets, Assets, Current liabilities, Non-current liabilities, Equity, Revenue (for companies), Total revenue (non commercial entities), Employee expense, Depreciation and impairment loss (reversal), Operating profit (loss), Annual period profit (loss), Average number of employees in full time equivalent units, Retained Earnings Loss, Profit (loss) before tax.

Attention

If open data contains personal data, the re-user of open data is obliged to comply with the terms and conditions of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including compliance with the principles of personal data processing and the requirements for the existence of a legal basis, in the further processing of personal data.

In certain cases, it is possible for companies and organizations to reuse public information, that is, to use it for some other commercial or non-commercial purpose than the original purpose of collecting the information. In several data sets under our responsibility, personal data is made public on the basis of the law, for example in the Land Register, Ametlikud Teadaanded (an official online publication of the Republic of Estonia), the court decisions section of the Riigi Teataja (an official online publication of the Republic of Estonia), the Business Register.

At the same time, disclosing the data does not mean that anything can be done with the data. Data, the reuse of which we have not limited (i.e. open data) and which may be used for other commercial or non-commercial purposes than the original purpose of collecting the information are published at the address https://www.rik.ee/en/open-data

Regarding other publicly accessible information and data under our responsibility, we have limited their reuse and have found that providing information and data for general use significantly damages the privacy of natural persons entered in the registers. Such data may not be reused for any commercial or non-commercial purpose.

In the event that someone (company, organization) uses (discloses, processes) your personal data from the data sets under our responsibility and the data is not part of open data, you can always contact the persons reusing personal data and exercise all rights related to your personal data, including the right to object to the processing of your personal data and to request the deletion of the personal data for which there is no legal basis according to Article 6 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If you find that your rights and freedoms are being violated during the processing of personal data, you have the right to appeal to the Data Protection Inspectorate or to an administrative court
