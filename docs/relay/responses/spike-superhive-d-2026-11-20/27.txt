Labcraft fills a science lab inside Blender: 161 ready designs across 22 kinds of lab equipment, from a 250 ml Griffin beaker with a printed scale to a Bunsen burner with a roaring blue flame, a binocular microscope, an analytical balance behind its glass draft shield, a fume hood or a full epoxy-top island bench with gas taps and a sink. Every item is built at its real size from catalog dimensions, and the glassware is accurate down to the scale: graduation marks and the liquid level sit at their true volumes, so 100 ml in a cylinder is really 100 ml.
Pick a design from the picture gallery, then change anything with one click: the vessel shape, capacity, glass (clear, amber, PMP or PP plastic), the liquid (clear, cloudy or glowing) and how full it is, closures, labels and colors, and the condition from new to grimy. Surprise Me makes a random item or a new look for the selected one.

Glassware
Beakers: Griffin, tall form and heavy wall, in glass or plastic, with printed scales and a writing spot
Flasks: Erlenmeyer, wide neck, filter, round bottom, three-neck, Florence and volumetric, with stoppers, caps, foil or cork rings
Test tubes, culture tubes, 15 and 50 ml conical tubes and microtubes, with corks, stoppers and caps
Graduated and mixing cylinders, burettes with PTFE or glass stopcocks, bulb, Mohr and Pasteur pipettes
Reagent, media, dropper and wash bottles with real chemical labels and hazard pictograms
Filter, separatory and Büchner funnels, Petri dishes with colonies, watch glasses, crystallizing and evaporating dishes, crucibles
Heating, racks and instruments
Bunsen, Tirrill, Meker and cartridge burners, spirit lamps, hot plate stirrers and heating mantles, tripods with gauze
Test tube racks full of colorful samples, retort stands with clamps, drying racks
Student, binocular, trinocular, stereo and vintage brass microscopes, balances, centrifuges, mortars and pestles
Lab furniture
Wall and island benches with epoxy, phenolic, laminate, wood or steel tops, fume hoods and lab stools
Glass and liquids have dedicated Cycles and EEVEE shaders and switch automatically with the render engine. Photoreal PBR materials also work in game engines. Labels, numbers and scales are image decals, never mesh text. Each item is one mesh; Make Real turns it into a plain mesh.
Requires Blender 4.2 or newer. Tested on Blender 4.2, 4.5 and 5.2 LTS.
