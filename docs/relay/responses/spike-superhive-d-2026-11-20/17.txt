Fountain Generator builds complete fountains and water features inside Blender, with the water already flowing. Pick one of 40 real-world designs, from a classical three-tier fountain, a Trevi-style Baroque wall and a piazza obelisk to the Court of the Lions, a Japanese tsukubai, a Roman nasone, a splash pad or a dancing fountain, then change anything: every part is a number, a choice or a switch, and the fountain rebuilds live as you drag.
Each fountain is made of real parts: a basin (round, oval, polygon, rectangle, quatrefoil, lobed, Islamic star or natural pond, with molded coping, steps, paving, half basins and infinity edges), a centerpiece (stacked bowls on balusters, acanthus columns, fluted columns or twisting dolphins, an obelisk on rockwork, a lion court, a statue, a sphere, a boulder, stacked stones or a modern sculpture), a backdrop (lion-head panel, shell niche, Ottoman çeşme, Moorish arch, Baroque facade, grotto, water wall or stele) and fixtures such as drinking fountains, tsukubai, shishi-odoshi and bird baths.
The water is built from the same parts: jets follow real ballistic arcs, bowls spill curtains or streams from every lobe, weirs pour sheets, bells and tulips open, foam crowns land where the water hits, and surfaces ripple. Press Play and the jets dance in pulses, waves, chases or at random, the water flows and the bamboo rocks, all without scripts at render time.

Features
40 designs in 6 groups: classical and piazza, wall fountains, Islamic and Japanese, drinking fountains, modern and interactive, garden and small
Tiered fountains with 1 to 5 bowls: dish, cup, scallop, lotus, octagon and square bowls on baluster, acanthus, fluted, dolphin or plain pedestals, with pinecone, pineapple, ball, urn or statue finials
Baroque wall fountains with columns, statue niches and rockwork, lion-head walls, garden niches, çeşmes, Moorish arches, grottoes, water walls and steles
Alhambra lion court, Moorish star basin and Persian rills with jets
Tsukubai with kakei and ladle, and an animated shishi-odoshi
Roman nasone, Victorian drinking fountain, steel bubbler and bottle filler
Infinity edges, floating granite spheres, stone monoliths, steel ribbons and rings, Corten spill bowls, water bells
Splash pads, dancing fountains and a floating lake jet
Garden cascades and water stairs, bird baths, tabletop bubblers, frog spitters, bubbling boulders and overflowing urns
Rings of jets or spitting frogs, fish, turtles and lion heads
Statue slots: parent your own statue and hide the placeholder
Realistic glass-like water for Cycles and EEVEE, or a Game mode with alpha-blended, scrolling water for game engines
Marble, travertine, limestone, sandstone, granite, concrete, terracotta and basalt, bronze, verdigris, painted iron, stainless, copper, brass, Corten and gold, zellige tiles, with weathering, algae, limescale and moss
Real-world sizes in meters, UV-mapped clean meshes, three detail levels and Convert to Mesh
Surprise, Vary and Random Finish buttons for endless new fountains
Picture-driven sidebar with eight tabs and a Shift+A menu
Requires Blender 4.2 or newer. Tested on Blender 4.2, 4.5 and 5.2 LTS.
