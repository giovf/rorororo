Bins & Mailboxes Generator builds the small things every street, park, office and backyard needs, right inside Blender: wheelie bins from 240 L household bins to 1100 L containers, rows of colored recycling bins and wooden bin stores, galvanized cans, pedal and office bins, park and street litter bins in cast iron, perforated steel, wood slats and concrete, bag holders and lamp-post bins, recycling stations, solar compactors, bottle banks, glass igloos, underground bins, front-load dumpsters, roll-off containers and builder's skips.
The mail side is just as wide: round and hexagonal pillar boxes, lamp and wall boxes, a blue collection box, a rural mailbox on a wooden post with its flag, cluster mailbox banks, parcel lockers, newspaper boxes, door letterboxes and post boxes in the style of Japan, Germany, France, China, Russia, Australia and India. Every emblem is a generic symbol, so the models are safe to use in commercial work.
Pick one of 40 designs, then change anything: the object rebuilds live while you drag a slider. Open the lid, fill the bin until it overflows, drop bags and litter around it, add dirt, rust, dents, stickers and graffiti, or press Surprise for a brand new one.
[One click, a new bin]
One click, a new bin
Press Surprise for a fresh random bin or mailbox, or pick a design from the picture gallery. Everything happens in the sidebar.
[A bin for every street]
A bin for every street
Wheelie bins, park bins, recycling stations, dumpsters and skips, pillar boxes, rural mailboxes, parcel lockers and post boxes from around the world.
[Open, fill, overflow]
Open, fill, overflow
Lids are hinged parts. Raise the fill level and garbage piles up until it props the lid open, with bags and litter around it.
[From new to wrecked]
From new to wrecked
Dirt, rust, fade, dents, stickers and graffiti on a slider, on top of physically based materials.
Features
40 designs in seven families: wheelie bins, indoor bins, street and park bins, recycling, dumpsters and skips, post boxes and mail
Round, box, polygon and half-round bodies with taper, flare, plinth, ribs and bands; solid, perforated, slatted or barred walls
8 tops and 4 lid types with a real hinge and an open angle, from flat and dome lids to split dumpster lids and rain hoods
13 mounts: wheels, casters, feet, legs, posts, twin posts, pedestals, walls, brick piers, skids and underground tanks
Letter slots, chutes, flaps, bottle holes and recycling apertures, panel, arched, glazed and locker doors
Painted text, pictograms, hazard and reflective stripes, waste-stream color coding per unit
Dirt, rust, fade, dents, graffiti, stickers, overflowing garbage, bags and litter
Concrete pads, paving, sidewalk curbs and bin store enclosures
Surprise, Vary, Colors and Wear buttons, a picture gallery in the sidebar and a Shift+A menu
Game-ready: real scale, origin on the ground, UVs, named parts in their own collection, PBR materials with scanned CC0 textures, three detail levels, Make LODs and Convert to Mesh
Works in Cycles and EEVEE
Requires Blender 4.2 or newer. Tested on Blender 4.2, 4.5 and 5.2 LTS.
