Tool Generator fills a workshop, a garage or a building site in a few clicks. Pick a kind of tool, pick one of 281 ready-made designs from the picture gallery and add it at real size, resting on the ground. Every design is built from settings, so you can change the size, the jaw opening, the handle, the colors and the condition, and the model rebuilds live.
There are 107 tool types in 8 kinds, each researched from real tools: real dimensions, real proportions and the small details that make them read as the real thing, like stamped sizes, knurled grips, set saw teeth, ribbed overmolds and battery packs.
[281 tool designs, one click each]

Wrenches, drills, hammers, pliers, tapes, saws, bars, clamps and meters. Pick one from the picture gallery and change anything.
[Drag a slider, watch it rebuild]
Drag a slider, watch it rebuild
Every tool is built from settings. Open the jaw of an adjustable wrench, change sizes, blades and handles, and the model rebuilds live.
[Any brand color]
Any brand color
Housings, grips and overmolds take a brand color and an accent color with one click, so a whole set can match.
