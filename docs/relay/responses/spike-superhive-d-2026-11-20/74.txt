Easy Dress: Adjustable Dress Generator for Blender
Easy Lacing (https://superhivemarket.com/products/easy-lacing-onion365)
Easy Dress turns dress modeling into a handful of sliders. Add a dress with one click, then shape the bodice, straps, skirt and a matching T-shirt in real time. When you are happy with it, press Finish Dress and you get a normal mesh with UV seams marked and the UVs unwrapped.
Features
Live editing: every slider rebuilds the mesh instantly, and Reset Settings brings back the defaults
Sweetheart bodice: heart notch, arcs and notches along the top edge, side and back drop, adjustable top edge bulge
Bust shaping: bump size, radius, height and position, center seam, chest smoothing
Straps: 1 to 4 parallel straps per shoulder, with width, height, tuck, gather, and straight or outward lean
Pleated skirt: pleat count, depth and size, round-lobe squeeze, random depth, width and lean, flare, bulge, waistband, hem wave, and raising or lowering the hem at the front, back and sides
Hem ruffle: a wavy frill with controls for waves, rows, flare, slope, droop and randomness
Lace holes: eyelets at the front, back and sides, optional open window with rounded corners, left/right mirroring
Split Top & Skirt: two separate pieces with a rolled hem, cloth bend, folds and twist
Belt and dress thickness: a belt band that follows the cloth, plus a built-in wall thickness
Matching T-shirt: length, width, neck shape (including off-the-shoulder), sleeves, sleeve ruffle, tank top or camisole, collar, rolled hem and wrinkles
Clean output: quad-based topology, adjustable mesh density, four material slots (dress, shirt, collar, belt) with color pickers
How it works
Open the sidebar (N) and go to the Easy Dress tab
Click Add Dress and adjust the sliders
Click Finish Dress to keep the mesh and unwrap the UVs (Resume Editing turns live editing back on, but manual edits are lost)
Requirements
Blender 5.2 or newer.
