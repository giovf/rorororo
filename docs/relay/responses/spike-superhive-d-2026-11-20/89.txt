Instant Clean is your one-click solution for fast, reliable mesh cleanup in Blender. Whether you’re importing models from CAD software, 3D scans, or online asset libraries, this addon automatically cleans up your geometry, fixes normals, and prepares everything for rendering, 3D printing, or export to game engines. With a user-friendly Preset System, it streamlines your workflow and lets you focus on what really matters: creating! Without Instant Clean, you start every time from scratch, you disrupt your workflow, you waste time and energy. With Instant Clean, you avoid all of this and focus on the creative part. One click away, Ultimate Mesh Clean is the all-in-one solution for mesh cleanup in Blender, where you know exactly what happened. The intuitive and dynamic Preset System helps you save as much time as possible with everything in one place. Customize reusable presets to fit your 3D modeling workflow in Blender. Single Execution allows every part of the mesh clean process to work on its own, with detailed results. You can also add them to the Quick Favorites menu in Blender. Mesh Repair eliminates culprits and provides a solution for most common Blender mesh errors and damages, such as non-manifold geometry, flipped normals, and broken edges. Fix Normals addresses face orientation issues, clears custom normals, enables auto smooth, and more. All essential when importing meshes into Blender or preparing them for shading and rendering. Geometry Dissolve removes unnecessary geometry while keeping the original mesh shape intact. Perfect for cleaning imported CAD models or scanned assets. Alter Topology allows you to convert the mesh topology to quads or tris depending on your needs. Useful for retopology or game asset export from Blender. Clean Objectdata merges double materials, clears empty material slots and vertex groups—a smart cleanup step when exporting assets or preparing your blend file. Make Manifold prepares your models to be manifold and watertight for 3D printing in Blender, with tools to identify and fix problematic geometry.
