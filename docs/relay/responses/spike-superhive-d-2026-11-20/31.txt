Loobox builds public and portable toilets inside Blender: the blue plastic porta-potty of every building site and festival, wheelchair-accessible units, hand wash stations, four-way and trough urinals, towable restroom trailers with steps and AC, self-cleaning street toilets, louvered steel kiosks with solar roofs, park restrooms, vault toilets, highway rest stops and wooden outhouses with a moon in the door. Pick one of 51 designs and change anything; the toilet rebuilds live.
Doors open on real hinges and every unit has an interior: a seat on the holding tank or a ceramic, stainless, squat or vault toilet, urinal, sink, mirror, paper rolls, sanitizer, grab bars, coat hook and a lamp that can light up. Vacant and occupied latches, pictogram plates for men, women and wheelchair access, and your own lettering on signs.
Line up festival rows of alternating colors with doors open at random, then age everything from fresh from the depot to a grubby day three or derelict, with mud splashed up from the ground, grime streaks, sun-faded plastic, rust and chipped paint.

Surprise, Vary, Random Colors and Random Age buttons
Picture sidebar, Shift+A menu and Mesh Copy for export to game engines
