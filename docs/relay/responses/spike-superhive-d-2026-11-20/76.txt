Balloon & Blimp Generator builds photorealistic lighter-than-air craft inside Blender: hot air balloons from one-person cloudhoppers to 24-passenger ride balloons, special shapes, gas balloons in their rope nets, non-rigid blimps, golden-age rigid zeppelins, modern hybrid airships, radar aerostats and small tethered advertising blimps. Pick one of 28 presets from pictures, press Add, then change anything. Every setting rebuilds the craft live.
The envelopes are made like the real thing. Each gore is a separate fabric panel between raised load tapes, the fabric bulges softly between the tapes and folds near the mouth, the horizontal panels carry double-stitched seams and every panel has its own slight dye-lot tint, so nothing looks like a smooth CG balloon. The crown has a parachute vent with its centering lines, the mouth a load ring, a scoop skirt and stainless flying wires down to the burner frame.
The baskets are woven wicker with leather-trimmed step holes, a suede rim roll, rawhide bands, ash runners, rope handles and partitions for the pilot and passenger compartments. On top sit single, double, triple or quad burners with vaporizing coils, jet rings, pilot lights and blast valves, fed by padded propane cylinders through sagging hoses.
Patterns are painted along the real seams, so stripes, checks and chevrons never cross a tape and never z-fight.

14 patterns with up to 4 colors, plus banner panels that print your own logo image
1 to 24 passengers: the basket, partitions, burners, cylinders and envelope volume follow
