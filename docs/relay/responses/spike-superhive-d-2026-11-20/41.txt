Scaffwright builds real scaffolding inside Blender in one click: classic tube and coupler with five-board lifts, Ringlock, Cuplock and Kwikstage system scaffolds, European facade frames, American walk-through frames, aluminium mobile towers and Baker scaffolds. Every tube is 48.3 mm, every bay and lift follows real sizes, and every coupler, rosette, cup, wedge, base plate, jack and castor is modeled, so it holds up in a close-up as well as across a street.
Pick one of 46 designs, from a terraced house front or a corner wrap to a birdcage, a stair tower, a mast hoist site or an abandoned, torn and rusting scaffold. Then change anything live: bays, lifts, bay length, width, lift heights, decks, guardrails, toe boards, brick guards, ladders or a stair tower, bracing, ties, sole boards, base plates or screw jacks. Wrap it in debris netting or sheeting that billows between the ties and tears in the wind, and add a gin wheel, a chain block, a mast hoist or a rubble chute.
Select your building and press Fit to Building: the scaffold sizes its bays and lifts to the wall and stands off it by the right gap. New, Used and Old wear settles the rust, mud, mortar splashes and grey weathered boards for you.

Features
46 designs in five families: tube and coupler, system, frames, towers and site life
8 scaffold systems with real parts: couplers, rosettes, cups, wedges, frames and braces
Facade, corner wrap, putlog, tower and birdcage layouts
Timber boards, planks, steel decks or plywood decks on every lift, every other lift or the top only
Ladders with ladder-gate openings or a full stair tower
Debris netting and sheeting with billow and tears, in any color
Gin wheel with bucket, chain block, mast hoist with cage, and rubble chutes
Photo-scanned PBR materials: galvanized steel, weathered timber, plywood, knitted netting
New, Used and Old condition, plus frame, clamp, hoist and netting colors
Fit to Building, Surprise and Vary buttons, Shift+A menu and Convert to Mesh
One mesh per scaffold with sensible face counts and Low, Medium and High detail
Every face carries its lift and element type as attributes, ready for build-up animations
Works in Cycles and EEVEE
