Fizzwright builds beverage packaging inside Blender: aluminium cans, glass beer, wine, spirits and soda bottles, PET bottles, milk and juice cartons and steel kegs. Pick one of 47 designs, then change anything. Every setting is a slider, a picture or a switch, and the design rebuilds live while you drag.
Each container is modeled after the real thing: cans with a necked top, double seam, stay-on tab and domed base; bottles with real glass walls, a punt and the right lip for their closure; PET bottles with a petaloid foot; gable-top and brick cartons; kegs with chimes and a spear valve.
Labels are painted for you with 18 brand-free designs in your own colors and lettering, or use your own image. Fill bottles with one of 14 drinks with foam and bubbles, cover them in condensation, crush a can, or put six in rings, a carrier, a tray, shrink film or a crate.
