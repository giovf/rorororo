Mirewood builds complete swamps and marshes inside Blender in one click: dark still water with duckweed and floating debris, muddy peat banks, beds of cattails and reeds, lily pads in flower, bald cypress trees on buttressed trunks with their knees, live oaks hung with Spanish moss, mangroves on arching prop roots, fallen logs and a wooden boardwalk winding through it all. Pick one of 45 designs in six wetland types, change anything, and the whole scene rebuilds in a fraction of a second.
Every plant is a real plant: trees, reeds, rushes and lilies are grown from scanned leaves and bark, and the water, mud, moss, peat and planks use photo-scanned PBR textures. Thousands of plants stay light because they are instanced from one shared library, so even a 60 m bayou stays fast in the viewport. Plants follow the water: reeds stand in the shallows, sedges line the banks, lilies float on deeper water and cypress knees ring their trees.
[Six kinds of wetland]
Six kinds of wetland
Bayou, cypress swamp, freshwater marsh, peat bog, salt marsh and mangrove swamp, one click each.
[A new swamp every click]
A new swamp every click
Randomize builds a fresh wetland of the same type: new water, plants, trees and banks.
[Raise the water]
Raise the water
Drag the water level from low-tide mudflat to high tide and the plants follow the new shore.
Wetland types
Bayou: slow black channels through cypress and moss-draped live oak
Cypress swamp: flooded cypress and tupelo forest, cypress domes, ghost swamps
Freshwater marsh: cattails, reeds, bulrush, sawgrass, pickerelweed and lily ponds
Peat bog: sphagnum, dark pools, cotton grass, stunted pine and birch, plank trails
Salt marsh: cordgrass, tidal creeks, low-tide mudflats and drowned ghost forests
Mangrove swamp: red mangrove prop roots and black mangrove pencil roots
Features
45 designs in 6 wetland types, picked from pictures
6 water looks: blackwater, tannin, algae green, muddy, brackish, peat
Duckweed carpet, algae scum, floating twigs and leaves, low mist
7 marsh plant beds, 4 lily kinds with white, pink or yellow flowers
10 tree kinds with Spanish moss, cypress knees, prop and pencil roots
Fallen logs, root plates, stumps and dead snags
4 boardwalk styles with viewing platforms
Water level, land amount, channel, relief and density sliders
Randomize and New Seed for endless new wetlands
Each swamp is independent: editing one never changes another or its copies
Make Plants Real turns the instances into objects for export
Works in Cycles and EEVEE
Requires Blender 4.2 or newer. Tested on Blender 4.2, 4.5 and 5.2 LTS.
