Blender 4.5+ · Windows · NVIDIA GPU
Pose a few key frames, or just type the motion. PoseFill AI fills every frame in between, on your own character.
Runs locally on your NVIDIA GPU. No cloud, no account, no subscription. The motions you make are yours.
✓ Write any prompt: unlimited, no credits ✓ Clips up to 30 seconds ✓ Feet planted, physics, collisions, hands and face built in ✓ Doesn't run on your GPU? Full refund within 30 days
New to PoseFill? Watch the step-by-step tutorial: How to make AI animation actually work (https://youtu.be/ox7c-QKZ_n8) (also in the Documentation tab).
[A front flip generated from one sentence]
"A person does a front flip." One sentence, real gravity in the air.
HOW IT WORKS
[Key poses filled in by the AI]
Pose a few key frames
Only the poses that matter, on your own rig. The motion in between is natural and hits every key exactly.
[A dance generated from a text prompt]
Or just type it
"A person dances, raising both arms and spinning around." Pick from ready-made motions or write your own.
[The character walks a curve drawn on the floor]
Draw a path on the floor
Any curve. Your character walks it, with the steps, turns and speed worked out for you.
[The character talks and looks at the camera]
Talks and looks at your camera
Lip sync from a sound strip, or from the prompt. Head and eyes follow any object you pick.
LAYERS ON TOP, EACH ONE A SWITCH
● Feet planted
Feet stay on the floor while they carry the body. No sliding.
● Physics
Jumps and flips follow real gravity and spin like a body does.
● Collisions
Hands stay out of the body, everything stays above the floor.
● Hands
Fists, open or relaxed hands that fit the motion. Your own hand poses are kept.
● Face
Blinks, gaze, expressions, a talking mouth and a look-at target.
● Loose parts
Hair, skirts and tails swing with the body and stay outside it.
Physics layer off and on: the same jump, now with a real ballistic arc and a clean landing.
SEE IT ON A REAL CHARACTER
Real loops from PoseFill AI on a VRoid character, no hand keying. Drag to orbit, pick a loop below the character.
WORKS WITH YOUR RIG
Rigify Mixamo VRoid / VRM Unreal / Unity skeletons MakeHuman / MPFB
Bones are matched by name, or from the shape of the skeleton. Character Creator and Daz Genesis names are recognised too.
Everything else you can do
Several takes at once: flip between results and keep the ones you like.
Loops: seamless cycles. In place works on any motion for game engines: walks tread on the spot, flips land where they took off.
Change the action mid-clip: "walks" on frame 1, "sits down" on frame 90, smoothly joined. Up to 30 seconds per clip.
Partial key poses: pose only one hand or foot and only that limb is pinned.
Redo Part, Mirror, Speed: regenerate one clumsy second, mirror left and right, or retime from 0.5x to 2x with jumps kept physically right.
FBX export: every kept motion as its own take, with Unity and Unreal presets.
Characters without a rig: place joint guides on a mesh and PoseFill builds the rig.
Requirements
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). macOS and Linux are not supported yet.
Blender 4.5 LTS or newer (tested on 4.5 LTS, 5.2 and 5.3).
NVIDIA GPU with 8 GB or more , GeForce RTX 20 series or newer. Everything runs on the GPU: on 8 to 16 GB cards the text model loads in 4- or 8-bit by itself, and in our tests the motions match the prompt just as well. With less than 8 GB the text model runs on the CPU and needs about 16 GB of free RAM.
About 30 GB of free disk and a one-time ~22 GB download of the AI engine. Internet is needed only for that.
Not sure your PC can run it? Message us from this page before buying. If it doesn't run on your hardware, you get a full refund within 30 days.
On an RTX 5090: install about 5 minutes on a fast connection, a 5-second clip in about 5 seconds, four takes at once in about 10 seconds. On an RTX 3070 Laptop (8 GB): the engine is ready in about a minute, four takes of a 5-second clip in about 20 seconds.
Installation
In Blender: Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions > Install from Disk and pick the zip.
Open the sidebar in the 3D Viewport ( N ) > PoseFill .
Press Install AI Engine , read and accept the licenses. It downloads in the background while you keep working.
Select your character > Animate This Character > type a prompt > Generate Motion .
Good to know
Humanoid, two-legged characters only.
Fingers are posed by the Hands layer, not generated by the AI.
Up to 30 seconds per generate; loops up to about 6 seconds.
Auto-Rig Pro control rigs are not supported yet. Use its export skeleton.
