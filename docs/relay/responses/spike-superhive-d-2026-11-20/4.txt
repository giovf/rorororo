Your Blender IK, live in Unity
FBX does not carry constraints. Your IK either reaches Unity frozen in the pose Blender baked, or not at all: a foot cannot find a step, a hand cannot find a door handle. Rebuilding it in Unity means another solver, another pole convention, another twist, and a pose that is not the one your animator made.
IKForge runs Blender's own IK solver in Unity. The animation goes to Unity without the IK; every frame, after the Animator, IKForge solves your chains from the targets, exactly as Blender does. Move a target in your game and the leg or arm follows the way it did in Blender, pole, limits, locked axes and stretch included.
Made for game rigs : the IK sits on the skeleton that skins the mesh, or the skinning bones follow the IK bones directly (Unreal's mannequin with IK added, mocap skeletons, your own character and creature rigs). Not for Rigify : its chains are solved, but its mesh follows stretchy B-Bone deform bones that Unity does not have. Scan tells you before you export which of your chains will run.
Measured, not promised
The solver : on 200 random chains from Blender (2 to 5 bones, poles, limits, locked axes, stiffness, stretch, influence, partial chains) 199 match Blender within 5 mm, 186 within 0.1 mm.
A real character in Unity 6 with leg and arm IK on a walk: every one of its 90 deforming bones within 0.025 mm of Blender's pose. The plain FBX without IK: up to 1.1 m off.
Check , in Blender and in Unity, measures your own rig on your own animation, bone by bone and frame by frame, and writes the report next to the prefab.
What you get
Scan : every IK chain of the armature, its bones, target and pole, locks, limits and stretch, and anything IKForge cannot run, with the reason.
Check in Blender : IKForge's solver against Blender's own IK on your active action, in millimetres.
Export for Unity : the FBX with the animation baked without the IK, a passport with the chains and Blender's poses, and the Unity scripts. Your scene is not changed.
In Unity : Tools > IKForge > Set Up Rig makes a prefab with the IKForge Rig component; Check Rig measures it against Blender. Each chain has a Target Override and a Pole Override that take any Transform of your scene, and a Weight that fades it in and out; a new export keeps them on their chains.
Honest limits
The Standard IK solver (not iTaSC); targets and poles are bones of the same armature; Rotation matching, IK trees (chains sharing bones) and animated IK settings are refused by name.
Rigs where the IK is on the skinning skeleton, or where the skinning bones follow the IK bones directly. Rigify's stretchy B-Bone deform bones read two bones at once and Unity has no B-Bones: Check names them. Rigify is not a target of 1.0.
The Unity scripts are MIT licensed: ship them in your games, closed source or not.
Blender 4.2 to 5.2. Unity 6. No Python packages, no cloud, no account, no AI model.
