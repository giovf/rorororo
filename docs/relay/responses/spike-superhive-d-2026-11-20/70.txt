Big Rig builds detailed semi trucks and their trailers in one click, in real-world meters. Pick one of 44 ready-made trucks: aero and classic long-nose sleepers, European and Japanese cab-overs, day cabs and bobtails, pulling dry vans, reefers, curtainsiders, livestock trailers, flatbeds with lumber, steel coils, beams, pipes or crates, step decks, lowboys, fuel and chemical tankers, dry bulk tanks, car carriers, container chassis, end dumps and doubles. Rigid box trucks, refrigerated trucks, stake beds, water tankers, dump trucks and concrete mixers are there too.
Then change anything and the truck rebuilds live: cab type, hood, sleeper and roof height, wheelbase, axles, super singles, trailer kind, length and axles, load, bumper, grille, headlights, stacks, fuel tanks, mirrors, visor, light bar, horns, fairings, wheels, livery and colors.
Every truck is modeled to be looked at up close: real window openings with an interior behind them, chrome stacks with heat shields, polished fuel tanks with straps and steps, a fifth wheel with air and electric coils, leaf springs and air bags, landing gear, lock rods and hinges on the doors, conspicuity tape, mud flaps and clearance lamps.
Wheels, steering, the trailer angle, tipping and the mixer drum are animated through drivers, so they play back in real time without a rebuild.

Features
44 presets in 5 groups: North American long haul, European cab-overs, Japan and Asia, box and delivery trucks, construction and heavy haul
Conventional cabs with aero, classic and vocational hoods; European and Japanese cab-overs; day cabs and sleepers with low, mid and raised roofs
13 trailer and body kinds, plus doubles on a converter dolly and container sizes of 20, 40 and 45 ft
Rigid trucks with up to two steer and three drive axles, liftgates and roll-up doors
Liveries: solid, two-tone, stripes, swoosh, hot-rod flames and racing stripes in gloss, metallic or matte
Chrome, aluminium, steel and black wheels, chrome stacks, light bars, air horns, visors and bug deflectors
Animatable Drive, Steer, Trailer Angle and Tip, plus real lamps for headlights and spot lights
Dirt and rust sliders, low, medium and high detail, Convert to Mesh for export
Surprise Me, Variation and New Seed buttons, picture pickers and a Shift+A menu
All names are generic and there are no real brand badges, so the renders are safe for commercial work.
Requires Blender 4.2 or newer. Tested on Blender 4.2, 4.5 and 5.2 LTS.
