Bathwright builds complete, photoreal bathrooms inside Blender, or any single piece of one: a Victorian clawfoot tub, a wall-hung toilet, a shaker vanity with a marble top, a frameless glass walk-in shower, an arched brass mirror, a heated towel ladder or a herringbone tiled wall. Pick one of 113 designs, then change anything: every part is a number, a choice or a switch, and the design rebuilds live as you drag.
Whole rooms come in six layouts (family bathroom, ensuite, master, powder room, wet room and tub-shower), with three walls cut away for the camera, a window, real tiles with grout on walls and floor, and every fixture placed against the right wall. Each fixture of a room keeps its own tab, so you can swap the tub for a slipper bath or the vanity for a vessel sink in one click.
Tiles are real tiles, not a texture: subway, stacked, herringbone, chevron, basketweave, hexagon, penny round, mosaic, diamond, checkerboard, pinwheel, plank and finger patterns, with bevelled edges, cut tiles at the borders, tone variation and handmade zellige faces.

Features
113 designs: 14 rooms, 16 tubs, 12 toilets and bidets, 19 sinks and vanities, 14 showers, 10 mirrors, 10 rails and 18 tiled surfaces
Bathtubs: roll top, slipper, double slipper, bateau, modern oval and rectangle, soaking, alcove, drop-in, corner, Japanese ofuro and walk-in, with claw, ball or block feet
Toilets: close-coupled, one-piece, back-to-wall, wall-hung, high tank with pull chain, low level, smart and squat, plus bidets
Sinks: pedestal, wall-hung, corner, console, vessel, undermount, drop-in, semi-recessed, integrated, double and trough, on vanities with doors, drawers, shaker or fluted fronts
Showers: walk-in screens, corner, quadrant, neo-angle, alcove, sliding, Crittall-style and curtain, with rain, ceiling, wall and exposed heads, trays and linear drains
15 tile patterns for walls and floors, wainscot or full-height wet zones and trims
Mirrors with frames, LED light and cabinets, towel rails, radiators and paper holders
Photoreal PBR materials that export to game engines: 11 metal finishes, glazes, woods, marble, terrazzo, concrete, slate
Surprise, Vary and random finish buttons, picture-driven sidebar, Shift+A menu, Convert to Mesh
Works in Cycles and EEVEE
Requires Blender 4.2 or newer. Tested on Blender 4.2, 4.5 and 5.2 LTS.
