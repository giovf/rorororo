Helicopter Generator builds detailed, ready-to-render helicopters in one click, in real-world meters. Pick one of 15 ready-made helicopters: a light piston trainer with a bubble canopy, a classic bubble helicopter with a truss boom, a news turbine, a police patrol helicopter, a medical twin with a fenestron, a corporate twin on retractable wheels, a Huey-style and a Hawk-style utility helicopter, a tandem-seat attack helicopter with stub wings and weapons, a heavy tandem-rotor transport with a rear ramp, a coaxial helicopter, a NOTAR helicopter, a sky crane, a search and rescue helicopter with a hoist and a cartoon toy.
Then change anything and the helicopter rebuilds live: fuselage length, width, height and nose shape, canopy, doors and windows, tail boom, tail rotor, fenestron or NOTAR, fins and stabilizers, main rotor layout, diameter, blade count, chord, twist and tip shape, rotor head type, engine, landing gear, mission equipment, weapons and livery.
Every helicopter is modeled to be looked at up close: rotor heads with hub, swashplate, pitch links and dampers, blades with a real airfoil, twist, erosion strips and painted tips, engine cowlings with intake grilles and exhausts, doors and windows with frames and rubber seals, and a cockpit with seats, an instrument panel and controls that you see through the glass.
Rotors spin through drivers at the RPM you set, with a spool-up time and counter-rotating tandem and coaxial rotors. One click sets up motion blur for Cycles and EEVEE. Doors, the ramp and the retractable gear are animated sliders that never rebuild the mesh.
Every helicopter also comes with a full animation rig, on by default. Fly it with one control at the center of mass, tilt the rotor discs (the cyclic sticks in the cockpit follow), slow the rotors, open every door, lower the ramp, retract the gear, roll the wheels, aim the searchlight, camera ball and chin gun at their targets, swing the hoist and lower its hook on a stretching cable, and work the collective and pedals. Bake Spin and Bake Roll turn rotor speed changes and taxiing into exact keyframes. Under the controls is a clean skeleton that exports to game engines as one rigged model.

Features
15 presets: light piston, bubble, news, police, medical, corporate, utility, attack, heavy tandem, coaxial, NOTAR, sky crane, search and rescue and toy
Single, tandem and coaxial rotors with 2 to 8 blades, teetering, articulated and hingeless heads, square, swept, parabolic, BERP and round tips
Tail rotor on either side, fenestron, NOTAR or none, swept, pylon, T-tail, twin and endplate fins
Skids with optional floats, tailwheel, tricycle, retractable, quad and long sky-crane legs
Searchlight, FLIR turret, rescue hoist, cargo hook, wire cutters, mast radar, antennas, steps and chin gun
Stub wings with rocket pods, missiles, gun pods or fuel tanks
Liveries: solid, two-tone, stripes, swoosh, news, police, medical, rescue, military, camouflage, desert, navy and VIP, painted into one texture with no z-fighting
Real panel layout in the paint: panel lines, rivet rows, access panels, light edge wear, rain streaks and exhaust soot, with a Wear slider
Navigation lights, beacon and tail light, with real lamps you can switch on and off
Photoreal PBR materials that also work in game engines, low, medium and high detail, Convert to Mesh for export
Surprise Me, Variation and New Seed buttons, a picture gallery and a Shift+A menu
All registrations and names are made up, so the renders are safe for commercial work.
Requires Blender 4.2 or newer. Tested on Blender 4.2, 4.5 and 5.2 LTS.
