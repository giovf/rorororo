Bandstand builds real-scale musical instruments in one click: 103 ready-made designs across 33 kinds, from acoustic and electric guitars to drum kits, grand pianos, violins, harps, trumpets, saxophones and flutes. Pick a design by its picture and the instrument appears, with strings, frets, keys, valves, pads and hardware in place.
Every instrument stays editable. Change the body, neck, pickups, drum sizes, piano lid, trombone slide or finish in the sidebar and the model rebuilds live.

What you get
103 designs in 7 families: guitars, folk strings, orchestral strings, drums and percussion, pianos and keys, brass and saxophones, woodwinds
Guitars: dreadnought, jumbo, parlor, classical, flamenco, archtop, semi-hollow, double cut, single cut, offset, superstrat, arrowhead, headless, 4 and 5-string basses
Ukuleles, mandolins (A and F style) and banjos with paired strings and resonators
Violin, viola, cello, double bass with bows, Celtic and concert harps
Drum kits (rock, jazz, fusion, double kick), snares, congas, bongos, djembe, cajon, timpani, xylophone, marimba, vibraphone, glockenspiel
Upright, baby, parlor and concert grand pianos, stage piano, synthesizer and digital console
Trumpet, cornet, flugelhorn, trombone, French horn, tuba, euphonium and four saxophones
Flute, piccolo, clarinet, oboe and recorders
Finishes: sunburst, see-through, solid, metallic, sparkle, pearl wrap and satin over real wood textures; chrome, nickel, gold and black hardware; lacquer, silver and raw brass
Surprise for a random instrument, Vary for a new take on the current design
Editing one instrument never changes another, Shift+D and Alt+D copies included
Easy to use
Open the Musical tab in the sidebar, click the picture of a design and press Add Instrument. Every option with a visual choice has its own picture gallery. Convert to Mesh keeps the result as ordinary meshes, optionally split into parts.
Requires Blender 4.2 or newer. Tested on Blender 4.2, 4.5 and 5.2 LTS.
