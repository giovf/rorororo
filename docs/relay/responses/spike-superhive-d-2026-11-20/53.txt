What it does
MM Caliper reads the real size of your model in millimetres , the way a slicer and a real caliper will see it. Select two verts, a hole's rim, two parallel faces or whole objects, click one button in the sidebar, and you get the number in mm (and inches), with the object's scale and rotation applied and your scene units taken into account. The last measurement is drawn in the 3D Viewport (lines plus a value tag), and one click copies the value to the clipboard. It only measures: your mesh is never changed.
Who it's for
Anyone who models parts for 3D printing in Blender and needs to check a size before slicing: does the 8 mm hole really measure 8 mm, is that wall 2 mm thick, how big is the gap between the lid and the box, will the part fit the printer? No more switching units, applying scale or turning on edge-length overlays and adding up numbers.
Key features
Distance: straight distance between exactly 2 selected verts, plus the size along X, Y and Z (dX / dY / dZ). Works across two objects in multi-object Edit Mode.
Edge length: total length of the selected edges, with count, shortest and longest. A selected loop gives its perimeter.
Diameter: select the verts of a hole, rim or edge loop (3 or more, any orientation): a geometric least-squares circle fit gives the diameter and radius. For a closed edge loop it also shows the flat-to-flat size of the faceted hole, which is what really prints (a 32-sided 8 mm hole is 7.961 mm across the flats), plus a roundness value and a warning when the points are not on one circle. Partial arcs work too. Verts on a straight line are refused, not turned into a giant circle.
Gap / thickness: perpendicular distance between selected parallel faces: wall thickness (outside face + inside face) or the gap between two parts. One or more faces per side, with a parallel limit in degrees; when the sides are slightly tilted it also shows the smallest and largest gap.
Angle: between 2 selected faces (between their normals, 0 = parallel, 90 = square, plus the inside angle where they meet) or 2 selected edges (the corner angle when they share a vert, else the angle between the lines), in world space.
Object size: size along the world X, Y and Z axes of the selected objects together, with or without modifiers. Meshes, curves, text, surfaces, metaballs, geometry-nodes instances and collection instances (linked assets) count; cameras, lights and plain empties are skipped with a note.
Units done right: 1 Blender unit = 1 mm by default (right for STL / 3MF), or Auto (follows Scene > Units), 1 cm, 1 m or 1 inch. A red hint appears when the model size does not fit the setting. Every result is also shown in inches.
Stays up to date: with Auto re-measure (on by default) the result and the overlay follow when you move, scale or edit the measured geometry; turn it off and the result is marked out of date instead. Changing Decimals, units or another setting re-measures at once.
Viewport overlay of the last measurement and a Copy button (value only, or with " mm"). Decimals are a setting (default 3).
Clear messages when the selection does not fit, shown in the panel as well as the status bar. Edit Mode changes are included right away. Precise far from the origin (double-precision maths).
How to use
Install the zip, press N in the 3D Viewport and open the Caliper tab.
Press Tab for Edit Mode and select: 2 verts for Distance , edges for Edge Length , the rim of a hole for Diameter , a face on each side for Gap / Thickness , or 2 edges or 2 faces for Angle . For Object Size just select the objects.
Click the button. Read the result in the panel and in the viewport, and click Copy to paste it anywhere.
Compatibility
Blender 4.2 LTS or newer, tested on 4.2 LTS and 5.2 LTS. It installs as a Blender extension (Get Extensions > Install from Disk), and a legacy add-on zip is included.
Good to know
It measures only: no scaling, no fixing, no modelling.
Distance, Edge Length, Diameter and Gap work on the mesh in Edit Mode (the base mesh, without modifiers). Object Size can include modifiers (on by default).
Object Size is the box along the world axes. A rotated part gives a bigger box than its own sides; rotate it flat first if you need its own length and width.
Gap / Thickness needs flat, parallel faces. Curved walls (a tube) are better measured with Diameter on the inside and outside rim.
Auto re-measure measures the last kind again with the current selection; if the selection no longer fits (for example you deselected a vert), the old result stays and is marked out of date.
The diameter is fitted to the selected verts. The flat-to-flat size is shown only when the selection is one closed edge loop (not for arcs or a few loose verts).
Linked duplicates (Alt+D) share one mesh and Blender edits only one of them: the result then says which object was measured.
Measuring adds no undo steps, so Ctrl+Z goes straight back to your last real edit.
