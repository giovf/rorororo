Earthmover builds construction machines inside Blender: crawler, mini and wheeled excavators, backhoe loaders, wheel loaders, skid steers and compact track loaders, bulldozers, road rollers, rigid and articulated dump trucks, site dumpers and motor graders. Pick one of 54 designs modeled on the machines working on real sites, from a 1 t micro digger to a 90 t mining excavator, then change anything and watch it rebuild live.
Every machine comes rigged. Booms, sticks, buckets, loader arms, blades, rippers, stabilizers, dump bodies and the articulation joint each have one slider, and the hydraulic cylinders follow every pose: barrels and rods slide in and out, the bucket linkage folds like the real four-bar, and telescopic hoists extend in stages. Pick a pose (Travel, Dig, Reach, Dump, Rest) with one click, or keyframe the sliders to animate a full dig cycle.
Tools and attachments include dig, trench, ditch and rock buckets, a hydraulic breaker, a sorting grapple, a ripper tooth and crusher jaws; loaders take general, rock, high volume and 4-in-1 buckets or pallet forks. Dozers get straight, semi-U, U and angle blades with single or multi shank rippers.
