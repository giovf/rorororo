Motorcycle Generator builds photorealistic motorcycles inside Blender, from superbikes and naked roadsters to cruisers, choppers, bobbers, café racers, scramblers, motocross bikes, big adventure tourers, baggers, classic standards and scooters. Pick a type, pick one of 25 designs modeled on real bikes, pick a condition from brand new to rusty, and press Add.
Then change anything. The sidebar is picture-driven: frames, forks, engines, exhausts, mufflers, rims, treads, tanks, seats, fairings, fenders, handlebars, headlights, mirrors, gauges, tail lights and luggage are chosen from rendered thumbnails of the actual result, so you never guess what an option does. Every measurement is a slider: wheelbase, rake, trail, wheel and tire sizes, seat height, tank and seat shape, bar width and rise, engine size and tilt, and many more. Each tab shows its pictures first and its sliders under them, and the detailed frame geometry stays folded away until you open it, so the panel never overwhelms.
The bikes are modeled, not faked: laced spokes and nipples, drilled and floating discs with calipers, chains with real links, cooling fins, cables and hoses, stitched seats, lenses with reflectors, number plates and indicators. Condition is a full wear system: chipped and faded paint, scratches, grime, mud, rust that eats into bare steel, flat tires and sagging springs. Materials are PBR texture sets ready for game engines.

Features
12 types and 25 real-world designs, plus Surprise Me for endless random bikes of any type
4 conditions: Brand New, Used, Old and Rusty, with wear, fade, rust, dirt, mud, flat tire and sag sliders
22 rim styles with thumbnails: wire, tubeless wire, 80-spoke, off-road, 3 to 10-spoke cast, Y-spoke, split, turbine, forged, mag star, Comstar, solid and holed discs, billet, mesh, spider and scooter steel
7 frames, 5 forks, 4 swingarms, 3 rear suspensions
8 engine layouts from single to inline four, boxer and electric, air or liquid cooled, chain, belt or shaft drive
10 exhaust routes and 8 muffler shapes
9 tire treads, single, dual and drum brakes, 4 disc styles
10 tanks, 8 seats with 4 stitch patterns, 7 fairings, fenders, tails and side panels
9 handlebars, 9 headlights, mirrors, gauges with real dials, tail lights, indicators and a number plate
Luggage, racks, top box, crash bars, skid plate and sissy bar
19 color slots with 46 PBR materials: paints, metallic flake, chrome, brushed and cast aluminium, carbon fibre, leathers, rubber and glass
Steering, side stand lean and wheel spin pose without a rebuild; Low to High detail; Make Static for export
Shift+A menu; works in Cycles and EEVEE
Requires Blender 4.2 or newer. Tested on Blender 4.2, 4.5 and 5.2 LTS.
