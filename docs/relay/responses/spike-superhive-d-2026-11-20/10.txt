Mirror Generator builds photorealistic mirror models inside Blender: wall mirrors, standing mirrors and ornate antique mirrors, each with a real frame, real glass and the hardware that holds it. Pick one of 47 designs from a picture gallery and press Add, then change anything you like. Every edit rebuilds the mirror live.
Wall mirrors come in 16 outlines, from rectangle, round and arch to Gothic, Moorish, scalloped, pill, hexagon and organic shapes, framed by 12 molding profiles, rope, a thin metal band or no frame at all. Hang them on a picture wire, a French cleat, a leather strap over a peg, a chain or chrome rosettes, add a shelf, window bars, an LED band or a backlight.
Standing mirrors stand on real stands: floor leaners, cheval mirrors on turned uprights, easels, metal floor stands, pole stands, vanity mirrors on a yoke, double-sided magnifiers, ring stands, a Georgian dressing box with drawers and a tri-fold vanity.
Ornate mirrors carry carved 3D ornament: baroque cartouche crests, rococo shells, Louis XVI ribbon bows, acanthus corners, pearl beads, rope moldings, sunburst rays, Venetian etched-glass borders, Federal convex glass with balls, Art Deco fans, Gothic pinnacles, Moroccan studs and overmantel pediments.
