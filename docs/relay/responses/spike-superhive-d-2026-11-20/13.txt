BLENDER ADD-ON · ROBLOX UGC
Make Roblox UGC that passes the first time .
UGCLift checks your hats, hair, layered clothing, bodies, heads and emotes the way Roblox's upload validation does, right in Blender. Every problem is explained, most are fixed in one click, and the item is exported ready for Roblox Studio.

NEW IN 2.0
Works with the files you really have, and Live Link to Roblox Studio
We put UGCLift 2.0 through 39 messy scenes and 58 real files, Roblox's own templates among them. Roblox's FBX templates in centimetres, clothing files with their rig, mannequins and cage templates now check cleanly, and Roblox's own clothing and accessory samples pass Make Ready with no error. Make Ready does more: it scales a centimetre file's whole item, turns text and curves into meshes, plays an action an import left in the NLA editor and bakes procedural materials into one texture. UGCLift tells the item's type from its name, follows Roblox's November 2026 rules (nothing below the feet, small part moves in animation packs) and never ends in a traceback: Copy Bug Report gives you what to send us.
Live Link to Studio: press Live Link in Blender and Live in the UGCLift Companion plugin, and your accessory sits on a dummy in Roblox Studio, changing as you model. Nothing is uploaded.
Live Link to Roblox Studio Roblox's own files check cleanly Bake to One Texture Nothing below the feet (new Roblox check) Copy Bug Report Tested on Blender 5.0-5.3
SOUND FAMILIAR?
Roblox said no. Again.
Studio's validation stops an upload with messages like these, after you have already exported, imported and fitted the item. UGCLift finds the same problems in Blender, before you export, and tells you what to do about them.
✕ ROBLOX STUDIO SAYS
Model mesh Wings resolution of 33120 is higher than max support value of 4000. You need to retopologize your model and try again.
✓ UGCLIFT, BEFORE YOU EXPORT
33,120 / 4,000 triangles. Fix simplifies it to 3,916 with the UVs and weights kept.
✕ ROBLOX STUDIO SAYS
Wings has size larger than max allowed bounding size. The max size for type BackAccessory is [9.86, 8.59, 4.87].
✓ UGCLIFT, BEFORE YOU EXPORT
The live size box turns red while you model. Fix fits the item into its box.
✕ ROBLOX STUDIO SAYS
Dynamic Heads on the marketplace must be properly caged so that the eyes and mouth vertices on the cage line up with the mesh vertices.
✓ UGCLIFT, BEFORE YOU EXPORT
Cage holes and face motion measured on the head, with a warning before you upload.
HOW IT WORKS
Three steps from Blender to Roblox
1
Check
Select the item and press Check. UGCLift works out what it is and runs Roblox's checks on it.
2
Make Ready
One click fixes what can be fixed, checks again and exports the FBX for Studio's 3D Importer.
3
Upload
Import into Roblox Studio, fit it, run UGC Validation: no errors on the items we tested.
[Before and after Make Ready: 33,120 triangles to 3,916, fitted into the Back box, no errors, exported]
EVERYTHING IN UGCLIFT
Tools for every Roblox avatar item
Not only a checker: UGCLift has the tools to make the items too.
01 · CHECK
See what Roblox would refuse
[Check: every problem explained, with what Roblox Studio would say]
Triangle budgets, the size box of each accessory type, attachments, cage distances and UVs, textures, skinning, FACS poses and body proportions, measured the way Roblox measures them. The triangle count and the size box stay live while you model. Each problem selects its faces in the viewport and comes with the reason, the fix and the message Studio would show.
Hats & accessories Hair Layered clothing Bodies Dynamic heads Emotes Classic clothing Makeup
02 · LAYERED CLOTHING
Cages that fit, without the pain
[Fit Outer Cage with its progress, then the heatmap, on a varsity bomber]
Fit Outer Cage builds the outer cage from Roblox's template and tucks the inner cage under the garment, checked with Roblox's own distance test. The heatmap shows the fit, weights come over from the body, skirts get loose weights, and the Motion and Pose tests walk, run and sit the avatar so you see stretching before Roblox does.
Fit Outer Cage Inner cage tuck Heatmap Weight transfer Loose skirts Motion & pose tests
03 · BODIES & DYNAMIC HEADS
Bodies and faces Roblox accepts
[Dynamic heads: the face auto-rig and the FACS poses Roblox requires]
Attachments placed for you, an R15 rig from your parts, body cages from Roblox's template, and the body checks Roblox runs: sizes, how solid each part looks from six sides, thin bits, pose. The face auto-rig turns 14 points into 21 bones and 47 FACS poses, the 17 Roblox requires included, and checks them the way Roblox checks a dynamic head.
Attachments R15 rig from parts Body cages Six-side check FACS auto-rig ARKit to FACS

Procedural materials in seven groups, smart masks for wear and dirt, decals and all-over prints, trim sheets and PBR baking to the four maps Roblox reads. Color Variants make every colourway in one go, each with its own materials, FBX and picture.
