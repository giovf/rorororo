Making Christmas baubles by hand takes ages. Christmas Bauble Generator makes them in seconds, and every setting stays editable.
What it does
7 shapes – Round, Egg, Onion, Teardrop, Drop, Finial and Icicle, with Stretch, Bottom Tip and handmade Wobble
Ribs – fluted grooves with depth and twist, for spiral glass looks
Caps – Classic, Ornate or Minimal, in gold, silver, copper or black, with ridges, a skirt that hugs the bauble and a hanging ring. Solid and watertight
Thread loops – a thread through the ring, with length, thickness and colour (gold, silver, red, green, white, black)
Finishes – glossy, satin, matte, mirror, glitter, clear glass and frosted glass, plus a Shine slider
Patterns – stripes, swirl, dots, a band and a frost band, with scale and width
Glow – make baubles light up in their own colours
Random sets – a grid of baubles in one click from 6 palettes (Classic red & gold, Nordic frost, Candy, Midnight glow, Woodland, Random)
Hang on Tree – select any tree mesh and baubles are hung around its outside, with spacing and height range
Rest On Surface – hung baubles lean on the branches like real ones, following the tree's slope, with the thread reaching the branch. No part of a bauble goes inside the tree. A Lean slider sets how much they tilt
Detail controls – separate detail sliders for the body, the cap and ring, and the thread, so you can go low-poly for games
Live web panel – control everything from your browser with real Blender renders of the bauble, preview angle and background options, and tooltips on every setting. It runs only on your own computer
Make Normal Mesh – turn baubles into plain meshes for export or sharing
All meshes are clean and watertight with correct normals and UVs.
Who it's for
Anyone making Christmas scenes, product shots, animations, game props or asset packs in Blender.
