Luggage Generator builds photorealistic suitcases, trunks and travel bags inside Blender. Pick one of 35 designs modeled on real luggage, from a glossy polycarbonate cabin spinner or a grooved aluminium check-in case to a softside upright, a rugged protective case, a 1950s leather suitcase, a wood-slatted steamer trunk or a leather weekender. Then change anything: every part is a number, a choice or a switch, and the case rebuilds live.
The shell is real geometry with 15 molded patterns: grooves, ribs, bands, diamonds, chevrons, honeycomb, waves, cubes and more, wrapping over the rounded edges like real molded shells. Size, corner radius, crown, draft, lid share and bead are all yours.
The hardware is modeled one part at a time: zippers with interlocking teeth, sliders and lockable pulls, a TSA lock with numbered dials, aluminium frames with flip latches, Pelican-style double-throw latches, brass clasps and hasps, hinges, double spinner wheels with bearings, inline wheels and trunk casters, a telescopic handle with up to three stages, carry handles, corner guards, rivets, belts, name tags, airline tags and travel stickers.
Open the lid and the inside is there too: lining, mesh dividers, cross straps, compression boards, zip pockets, pick-n-pluck foam and optional packed clothes. Open the lid, pull out the handle, swivel and roll the wheels, lift the carry handles: all are instant poses you can keyframe.
