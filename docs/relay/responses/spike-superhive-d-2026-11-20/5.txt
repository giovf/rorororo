Build Edo-inspired Japanese villages in Blender with editable machiya houses, curve-based streets, shrines, pagodas, and landscape props. Start from a preset or place individual assets to create a market lane, temple court, or lantern-lined approach.
13 generators for architecture and landscape, including torii gates, stone lanterns, and stairs.
Five tree variants and three stone types with live viewport sizing.
One-click variation for newly placed or selected trees and stones.
Optional procedural materials , reusable JSON presets, and Blender asset library export.
Machiya & Merchant Streets - Create timber townhouses with lattice facades, door openings, and optional courtyards. Arrange up to 24 houses along a curve and control their spacing and variation.
Shinto Shrines & Approaches - Assemble torii gates, prayer halls, stone stairs, and lantern-lined paths. Generate individual elements for custom layouts.
Buddhist Temples & Pagodas - Create temple courts with halls, paths, and perimeter walls. Adjust pagoda height from two to five storeys and control roof overhangs.
...and more
Refine buildings in place. Select a structure, load its settings, and regenerate it while keeping its position and rotation. Edit a single house in a street without rebuilding its neighbors. Choose from three detail levels and use built-in procedural materials, your own materials, or geometry only.
Reuse your work. Save generated structures as Blender collection assets or create independent mesh copies for further editing and export.
Compatibility and download. The download is an installable Blender Extension ZIP with presets built into the panel. Blender 4.2+. Tested on Windows, macOS and Linux. The gallery shows scenes created with the add-on.
