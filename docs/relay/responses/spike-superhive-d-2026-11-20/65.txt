Kitchen Cabinet Generator builds complete fitted kitchens inside Blender: carcasses, doors and drawers, handles, worktops, tiled backsplashes, sinks with faucets and a matching set of appliances, all on a real 600 mm (or US inch) module system with 2-3 mm door gaps. Pick one of ten designer presets or press Surprise, then change anything. The kitchen rebuilds live in a fraction of a second.
Draw your room as a polyline and the kitchen follows the walls, or build it from dimensions: straight, L-shape, U-shape, galley, straight or L with an island, and peninsula. Corners are handled like a kitchen fitter would, with blind, L-shaped and diagonal corner units, fillers and end panels.
Every surface uses photoreal PBR materials that also export to game engines: Poly Haven oak, walnut, white oak, granite, concrete and butcher block, plus marble, quartz, soapstone and stainless steel. The geometry is clean and free of overlapping faces, so nothing flickers in renders or real-time engines.

Ten designer kitchens
