Build human, fantasy and sci-fi game characters, rigged, dressed and animated, without leaving Blender. Choose from 29 species, including humans, elves, dwarves, orcs, goblins, demons, vampires, zombies, aliens, robots and animal folk. Pick an art style and a preset, then change anything: body, face, skin, hair, beard, clothes, accessories and weapons. The character rebuilds live while you drag a slider, and every choice stays editable.
The deform skeleton uses the Mixamo bone names and hierarchy, so the character works in Unity, Unreal, Godot and every Mixamo-ready tool. On top of it sits a full control rig for animators: IK/FK switching with snapping, pole targets, stretch, space switching, a head and eye aim, and a face rig with 52 ARKit shape keys.
