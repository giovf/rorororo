Washday builds photorealistic laundry rooms inside Blender, with every piece you need: front-loading and top-loading washers, tumble dryers, stacked pairs, laundry baskets and hampers, ironing boards with irons, and drying racks with washing hung on them. Pick one of 66 designs based on real appliances and household items, then change anything. Every part is a number, a choice or a switch, and the model rebuilds live as you drag.
The machines are modeled like the real ones. Front-loaders have a glass door that opens on its hinge, a rubber gasket, a steel drum with lifters, a detergent drawer and a printed control panel with knobs, a dial, touch keys or a lit display. Top-loaders open their lid onto an agitator or an impeller. Dryers come vented, heat-pump, console, wall compact and coin-op. Stack a dryer on a washer, put a pair side by side under a worktop, or raise them on drawer pedestals.
Whole rooms are ready in one click: a side-by-side laundry with cabinets and a sink, a stacked closet, a utility room, a basement with a utility tub and exposed pipes, a laundromat and a Scandi room. Each comes with tiled, wooden or concrete floors, shelves, detergent bottles and a window.
