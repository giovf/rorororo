Lighthouse Generator builds complete lighthouses inside Blender, from Cape Hatteras with its black and white spiral to a wave-swept rock tower, a screw-pile cottage on iron legs, a cast-iron sparkplug light, a red lightship or a modern pier-head beacon. Pick one of 32 designs modeled on real lighthouses, then change anything: every part is a number, a choice or a switch, and the lighthouse rebuilds live as you drag.
Eight tower types cover the real world of lighthouses: conical brick and stone towers, straight cylinders, the curved "oak tree" profile of offshore rock lights, octagonal and square towers, open skeletal iron frames, slim masts and lanterns sitting straight on a house roof. They stand on the ground, a stepped plinth, a rock in the sea, a caisson, screw piles, the end of a pier or the deck of a lightship.
The top is built like the real thing: corbels or brackets under the gallery, railings, a watch room, a glazed lantern room with vertical or helical astragals, a dome, cone or low cap with ventilator, vane and lightning rod. Inside the lantern sits a Fresnel lens from 1st to 6th order with prism rings and bull's-eye panels, or an LED beacon or aerobeacon, and it turns: the beams sweep around on a looping animation with real spot lights.

Features
32 real-world designs in 6 groups, each fully editable
US coast towers, rock lights, skeletal, caisson and screw-pile lights, the lightship, keeper's houses, French, Spanish, Italian, Irish, Japanese and Nordic lights, pier beacons, concrete and fiberglass towers
8 tower types with real dimensions in meters
Daymarks: solid, bands, spirals, diamonds, vertical stripes and top bands in any two colors
7 bases: ground, plinth, sea rock, caisson, screw piles, pier and lightship hull
Keeper's houses: detached house, Cape Cod, schoolhouse, Mediterranean and hexagonal cottage
Fresnel lenses from 1st to 6th order, flashing panels, LED beacon and aerobeacon
Rotating beams as glow cones or volumes, real lights, color and period controls
Doors, windows, spiral stairs with landings and a cutaway to show the inside
9 wall surfaces (painted brick, stone, granite, concrete, cast iron, shingle, fiberglass, steel) with weathering
UV-mapped geometry, origin at the ground, three detail levels and Convert to Mesh for game engines
Surprise, Vary and Random Colors buttons for endless new lighthouses
Picture-driven sidebar with eight tabs and a Shift+A menu
Works in Cycles and EEVEE
Requires Blender 4.2 or newer. Tested on Blender 4.2, 4.5 and 5.2 LTS.
