Staircase Generator builds complete, photoreal staircases inside Blender: treads, risers, stringers, landings, balusters, newel posts and handrails, all in one clean mesh with real PBR materials. Pick one of 48 designs, from a classic oak hall stair with turned spindles to a floating walnut stair with LED strips, a steel spiral, a sweeping marble curve or an imperial staircase for a palace, then change anything and watch it rebuild live.
Every common plan is covered: straight flights with or without a mid landing, L stairs with a quarter landing or winders, U stairs with a half landing or winders, three flights around an open well, spiral stairs on a centre column, curved helical stairs, imperial and bifurcated stairs that split at the landing, and steep alternating-tread loft stairs. Set the floor height and the number of risers, and the stair keeps every step equal, with comfort notes when it gets too steep.
Choose what carries the steps: closed or cut stringers, a central steel spine, cast concrete, solid blocks, folded steel plate, treads floating out of the wall, or treads hung from the ceiling on steel rods. Railings come as spindles and newels, posts and rail, frameless glass in a shoe, glass between posts or on standoffs, stainless cables, flat bars or solid panels, with seven baluster and six handrail shapes.

Features
48 designs in 8 families: straight, L-shaped, U-shaped, spiral, curved, floating, grand and exterior
10 layouts with left or right turns, landings and winders
8 support systems from timber stringers to floating and suspended treads
9 railing styles, 7 balusters, 6 handrails and 5 newel posts, on either or both sides
Nosing shapes, open or closed risers, bullnose and flared starter steps
Carpet runners with stair rods, LED strips under the nosings, a wall with a wall handrail
18 image-based PBR finishes: oak, walnut, mahogany, painted wood, concrete, marble, granite, steel, brass, corten and more
Every staircase keeps its own settings; duplicates never change each other
Surprise, Vary and Random Finish buttons, picture-driven sidebar, Shift+A menu and Convert to Mesh
Game-ready: one mesh, low/medium/high detail, materials that work in game engines
Works in Cycles and EEVEE
