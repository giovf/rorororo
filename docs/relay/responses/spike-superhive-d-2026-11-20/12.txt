Acreage grows whole farm landscapes inside Blender: golden wheat with tractor tramlines, maize and sunflower rows, flooded rice paddies, lavender and tulip stripes, vineyards on trellis posts and wires, fruit orchards, ploughed furrows and harvested stubble with hay bales. Pick one of 59 presets, change the crop, the growth stage or the season, and the field rebuilds in a fraction of a second.
Every plant is an instance of a small, detailed prototype, so even a hectare of wheat with millions of rendered faces stays light in the viewport and in the file. A viewport thinning option shows fewer plants while you work, and renders always show all of them.
[From seedling to harvest]
From seedling to harvest
One wheat field grows from green shoots to golden ears, then the combine leaves stubble and round bales.
[Every kind of field]
Every kind of field
Wheat, maize, sunflowers, rice paddies, lavender, tulips, vineyards and orchards, one click each.
[Pick a picture, get a field]
Pick a picture, get a field
Choose a preset or a crop in the sidebar and the field rebuilds on the spot.
[A vineyard through the year]
A vineyard through the year
Bud burst in spring, a green canopy in summer, ripe bunches and red leaves in autumn, pruned vines in winter.
Crops
Wheat, barley, oats, rye, rice, maize, sunflowers, rapeseed, soybeans, cotton, potatoes, cabbage, lettuce, pumpkins, carrots, onions, strawberries, lavender, tulips and hay meadows, each from seedling through growing and flowering to ripe and harvested.
Vineyards and orchards
Grapevines trained on VSP, Guyot, high cordon, pergola or as free-standing bush vines, on wood, steel or concrete posts with sagging wires and anchored end posts. Apple, cherry, almond, olive and citrus trees in rows, with spring blossom, fruit, autumn color, bare winter branches, whitewashed trunks and stakes.
The ground
Drilled seedbeds, furrows, ridges, raised beds, plough slices and paddy bunds, with tractor tracks on the headland, grass margins, grass alleys, wet patches, snow, slopes and rolling hills. Round, square, large square and wrapped silage bales lie along the windrows, scattered or stacked.
Features
59 presets in 7 categories, 27 crops, 5 growth stages and 4 seasons, each a picture in the sidebar
Light: instanced plants, a viewport thinning setting and a face count in the panel
Photo textures from Poly Haven and photo leaf atlases, physically based and game-engine friendly
Surprise Me, Variation and New Layout buttons
Make Plants Real turns the instances into linked objects for export
Shift+A menu with every preset
Requires Blender 4.2 or newer. Tested on Blender 4.2, 4.5 and 5.2 LTS.
