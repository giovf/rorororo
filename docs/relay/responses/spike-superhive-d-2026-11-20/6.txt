NexFoliage at a glance
150 procedural plants for Blender: garden flowers, bulbs, wildflowers, tropical flowers, water lilies, grasses and reeds, shrubs and berry bushes, ferns and ground cover, and mushrooms. Every plant is a normal mesh with clean UVs and one material, and it stays editable after you add it.
Shape every plant
Bloom slider from closed bud to full bloom. Keyframe it and the flower opens.
Height, curve, lean, stems, leaves, petals and colors, all live.
Randomize gives a new individual of the same species in one click.
Draw the stem with a curve: the leaves and the flower follow it.
Save your own presets, or save plants to the Asset Browser.
Ready for games
Export FBX or glTF with 4 LODs. Library median: 26,000 / 6,900 / 2,800 / 1,600 triangles.
Generated PBR textures from 128 px to 4K: base color with alpha cut-out, normal (OpenGL or DirectX), ORM and translucency.
One material per plant, so one draw call. Wind masks are stored in the vertex colors.
Works in Unreal Engine 5, Unity and Godot. A short texture guide is written next to the exported files.
What you get
The NexFoliage add-on: 150 species in 11 categories.
The NexFoliage screen: a plant shelf with drag and drop, and every setting in one place.
An Asset Browser library of only about 19 MB.
Full documentation (Documentation tab) and free updates with more plants.
Requirements
Blender 4.2 to 5.3. Renders in Cycles and EEVEE. Pure Python, so there is nothing else to install.
