Data and statistics
Our dashboards and data reports cover important data about USPTO operations that can help you gain a better understanding of USPTO’s processes, and enable you to make more informed decisions about your application.
Title
Patents dashboard
Monthly summaries for patents data, including pendency, backlog, and other tracking measures.
Launch Patents dashboard (https://www.uspto.gov/dashboards/patents/)
Title
Trademarks dashboard
Q uarterly summaries for trademarks data, including quality, pendency, application filings, and other metrics.
Launch Trademarks dashboard (https://www.uspto.gov/dashboards/trademarks/)
Access quarterly TTAB summaries (https://www.uspto.gov/dashboard/ttab/)
Title
Open Data Portal
Our new hub for open data, built on the idea of finding several datasets and APIs in one platform.
Launch the Open Data Portal (https://data.uspto.gov/home)
Title
PTAB dashboard
Annual summaries for Patent Trial and Appeal Board (PTAB) data, including appeal inventory and other metrics.
Launch PTAB dashboard (https://www.uspto.gov/dashboard/ptab/)
Access PTAB performance benchmarks (https://www.uspto.gov/patents/ptab/statistics)
Title
OPIA dashboard
Quarterly summaries of Office of Policy and International Affairs (OPIA) data, such as programs, people trained, and more.
Launch OPIA dashboard (https://www.uspto.gov/dashboard/opia/)
Title
USPTO annual reports
Fiscal year patents and trademarks data as represented in the Agency Financial Report and the Annual Performance Plan and Annual Performance Report.
Launch USPTO annual reports (https://www.uspto.gov/about-us/performance-and-planning/uspto-annual-reports)
Legacy patent statistic reports
The Open Data Portal provides public data that was in the Patent Examination Data System (PEDS), Bulk Data Storage System (BDSS), and some Developer Hub services. It does not include the legacy patent statistic reports known as the Patent Technology Monitoring Team (PTMT) Reports and Technology Assessment and Forecast (TAF) Reports. If you're looking for the metrics and data found in these reports, please visit the IP5 statistic reports (https://www.fiveipoffices.org/statistics/statisticsreports) and the WIPO IP Statistics Data Center (https://www.fiveipoffices.org/statistics/statisticsreports) .
