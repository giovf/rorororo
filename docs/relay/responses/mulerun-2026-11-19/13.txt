That’s why today, we’re introducing the MuleRun Creator Studio (Beta) (https://mulerun.com/creator/studio) —the workspace designed to bridge your prototype to a product. It lets you focus on what you do best —building the agent’s core logic— while we handle all the backend complexity . This means you can finally:
Build Without Barriers: Package your existing workflows or expertise into an agent, ready for the marketplace.
Launch Instantly: Go from a working prototype to a live, monetizable product in minutes .
Get Paid for Usage: Earn revenue every time a user runs your agent. It’s that simple.

#fully-managed-infrastructure-focus-on-what-you-do-best Fully Managed Infrastructure: Focus on What You Do Best
Forget about managing servers, configuring environments, or worrying about scalability. The MuleRun Creator Studio (Beta) (https://mulerun.com/creator/studio) provides end-to-end hosting, including compute, storage, security, and auto-scaling. This fully managed environment, built specifically for AI agents, is offered by very few platforms globally . It allows you to concentrate entirely on refining your agent’s business logic, knowledge base, and user experience – without the overhead of maintaining complex backend systems.
#your-agent-your-way-choose-your-favorite-tools Your Agent, Your Way: Choose Your Favorite Tools
No matter your technical background, the MuleRun Creator Studio (Beta) (https://mulerun.com/creator/studio) supports your preferred development style:
For Low-Code & No-Code Creators (n8n Workflow Integration): Quickly build agents using n8n’s intuitive visual workflow editor. Drag, drop, and connect nodes to design automation logic – no coding required . Once you’re done, export the workflow JSON and upload it directly to the MuleRun Creator Studio (Beta) (https://mulerun.com/creator/studio) . We’ll process it and provision all the required infrastructure, and deploy it as a market-ready agent. Perfect for rapid prototyping and turning domain expertise into a market-ready agent.
For Pro-Code Creators (API-First Custom Development): For developers seeking maximum control and flexibility, our comprehensive API is your best friend. It allows you to programmatically build and manage agents using tools you prefer, including LangChain, LangGraph and more. You can integrate with your existing systems, implement complex logic, and maintain end-to-end control over your agent’s entire lifecycle.
If neither of the options above fits your needs, and you still want to publish your agent on MuleRun (https://mulerun.com/) , we’d love to hear from you. Just send us an email at bonnie@mulerun.com (mailto:bonnie@mulerun.com) , and we’ll get back to you promptly.
