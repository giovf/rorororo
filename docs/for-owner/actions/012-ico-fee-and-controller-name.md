# 012 — ICO data protection fee (legal requirement, £47–52/yr) and one yes/no

**Status:** open · **Urgency:** medium (a legal requirement; the ICO fines non-payers, though
it writes first) · **Cost:** £52/year, or £47 by direct debit — approved from the £100 cap.

## Why
UK organisations that process personal data must pay the ICO's data protection fee unless they
only do it for staff, accounts and their own marketing. gankdat serves sanctions and exclusion
lists (names of designated people) as its product, so it is not exempt. This is the one real gap
in our GDPR position; the substance (minimisation, lawful bases, policy, processors, rights,
retention) is in place and documented in `ventures/gankdat/docs/GDPR.md`.

## What to do (~10 minutes)
1. https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/ → "Pay now / register". Tier 1
   (turnover under £632k). Type of organisation: sole trader. Organisation name: **gankdat**
   (you can register a trading name). Pay by direct debit for the £5 discount.
2. **Address caveat:** the ICO register is public and shows the name and address you give.
   Options: your home address, or a virtual business address (~£6/month, not yet in the ledger;
   say "012 virtual address" and I'll pick one and add it). There is no third option that is
   both private and compliant.
3. Reply "012 done, reg no. ZAxxxxxxx" and I'll add the number to the privacy policy and ledger.

## One yes/no: name the controller in the privacy policy?
UK GDPR asks the privacy policy to identify the controller. Right now it says "gankdat" and
gives info@gankdat.com. Most sole traders add one line: "gankdat is the trading name of
<your name>, a sole trader in the United Kingdom." It puts your name (not address) on the
site. Reply "012 name yes" or "012 name no". Default if I hear nothing: no change.
