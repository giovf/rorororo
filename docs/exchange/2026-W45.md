### 1. The Claude Code kit on Leanpub — winner (3 × 2 = 6)

- **Why now.** The owner asked for this line on 10-18 ("a course … giving all the setup they need as downloadable
  files"). Three exchanges parked it for want of a channel: the non-technical framing had 0–1 buyers across six
  listings (10-19), Gumroad's Discover lists a product only after US$100 of sales we would have to bring (10-24), Etsy
  bars AI-generated instructional content (11-02). The 10-31 Leanpub spike answers the channel question from the
  shelf's own pages: Leanpub lists a published book in its bookstore and topic pages from day one at no cost, pays 80 %
  with no exclusivity, takes a UK individual with an email sign-up and the owner's existing PayPal (US$20 floor), and its
  catalogue — read from 31 book pages' own `totalCopiesSold` — carried **11–13 paying readers to each of four Claude Code
  operations handbooks of exactly our kit's shape** (yurukusa, US$9.99–12.99, five months) and 81–530 to the broader
  guides (De Vos 530 at US$15–33, Steve Publications 116, Berton 81). The "first four buyers" the 10-24 re-open condition
  asked for arrive from the catalogue itself. Prices cluster at US$19–25 minimum / US$29–39 suggested; net per sale
  US$15.20–23.20, five to seven times the Kindle guide's.
- **Honest expectation.** A single operations handbook from an unknown author sells **10–40 copies in six months →
  US$150–900 net, most likely the low end**; the kill rule is < 5 copies in the 60 days after the listing is live. It
  beats the baseline because the baseline is nothing: the pipeline is starved, the week's build and burn-down slots went
  cold, and this is 1.5 days of owner-free work on a shelf whose buyers are counted. It is not a line that captures a
  new audience — Leanpub's readers are developers already buying Claude Code books — and it costs £0.
- **What the build does (four items, `docs/pipeline/queues/claude-code-kit.json`).** (1) `kit-handbook-manuscript` (3):
  the 20–30k-word handbook *Running Claude Code unattended* as a content module → EPUB + PDF (the Kindle guide's
  builder is the starting point), with the hard requirement of a preface *How this book was written* that states the
  real process; (2) `kit-extras-zip` (3): the MIT-licensed Extras zip assembled from the repo by a script with a scrub
  test (no secrets, no venture names, no owner identity); (3) `leanpub-listing-pack-and-owner-step` (2.5): the listing
  copy, the AI Usage page text, the Package pricing, a `SELF-CAUSED.md` row for Leanpub mail, and the one owner request;
  (4) `leanpub-listing-live` (3, blocked on the owner's note): records the URL, sets `launched`, queues the day-60 read.
- **The one owner step, and the line this exchange draws.** Leanpub's terms say "AI agents are not permitted to accept
  these Terms" and "we don't permit AI agents to use Leanpub", so the account is the owner's and this operation never
  touches it, its API, its forums or its author messages. The request is ≤ 15 minutes of mechanical steps (sign up,
  upload, price, fill the AI Usage page, choose PayPal, publish) and asks no identity check and no card. But Leanpub's
  published rule is "human-created and human-curated, and enhanced or transformed by AI tools with human guidance", with
  permanent suspension as the penalty for slop. A 25k-word book takes about two hours to read, and the owner-time rule
  (STRATEGY §3) caps what the operation asks at 15 minutes for a line scoring under 10. **So the request will say
  plainly: Leanpub requires the author to stand behind the text; the two-hour read is yours to choose and is not asked
  for; if you do not want to read it, do not publish it.** The preface describes whatever actually happened. A listing
  pushed through without that read would be the thing Leanpub's rule is written against, and CLAUDE.md's ToS rule
  applies; the exchange would rather the book sit unpublished than mislead the shelf. The risk is bounded either way: a
  new account holding one disclosed book, nothing else of the owner's on the platform, the files sellable elsewhere.
- **Kill.** < 5 copies in the 60 days after the listing is live → retire the book from the queue, keep the files for the
  Gumroad route if a channel of ours ever appears (public mirror ≥ 500 stars, developer traffic on a gankdat page).
