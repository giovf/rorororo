### 1. Companies House lookup + monitor — winner (8.0)

- **Where the buyers are.** Today's store read is the first measurement of *which* UK-register
  data Apify users pay for, and it is one category: Companies House company lookups. 509 results
  for the query; the thirteen paid actors on page one have 5–168 total users each and the leader
  has 47 paying users in the last 30 days and ~50k runs. Every category gankdat lists in today
  has no actor above the QA floor. Prices are public: US$0.80/1k (nocodeventure), $2/1k (dominvo),
  $3/1k (chrisp1211), $4 (memo23). Off Apify the same job is sold by Creditsafe, Red Flag Alert
  and Company Watch as monitoring subscriptions (£50+/month), and by Companies House itself only
  as a free per-company "Follow" email.
- **Gap we fill.** Of the thirteen, none offers a *monitor*: a scheduled run over a buyer's own
  list of company numbers that returns only what changed (status, new charge, accounts overdue,
  confirmation statement, filing events, corporate PSC changes). gankdat's change-feed machinery
  and the actors' `mode: changes` (10-05) are exactly that shape; the buyer's list is the input,
  the previous run's dataset the baseline. MCP/x402 and the keyless preview come free.
- **Supply, not shelf — why it still wins.** The Apify shelf is open and searchable within hours
  of a push; buyers are demonstrably searching "companies house" there now. The shelf candidate
  (#2) cannot put a buyer in front of the product within a week (Docker reviews the PR) and the
  agent shelves already open have converted nobody. STRATEGY §5's "distribution beats new
  datasets" stands — this is not a new register, it is the first listing in the one place on the
  only shelf with runs from outside the repo where the money already is.
- **Constraints.** `COMPANIES_HOUSE_API_KEY` is already configured for `uk-companies` (600
  requests per 5 minutes; a lookup is ≤ 4 calls, KV-cached 24 h). **Blind Mode stays**
  (`GDPR.md` LIA table: officer/PSC/person fields never ingested) — the dataset carries
  organisation-level fields and filing *events* (an "AP01 director appointed" category without the
  name), corporate PSCs only. That is why the applied score is 8.0 and not 10.7: the incumbents
  lead with directors & PSCs. Person fields are parked as #4 behind a new LIA line and the ICO fee
  (owner decision 2026-09-20, action 012). Licence: Companies House data is free to reuse under
  the API terms (attribution, no implied endorsement). Effort 1.5 days, 0 owner minutes.
- **Proof.** Apify actor ≥ 5 users from other accounts in its first 30 days (`apify 30d by actor`
  in the Daily numbers row) and ≥ 1 `changes`-mode run; `datasets 30d:` for the new slug above the
  automated floor. Day-30 read queued for 2026-11-07.
