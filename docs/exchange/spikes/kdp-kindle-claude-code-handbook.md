| Shelf | List | Net per copy | Note |

| Kindle | US$9.99 | **US$6.96** | top of the band |
| Kindle | US$14.99 | US$5.25 (35 %) | above the band — worse than US$9.99 |
| Kindle, amazon.co.uk | £7.99 inc. VAT (£6.66 ex) | **£4.64** | the UK's 20 % e-book VAT comes off first |

A Kindle copy earns a third to a half of a Leanpub copy; the shelf's job is buyers Leanpub does not have (Amazon brings
them, Leanpub's 165,000 monthly users mostly land on a book page from elsewhere). The twin is worth it if it sells
more than ~2 copies for every Leanpub copy it cannibalises — unlikely to cannibalise at all, since the Extras zip is
Leanpub-only.

## 4. Call

**Park.** The Kindle twin costs nothing to list and nothing in rules (not enrolled in Select; AI disclosure at publish;
Leanpub has no exclusivity), but the shelf shows no buyers for an independent Claude Code book: eight of nine live
independent titles rank #1.3M–4.9M after four to six months, the one seller is trade-published, and a US$7.99–9.99 copy
nets US$5.56–6.96 against Leanpub's US$15.20. Listing it now would add an owner step (a KDP upload with the disclosure
form, ~10 min) to action 038 for an expected ≈ US$0–7 a month, below the owner-time rule's bar for any step that is not
already asked. The handbook's own test of demand is the Leanpub listing (action 038, open since 11-05).

- **Trigger (exchange.json `kdp-kindle-claude-code-handbook`):** the Leanpub day-60 read shows ≥ 10 copies sold (demand
  proven, buyers exist beyond the shelf) → the kit's queue gets `kindle-edition` (0.3 day: the Kindle guide's packager
  under `ventures/tax-workbook/src/kindle/` builds the EPUB, the AI disclosure and the not-in-Select choice are written
  into the owner note, one more 10-minute step on the kit's existing request); or Blumond's rank is matched by a second
  independent title in a later read of these ten pages (one relay line each, same ASINs) — then the shelf has changed.
- **Not a trigger:** price. The rivals' US$13–61 prices with #1M+ ranks say the band is not what stops sales.
- **Kept from this read:** the KDP Select exclusivity sentence (§1), so the tax guide's enrolment is never copied onto a
  book that is also on Leanpub; the product-page route through the relay works on amazon.com and is walled on
  amazon.co.uk, the search route is walled on both today.
