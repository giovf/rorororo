## 1. The policy (primary, read in full through the relay)

help.etsy.com "What Can I Sell on Etsy?", section **AI-generated content**:

> Etsy allows sellers to use artificial intelligence (AI) tools to create certain items, as long as they contribute their
> own creative input and follow Etsy's policies. Sellers must disclose within their listing description if an item is
> created with the use of AI. […] Some forms of AI-generated content are not allowed on Etsy, including:
> **Bot-generated content** · **AI-generated functional instructions, such as craft patterns, recipes, and instructions** ·
> **AI prompt bundles**.

Section **Designed by a seller**: "you can offer your original design as a digital download […] This category also
includes seller-prompted AI creations. Digital items listed in your store must be made and/or designed by you, the
seller. […] Seller-prompted AI creations must disclose the use of AI."

The Seller Handbook article "Important updates to AI use on Etsy" (1522733284810, www — 403 on the runner, quoted from
etsy.com's own index): "the information buyers rely on to create or use your item must be your own work, not generated by
AI. This means AI-generated functional guides are no longer allowed on Etsy"; the covered types are "craft patterns […];
recipes; building, woodworking, or other project plans; instruction or assembly manuals, and **instructional e-books**";
"Bot-generated listings without any human involvement aren't allowed. These listings will be removed and may also result
in your shop being removed from Etsy." Trade press dates the change to 2026-10-22 (crowdfundinsider, listinglab); the
help-centre text above is the live rule either way.

**Reading for the kit.** The kit *is* an instructional e-book plus configuration files that instruct an agent; the guide
is drafted by this operation, which is the AI use the listing would have to disclose. A disclosed listing falls inside
"AI-generated functional instructions"; an undisclosed one breaks the disclosure rule and the UI-copy convention here.
Rewriting the guide in the owner's own words would make it "your own work" in letter — and would take the owner hours the
2026-10-29 owner-time rule does not allow (≤ 15 min per request), on a line scoring ≈ 6. No honest version fits.

## 4. Call

**Decline.** Etsy's own help centre lists "AI-generated functional instructions" among the content not allowed, and the
handbook names instructional e-books; the kit cannot be listed with an honest disclosure, and the owner-time rule rules
out a rewrite in the owner's words. Reopen only if help.etsy.com drops "AI-generated functional instructions" from the
not-allowed list, or the owner volunteers to write the guide (then re-score with §2's numbers). Leanpub (80 %, AI Usage
page, promoted 10-31) stays the kit's shelf for the W45 exchange.
