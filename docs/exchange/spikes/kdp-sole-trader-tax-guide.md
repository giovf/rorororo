Against Etsy's £6.99 workbook (≈ £5.70 kept after fees, RESEARCH §6) a Kindle guide nets about half per copy and costs

## 4. Call: promote — a standalone 2026/27 guide, not a companion to the workbook

- **Shape.** "UK Sole Trader Tax 2026/27: Self Assessment, Making Tax Digital and the numbers" — a complete guide that
  stands alone (registration, SA103S boxes and the expense categories, the 2026/27 rates and thresholds, Class 4 NI,
  the trading allowance, MTD quarterly updates and who is in from April 2026, mileage, payments on account, a worked
  example), built as an EPUB from TypeScript with the workbook's tables as its tables. The content-quality rules shape
  it: it may not redirect readers elsewhere for "the full content" nor exist to advertise, so the workbook is one plain
  sentence in the back matter, not the book's purpose; it is not a companion to another book; it must not be short.
  List £4.99 at 70 % (£2.70 a sale), Kindle Countdown to £2.99 for the launch week.
- **Disclosure.** The text is AI-generated under KDP's definition and is declared so at publication; nothing in the
  listing hides it or claims a human author. The disclosure is Amazon's, the listing copy says what the buyer gets.
- **Owner steps (batched into action 029's sitting).** Open the KDP account in the owner's name, complete the tax
  interview (W-8BEN, UK resident, UTR or NI number) and add the UK bank account (~15 min); publish is one upload the
  owner clicks after reading the disclosure checkbox — the routine prepares the EPUB, cover, description and keywords.
- **Score.** Evidence 2 (rivals with ratings counts on the shelf, the newest a near-identical product five days old) ×
  reach 2 (Amazon UK's Kindle Store, billing and VAT supplied) ÷ (1 build day + 20 owner minutes) ≈ 3, × queue weight 2.
  Queued as `tax-workbook/kdp-tax-guide-ebook`; proof: listing live, first sale within 30 days, ≥ 10 within 60 (the §4
  Etsy row's rule, counted from KDP's own sales report, not from mail). Kill with the workbook at day 60.
- **Park triggers that did not fire.** The content-quality guide bars companions to other books and incomplete or
  advertising books, not an original guide; AI-disclosed books are not labelled to buyers on any page read; the price
  band takes £3.99–6.99 at 70 %. Unread and carried as risks: the rivals' sales ranks (bot wall) and whether Amazon's
  2025–26 enforcement sweeps (*index*) treat a declared AI book differently from an undeclared one.
