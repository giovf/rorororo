### 1. UK Trade Marks Journal — winner

- **Job & buyers.** Trade-mark watching: brand owners and their attorneys pay a firm to scan each
  week's journal for similar new applications and report monthly, because the two-month
  opposition window runs from journal publication. Recurring, priced per mark per year.
- **Paid rivals with public prices.** UK Trade Marks Bureau £180 + VAT/mark/year (UK) and £225
  (EU) — https://www.trademarksbureau.co.uk/services-info/uk-trade-watch ; National Business
  Register from £200/year — https://nationalbusinessregister.co.uk/trade-mark-monitoring-service/ ;
  Cameron IP £320 ex VAT (EU) / £420 (international) —
  https://cameronintellectualproperty.com/services/trade-marks/trade-mark-watching-services/ ;
  Harper James, Thorntons, Trade Mark Direct sell the same service. A startup, TMGuard
  (https://tmguard.uk/blog/the-2-month-deadline-every-uk-trademark-owner-should-know), is built
  on exactly this journal. On Apify, `nexgendata/uk-trademark-search` charges **$0.05 per record**
  (50× our per-row price), `dltik/euipo-trademarks-scraper` $0.01/record,
  `parseforge/tmview-trademarks-scraper` $0.021/result — https://apify.com/nexgendata/uk-trademark-search
  and https://apify.com/parseforge/tmview-trademarks-scraper.
- **Gap.** Every rival is either a human service (£180+/mark) or a per-search scraper of the IPO
  site. Nobody serves the weekly journal as a queryable, agent-readable feed with the opposition
  deadline computed per mark. That is our change-feed edge (STRATEGY §2) applied to a market that
  already pays for change detection.
- **Data & licence.** The Trade Marks Journal is published every Friday as PDF **and XML** (≈145 MB
  XML, ≈15 MB zip) under **OGL v3** — data.gov.uk record `ipo-tmj`
  (https://ckan.publishing.service.gov.uk/dataset/ipo-tmj), journal index
  https://www.ipo.gov.uk/t-tmj.htm, per-issue pages like
  https://www.ipo.gov.uk/t-tmj/tm-journals/2026-029/index.html, and the publication timetable
  https://www.ipo.gov.uk/t-tmj/t-tmj-timetable.htm. Contents: UK applications and international
  registrations designating the UK accepted in the previous week (mark, classes, applicant,
  filing date, journal number). A full-register bulk file is FOI-only and stale (2016), and the
  One IPO Search APIs are not public yet
  (https://github.com/parkerhancock/patent-client-agents/blob/main/research/connectors/ukipo.md),
  so the weekly journal is the only open feed — and the one the watch services actually read.
- **Build.** One weekly ingest (Saturday wave, own cron) of the zip → XML → rows, rolling 52
  journals (≈100–150k rows), a `uk-trademark-journal` source, stats page, Apify actor, terms row.
  1.5 days. Risk: the XML schema is undocumented and the routine containers cannot reach
  `ipo.gov.uk` (same handoff pattern as GIAS and NHS ODS, both verified by the next interactive
  session). Personal data: applicants can be individuals — organisation-level fields only, drop
  representative addresses (same rule as uk-charities trustees).
- **Proof.** Queries + paywall hits on the dataset and `get_changes` calls for it, 30 days after
  launch (Analytics Engine); Apify paid runs once actors are unblocked.
