Three steps and you're reading.

  1. Pin it. Click the puzzle-piece icon 🧩 at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to ReadFocus. The icon stays visible.
  2. Turn it on for a site. On any article, click the icon and flip On for this site (Chrome asks once to allow ReadFocus on that site). Or press …. It stays on for that site and never runs anywhere you haven't enabled.
  3. Try the ruler. … shows a soft band that follows your mouse. Change the shortcuts any time.

Reading on a screen asks a lot of your eyes: they have to find the start of every word, decide where it ends, and keep the line while a dozen other things on the page compete for attention. ReadFocus emphasises the first part of each word so there is always somewhere to land, and the rest of the word fills itself in. Most people notice they read a little faster and lose their place a lot less. Use it on articles, documentation and forums; it leaves anything you are typing alone.
