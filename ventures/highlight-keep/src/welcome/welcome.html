Highlight anything, add a note, find it again. Everything stays in your browser.

  1. Pin it. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Highlight Keep.
  2. Turn it on for a site. On an article, click the icon and flip On for this site (Chrome asks once to allow it there), or press ….
  3. Select text. A small bar appears — pick a colour. Click a highlight later to change it, add a note, or remove it. Like this.
  4. Get it out. The popup copies a page's highlights as Markdown; the library (unlock) searches everything and exports it all.
