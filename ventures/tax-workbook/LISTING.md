## Description

**Keep your sole-trader records for the 2026/27 tax year in one spreadsheet, and know what you will owe before January.**

This workbook gives you three simple logs — income, expenses and business mileage — and turns them into everything you
need at year end: the SA103S self-employment boxes, the quarterly totals Making Tax Digital asks for, a Trading Allowance
check, and an estimate of your Income Tax, Class 4 National Insurance and payments on account.

**What you get (one .xlsx file, instant download)**

- Setup tab with every 2026/27 rate and threshold — Personal Allowance, tax bands, Class 4 limits, HMRC mileage rates,
  Trading Allowance, the MTD threshold — each with its gov.uk source. Change a figure once and every formula follows.
- Income log: date, customer, type (sales or other business income), amount.
- Expense log with a drop-down of the SA103S expense categories (boxes 11–19), a business-use share for mixed costs,
  and the allowable amount worked out for you.
- Mileage log at HMRC approved rates, including the 10,000-mile step-down for cars and vans.
- Quarterly tab: the four MTD quarters (standard or calendar) with income, expenses, profit and each update deadline.
- Allowance tab: whether the £1,000 Trading Allowance or your actual expenses leaves you the lower profit.
- SA103 tab: your year-end figures box by box, ready to copy into the self-employment pages or your filing software.
- Tax tab: Income Tax, Class 4 NI, the balancing payment due 31 January and the two payments on account — with room for
  a salary or pension and tax already deducted so the bands are right.
- Example tab: a filled, hand-checked year so you can see what a finished workbook looks like.
- README tab: how to use it, what it covers and what it does not.

**Works in** Microsoft Excel 2010 or later, Google Sheets (File → Import), LibreOffice Calc and Apple Numbers. Formulas
only — no macros, nothing to enable. The blue cells are yours to type in; everything else is calculated.

**Please read before buying**

- It is a record-keeping and estimating spreadsheet, **not MTD-recognised filing software** and not tax advice. Quarterly
  updates and the tax return are still sent through HMRC's online service or MTD-compatible software; this workbook
  gives you the numbers to send.
- Income Tax bands for **England, Wales and Northern Ireland** (Scottish bands differ).
- For a **sole trader with one business, not VAT-registered**. Not for partnerships or limited companies.
- Not covered: student loan repayments, savings and dividend income, Marriage Allowance, pension and Gift Aid relief,
  losses brought forward, the High Income Child Benefit Charge. Check the final figures against HMRC's own calculation.
- Rates are the 2026/27 figures published on gov.uk, read in October 2026.
